Abstract; §§3 and 3.1
The instrument paper identifies a 570-megapixel focal plane using 250 μm fully depleted CCDs. The LBNL back-illuminated devices met the science programme's required high z-band response. LBNL supplied diced CCDs to Fermilab, where they were packaged.

§9.3, Installation and Commissioning
The paper dates the official first-light night to 12 September 2012 UT, identifies the instrument and names objects in the first images.

§10, Summary
The collaboration describes DECam at the Blanco telescope on Cerro Tololo in Chile and includes the planned LSST Camera in the same generation of very large astronomical imagers. It describes community observing use alongside DES.
