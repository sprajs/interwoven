 Abstract
 The Antikythera Mechanism is a unique Greek geared device, constructed around the end of the second century bc . It is known 1 , 2 , 3 , 4 , 5 , 6 , 7 , 8 , 9 that it calculated and displayed celestial information, particularly cycles such as the phases of the moon and a luni-solar calendar. Calendars were important to ancient societies 10 for timing agricultural activity and fixing religious festivals. Eclipses and planetary motions were often interpreted as omens, while the calm regularity of the astronomical cycles must have been philosophically attractive in an uncertain and violent world.
