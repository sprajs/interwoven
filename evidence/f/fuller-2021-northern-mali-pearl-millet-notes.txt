Source: Transition From Wild to Domesticated Pearl Millet Revealed in Ceramic Temper at Three Middle Holocene Sites in Northern Mali
URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8550313/
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passages read: Abstract and discussion
Third-millennium BCE ceramic temper includes domesticated-type millet chaff; morphology changes over time and wild types occur earlier.
Limitations: The broad period dates archaeological material, not the beginning or completion of selection; traits and sites vary.
