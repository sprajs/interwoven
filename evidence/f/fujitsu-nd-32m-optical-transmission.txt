Access: the complete web page was inspected in the web renderer. Direct byte retrieval returned 429; original HTML bytes are not retained.
Fujitsu reports it delivered 32-Mb/s optical-fiber transmission equipment to the Japanese telephone organization it calls NTT in 1974, as part of its optical-device development. The source does not document an amount paid, actual service operation, an operational route, or first public use, and does not assert that this device was used on the USS Little Rock.
