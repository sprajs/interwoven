FilmTec Corporation v. Hydranautics, 982 F.2d 1546
https://law.resource.org/pub/us/case/reporter/F2/982/982.F2d.1546.92-1091.html
Inspected 2026-11-17. Selected passages paraphrased.

Background ¶¶8–16 and opinion ¶¶29–35 — The record places Cadotte's experiments with a crosslinked aromatic polyamide reverse-osmosis membrane at the North Star Division of Midwest Research Institute in November 1977. The court found that the prototype desalinated seawater, although the November membranes showed lower salt rejection than the later ones. Cadotte tested versions of the same design at FilmTec on 23 February 1978 and achieved higher rejection.
Opinion ¶¶33–37 — The court held that the November 1977 experiments were part of the same invention described in US 4,277,344. The court reversed the district court on title; the decision did not reach the infringement holding. Use the actual patent face for the filing date because the opinion misprints it.
