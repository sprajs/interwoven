Thurber, “The Birth of CT,” American Journal of Roentgenology 167 (1996), pp. 1365–1370, DOI 10.2214/ajr.167.6.8956560, https://www.ajronline.org/doi/pdf/10.2214/ajr.167.6.8956560. The original PDF was inspected through the web search renderer; direct download returned 403. This locally retained note paraphrases the indexed original passage and is not the article. Printed p. 1366 discusses Kuhl and Edwards' Mark II and Mark III emission scanners. It says Kuhl, Hale and Eaton obtained a transverse transmission image of a patient's thorax on 14 May 1965 using a Mark II and an americium-241 photon source. The article distinguishes its back-projection reconstruction and its blur from Hounsfield's later X-ray system. It treats the 1970 Mark III computer-assisted emission system separately. The source's priority interpretation does not establish a first worldwide claim.
