Attributed research note, not a reproduction. Finnish Computer Museum Association, collection page “IBM 305 RAMAC,” https://suomentietokonemuseo.fi/laitteet-esineet/, inspected 6 November 2026. The Finnish-language description says the 305 RAMAC used an IBM 350 disk, reports that production ended in 1961 and market withdrawal occurred in 1969, and says the particular machine belonged to Helsinki wholesale cooperative OTK and was used there during the 1960s. It separately describes later museum custody. The site is a retrospective collection account, not an inspected IBM production notice or OTK operating log. Its machine-type disk description does not establish the serial identity of the disk used at OTK, exact operating dates, or the worldwide last use of RAMAC.
