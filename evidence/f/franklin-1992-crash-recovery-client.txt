Attributed research note, not a reproduction. Michael J. Franklin, Michael J. Zwilling, C. K. Tan, Michael J. Carey and David J. DeWitt, “Crash Recovery in Client-Server EXODUS,” ACM SIGMOD 1992, printed pp. 165–168 and 171–174, https://www.csd.uoc.gr/~hy460/pdf/p165-franklin.pdf . Wisconsin researchers report implementing and measuring ARIES-based recovery in client-server EXODUS. They explain modifications required by separate client buffers and server-side logs and the handling of chained compensation records. Their implementation uses data locks at page granularity or coarser. This is independent research implementation and evaluation by June 1992, not proof of routine production use, record-level locking or an unmodified implementation.
