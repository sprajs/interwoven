24 Percent Efficient n-Type Silicon Solar Cell
2013-11-27 | Press release 31/13, 27 November 2013

Contemporary laboratory-issued press release.
Inspected 2026-11-17

pp. 1–2, description of the cell and research support
Fraunhofer ISE in Freiburg reported 24% conversion efficiency for an n-type silicon cell with a full-area rear passivating contact. The tunnel oxide/silicon stack let majority carriers through while suppressing recombination. The institute compared it with partial-area contacts and identified German Federal Ministry for the Environment, Nature Conservation and Nuclear Safety support.
