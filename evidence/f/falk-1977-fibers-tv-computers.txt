Source: Howard Falk, “Fibers for TV and computers,” IEEE Spectrum, February 1977, printed p. 40, https://www.worldradiohistory.com/Archive-IEEE/1977/IEEE-Spectrum-1977-02.pdf
Access: the original scan was inspected through an online text-rendering provider; the downloadable original was not retained and no byte hash is claimed. Text was interleaved from adjacent columns, so those lines were reconstructed by context, not read as a continuous paragraph.
Falk reports that Dorset police installed a 10 Mb/s fiber-optic computer-to-terminal link in fall 1975. Separately, the source says Rediffusion began using a glass-fiber television trunk cable at Hastings in March 1976; it describes the system as serving approximately 34,000 subscribers, not fiber running to every home. The Dorset installation date alone does not prove its first operating day.
