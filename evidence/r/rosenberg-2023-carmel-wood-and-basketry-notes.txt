The Unseen Record: Ninth–Seventh Millennia Cal. BP Wooden and Basketry Objects from Submerged Settlements off the Carmel Coast, Israel
https://www.mdpi.com/1999-4907/14/12/2373
Forests 14(12), 2373. Publisher HTML, DOI 10.3390/f14122373.
Inspected 2026-11-17. Original technical description of KS 2 and archaeological context. The possible oil-processing function refers back to Galili et al. (1997), a coauthored earlier excavation study.

Section 3.2, KS 2, Fig. 7: Three fragments of a twig basket, possibly from the base and walls, exhibit alternate pair-twining and were recovered alongside olive pits and wood fragments in a clay-filled pit at Kfar Samir. The authors propose a possible aqal-like oil-processing use.
Table 1, object KS 2: The authors date KS 2 by the site chronology, with outer bounds of 7964–6299 cal BP across the reported sample intervals. The basket itself was not directly dated.
