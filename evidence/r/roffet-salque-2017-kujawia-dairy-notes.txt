Source: From the inside out: Upscaling organic residue analyses of archaeological ceramics
URL: https://research-information.bris.ac.uk/files/139976155/1_s2.0_S2352409X16301390_main.pdf
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passages read: Dairy processing, p. 630
The synthesis describes dairy fat in sieves dated 5200–4800 BCE and interprets the combination as evidence for cheesemaking.
Caveat: The interpretation combines vessel form and fat residues; the specific cheese preparation, recipe and organism are not directly identified.
