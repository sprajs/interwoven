J. L. Russell, “Kepler’s Laws of Planetary Motion: 1609–1666”, British Journal for the History of Science 2(1) (1964), 1–24. DOI 10.1017/S0007087400001813. https://www.astro.rug.nl/~vdkruit/jea3/homepage/Russell.pdf

INSPECTED PASSAGES (researcher paraphrase; not the full source): Printed pp. 2–3: the first law uses an elliptical orbit with the Sun at one focus, and the 1609 formulation was explicit for Mars; the history describes displacement of circles and epicycles by ellipses. Printed p. 6: Russell interprets Lower’s letter to Harriot as acceptance of Kepler’s elliptical orbits and dates it February 1610. Stevens’s later transcription, rather than the original manuscript, was inspected for this proposal; Chapman discusses the year ambiguity.
