Belovode: technology of metal production
https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10134145/
The Rise of Metallurgy in Eurasia, Archaeopress, chapter 11, pp. 123–151
Inspected 2026-11-17. Open UCL deposit of the published chapter. Same analytical project lineage as earlier Belovode reports, but the excavated Trench 18 finds and their dating associations are from a separate 2012–2013 campaign.

pp. 123–127, Figs. 2–3 and Tables 1–2: Slag, a slagged sherd and a metal droplet are reported from Trench 18 spit 5 in spatial association with Feature 6; the reported Feature 6 contextual sample MAMS23380 is 4724–4555 cal BCE, 2σ. Approximate assemblage placement is the 47th century BCE.
pp. 128–129 and 133–138, installation discussion and analysis: The authors infer that Feature 6 may have been a pottery-lined pit for smelting from the burnt pit and the nearby slagged sherds. Feature 35 is a separate possibility with a different evidentiary basis.
pp. 149–151, conclusion: New slag and related finds are consistent with the use of manganese-rich black-and-green ore reported in earlier excavations.
