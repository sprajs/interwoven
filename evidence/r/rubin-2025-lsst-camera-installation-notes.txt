NSF–DOE Vera C. Rubin Observatory Installs LSST Camera on Telescope
NSF–DOE Vera C. Rubin Observatory
2025-03-12 | https://rubinobservatory.org/news/lsst-camera-installed
Inspected 2026-11-17. Operator press release for installation.

Opening paragraphs and paragraph beginning ‘In early March’
The completed camera was installed on the Simonyi Survey Telescope on Cerro Pachón in early March 2025. The camera's completion is dated to April 2024, ahead of its transport from SLAC to Chile. The operator gives 3200 megapixels.
