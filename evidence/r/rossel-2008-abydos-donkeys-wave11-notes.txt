Title: Domestication of the donkey: Timing, processes, and indicators
URL: https://www.pnas.org/doi/full/10.1073/pnas.0709692105
Accessed: 2026-11-10
Passage: Abstract and Abydos skeleton analysis

Authors report ten articulated ass skeletons from a mortuary complex at Abydos around 3000 BCE with skeletal changes consistent with load carrying; pack work is inferred from pathologies, not an observed journey.
