Opening and trial-use paragraphs
Development by RIKEN and Fujitsu began in 2014 to serve as the core of Japan's MEXT-led HPCI. Trial use started in April 2020. RIKEN reports completion and shared use starting 9 March 2021, with project selection by RIST.
