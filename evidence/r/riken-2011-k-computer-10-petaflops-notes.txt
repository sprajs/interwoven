K computer Achieves Goal of 10 Petaflops
https://www.riken.jp/en/news_pubs/news/2011/20111102/index.html
Joint announcement dated 2 November 2011
Opened 2026-11-17. Paraphrased reading notes.

Opening announcement and second and third paragraphs
The partners reported measured LINPACK performance of 10.51 petaflops, using 88,128 CPUs in the later configuration. They describe the machine as still under development and part of Japan's HPCI initiative led by MEXT. The reported theoretical figure is 11.28 petaflops.
