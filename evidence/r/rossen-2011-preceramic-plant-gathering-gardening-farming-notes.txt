Chapter 9, final paragraph of summary: In the Tierra Blanca phase, 7800–5000 BP, Rossen describes "a shift from single-family house gardening to multifamily fields and canal systems". The summary places community field irrigation in the upper valley while foraging persisted in much of the valley.
