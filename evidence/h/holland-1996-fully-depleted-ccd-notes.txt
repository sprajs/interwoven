A 200 × 200 CCD Image Sensor Fabricated on High-Resistivity Silicon

PostScript p. 1, abstract and §§1–2
A 200 × 200 pixel CCD was fabricated on high-resistivity silicon and could operate with its 300 μm substrate fully depleted. The paper reports better red and near-infrared response than conventional and thinned CCDs and supports backside illumination. In §1 the authors explicitly base their work on research to develop fully depleted p-i-n detectors for high-energy physics, citing Holland's 1989 device fabrication paper.

PostScript p. 4, conclusions and reference [2]
The authors report demonstrated short-wavelength imaging by back illumination. Reference [2] names Holland's 1989 article on detectors and transistors fabricated on high-resistivity silicon.
