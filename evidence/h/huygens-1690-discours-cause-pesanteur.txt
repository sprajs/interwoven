Christiaan Huygens, “Discours de la cause de la pesanteur” (1690), transcribed in Oeuvres complètes, vol. XXI, ed. J. A. Vollgraff (1944), digital DBNL version. https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huyg003oeuv21_01/huyg003oeuv21_01_0084.php

INSPECTED PASSAGE (researcher paraphrase; not the full source): The Addition, critical-edition pp. 466–472 (1690 original pp. 154–160 as indexed in the critical edition): Huygens says he read Newton’s Principia, applies Newton’s communicating-channel equilibrium idea to an alternative Earth-shape calculation, and explicitly states that he cannot accept Newton’s mutual attraction among the small parts of bodies; he accepts the centripetal attraction of planets toward the Sun and the Moon toward Earth. This is substantive contemporary critical recognition of the universal proposition, not acceptance of the full law. The 1944 edited transcription rather than the 1690 printed object was inspected.
