Hitachi and Toshiba to Deliver the Future of High-Speed Rolling Stock to Taiwan
2026-07-30 manufacturer release
https://www.hitachi.com/en/press/articles/2026/07/0730a/
Inspected 2026-11-17. Research paraphrases of the original source.

Opening two paragraphs and Overview of Railcars
The consortium reports building and shipping the first complete 12-car N700ST trainset from Hitachi’s Kasado Works to Taiwan. It identifies the N700S in JR Central operation as the basis, with the derivative designed for THSRC’s operating environment and needs. It describes silicon-carbide traction equipment using blowerless cooling and battery self-movement.
