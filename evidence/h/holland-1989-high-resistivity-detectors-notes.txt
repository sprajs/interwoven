Fabrication of detectors and transistors on high-resistivity silicon
Steve Holland

Inspected 2026-11-17. Author's journal article, Nuclear Instruments and Methods in Physics Research A275, 537–541, DOI 10.1016/0168-9002(89)90741-9, in a third-party mirror of the typeset paper.

p. 537, abstract and §1
At Lawrence Berkeley Laboratory, Holland reports fabricating p-i-n silicon radiation diodes and p-channel MOSFETs together on high-resistivity silicon. Backside gettering removes impurities from the device region and reduces diode leakage, while permitting processing compatible with integrated circuits. Test transistors on the same wafer were an initial step toward integrating the detector and readout.
