The account relates a lightning-damaged Dorset police radio system, a subsequent commercial Standard Telephones and Cables installation, and the return to normal police operation around mid-September 1975. A 1977 IEEE account gives fall 1975 for the installation; neither claim establishes global priority. Any exact September day of first operation remains unverified.
