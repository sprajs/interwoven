Selim Hassan, with Mahmoud Darwish, Excavations at Giza IV, 1932–1933 (Cairo: Government Press, 1943), printed pp. 141–142, tomb of Neb-em-akhet. Original scanned volume inspected through the web provider at https://gizamedia.rc.fas.harvard.edu/images/MFA-images/Giza/GizaImage/full/library/hassan_giza_4.pdf; local transfer failed. Hassan interprets a relief as a grape press: four men turn poles through opposite ends of a sack while a fifth person keeps the sack extended; twisting contracts the sack and the expressed liquid is caught below. The tomb is one of Khafre’s family group at Giza. The ancient scene documents combined torsion and maintained lengthwise tension, not tension alone, and does not identify the bag’s exact weave geometry. It is an earlier comparative configuration, not evidence of a causal link to American cassava processing.
