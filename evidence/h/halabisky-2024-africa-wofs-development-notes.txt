A dynamic surface water extent service for Africa developed through continental-scale collaboration
https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/environmental-science/articles/10.3389/fenvs.2024.1251315/full
Source date: 2024. Read 2026-11-17.

Abstract and Section 1, Introduction
The developers describe transferring the Australian WOfS algorithm and running it at continental African scale. Consistent analysis-ready Landsat inputs and similarities in water's spectral response enabled reuse without starting training afresh. Four African regional organisations participated in validation, coordinated through the DE Africa Product Development Task Team.

Section 2.3, Water observations from space (WOfS)
Confirms Mueller et al.'s decision tree, its use with Landsat Collection 2 surface reflectance over Africa, and derived water observations and time summary layers.
Short excerpt: “This algorithm is applied to the Landsat Collection two surface reflectance product hosted by DE Africa”

Sections 4.1–4.2, limitations and access
Water can be missed near shorelines, in mixed pixels and in some water bodies with suspended sediment or floating vegetation. The service offers annual updates as well as processing new Landsat scenes.
