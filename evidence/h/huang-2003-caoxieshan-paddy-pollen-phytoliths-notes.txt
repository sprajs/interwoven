Pollen and phytolith records from primitive paddy fields during the Neolithic at Caoxieshan, Taihu Plain, southern China
Huang Fei, Li Minchang. Terra Australis 19, chapter 14, in Phytolith and starch research in the Australian-Pacific-Asian regions; ANU repository scan.
https://openresearch-repository.anu.edu.au/bitstream/1885/127433/1/TA_19.pdf

Original analyses of pollen and phytolith samples from field pits and archaeological layers, paired with the Caoxieshan excavation evidence.

pp. 164–165, Table 1 and Fig. 2: Pit S13 is interpreted as a natural depression by a natural ditch. Pits S′ and S15 have artificial features including ditches and furrows.

pp. 167–169, Table 2 and Fig. 3; p. 172 conclusions: The authors report rice pollen and phytoliths and a decline in pondweed-family pollen between their phases. They interpret the decline as falling local water levels and propose that changes in available water prompted construction of the artificial system. The chapter’s Majiabang terminology and phase dating are left as reported.
