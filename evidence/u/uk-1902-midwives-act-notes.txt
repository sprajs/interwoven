The statute was enacted on 31 July 1902, provided for board formation and certification, and generally commenced 1 April 1903.
Caveat: Midwifery long predates the statute. The Act established a regulatory framework, and enforcement and practice changed over time; the original statute excluded Scotland and Ireland and used a women-only definition.
