Neolithic Site of Çatalhöyük
URL: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1405/
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Locator: Description and outstanding universal value
Paraphrased research note: The site description reports 18 occupation levels on the eastern mound dated 7400–6200 BCE and clustered roof-access houses.
Limitations: The interval dates the eastern mound occupation; the source does not establish that every level had the same layout or that this was the earliest settlement.
