Passage: Kuelap Archaeological Complex and comparative analysis

The nomination describes Kuelap’s enclosure and Chachapoyas buildings with possible defensive and slope-stabilizing functions. It dates most visible site buildings broadly to 900–1470 CE, not the construction of any particular wall.
