The court quotes a 1953 disclosure of a solid crystalline polypropylene fraction with molecular-weight range approximately 5,000–20,000. It separately describes Natta's 1954 higher-molecular-weight polypropylene and testimony that later commercial products had substantially higher molecular weight than the 1953 disclosed range. Parties disputed whether the earlier disclosure was adequate; the court rejected the legal challenge, so no blanket statement that the earlier material could not be used as plastic follows. The decision is evidence about the contested record, not an independent assay of a 1957 Moplen specimen.
