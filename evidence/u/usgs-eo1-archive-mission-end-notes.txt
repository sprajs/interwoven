Overview; January 2017 notices
The original one-year validation mission was completed in November 2001 and an extended mission collected further ALI and Hyperion observations. USGS notices in 2017 describe the end of normal imaging and continued access to historical observations. Dates attached to imaging, decommissioning and spacecraft shutdown refer to different milestones or announced dates.
