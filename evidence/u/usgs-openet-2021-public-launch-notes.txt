OpenET Launch Gives EROS Scientists—and Water Managers—Reason to Celebrate
https://www.usgs.gov/news/openet-launch-gives-eros-scientists-and-water-managers-reason-celebrate
Source date: 2021-10-21. Read 2026-11-17.

Opening; Six Models Add Up to One Ensemble; Landsat and Cloud Computing Are Essential
The 21 October OpenET public opening covered 17 western states. Six models use Landsat and additional inputs to estimate field-scale evapotranspiration. The project aimed to give water managers and farmers more accessible data under water demands and scarcity. Google Earth Engine enabled regional processing.
