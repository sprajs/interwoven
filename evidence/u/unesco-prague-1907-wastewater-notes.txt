Source: Old Wastewater Treatment Plant in Prague-Bubeneč
URL: https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6485/
Accessed: 2026-11-09

Prague plant built 1901–1906 by Quido Bělský company; fully operational 1907 after trial year. The gravity-fed plant used screens, grit chambers and clarifiers before discharge. Lindley designed the sewer system.
