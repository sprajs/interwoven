Sections 1, 2.1 and 3
The JR Central authors date first commercial service to 1 July 2007. Figure 2 and section 2.1 show the new ATC supplying precise position data to the tilting system through an onboard communications network.

Section 2.2
The train body and undercarriage were streamlined, and regenerative braking increased from 12 to 14 cars per 16-car formation compared with Series 700.
