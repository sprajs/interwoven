Abstract and §1, Introduction
JR Central's operations authors date the entry of the replacement ATC to March 2006 on the Tokaido line. Its onboard controller uses precise location detection. The previous ATC used stepwise braking and the new set of ground and onboard equipment was introduced with single-stage brake control.
