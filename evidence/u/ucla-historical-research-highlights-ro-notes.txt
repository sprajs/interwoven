Reverse Osmosis (RO) Membrane — Dates UCLA's membrane breakthrough to 1959. Loeb and Sourirajan used cellulose acetate to make a membrane that retained salt while passing water at usable rates. Joseph McCutchan led work on pilot plants using the UCLA membranes from 1960. The programme led to the Coalinga pilot, where Loeb continued refining the process.
