Old Walled City of Shibam
URL: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/192/
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Locator: Brief synthesis and criteria iii–iv
Paraphrased research note: UNESCO describes a sixteenth-century walled city with mud-brick tower houses and a rectilinear street grid.
Limitations: Individual buildings have different histories; the city superseded an earlier settlement partly destroyed by flood in 1532–33.
