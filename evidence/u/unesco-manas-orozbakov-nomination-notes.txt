UNESCO Memory of the World nomination for the manuscript of Manas narrated by Sagymbay Orozbakov, sections 6.4–6.5. Describes transcription during 1922–1926, names collectors Kayum Miftakov and Ybyray Abdrahkmanov and identifies a collection of ten handwritten volumes. The nomination makes differing statements about supplies and financial assistance, so no funding conclusion is drawn. The manuscript witnesses this version, not the origin of the oral epic. Inspected 2026-11-09.
