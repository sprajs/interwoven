The Persian Qanat
URL: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1506/
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Locator: Description and management
Paraphrased research note: UNESCO describes eleven qanats in Iran and their communal management, supporting settlements and agriculture by gravity-fed aquifer water.
Limitations: The date is the inscription and documentation of a serial property, not the systems’ invention; individual qanats have different histories.
