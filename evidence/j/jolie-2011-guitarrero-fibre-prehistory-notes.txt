Table 2 and Figs. 2–3, pp. 289–291
OxA-21269 is two-ply cord, dated 12,130–11,800 cal BP. AA81781 and AA81782 are close and open twining, dated respectively 11,330–11,100 and 11,280–11,080 cal BP. The spiral-interlinked fragment AA81780 instead dates to 2310–2040 cal BP.

Figs. 2 and 4, pp. 289–292
The close-twined piece is made from leaf fibre consistent with Agavaceae or Bromeliaceae. The open piece combines twisted yarns with whole rushlike stems. Coils and plant material support on-site fibre work. Proposed uses of the surviving fragments remain interpretive.
