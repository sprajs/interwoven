Introducing New Shinkansen Rolling Stock N700S
The operator dates the start of passenger service to July 2020, describes the prototype tests as informing production specifications, and describes the N700S as the replacement for the N700A-type fleet. It lists standardized design for flexible trainset lengths and battery self-movement.
