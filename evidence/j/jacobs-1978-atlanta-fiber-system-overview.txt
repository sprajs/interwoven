Printed p. 1717 / PDF p. 1, opening and experiment objectives
The project sent 44.7 Mb/s over the entire system on 13 January 1976 at the joint Western Electric and Bell Laboratories Atlanta facility, using infrastructure intended to approximate field conditions.

Printed pp. 1720–1721 / PDF pp. 4–5, system configuration and Chicago follow-on
The source diagrams the DS3 optical system, describes the M13 multiplex and D3 channel bank and distinguishes Atlanta’s evaluation from trial customer traffic at Chicago from 11 May 1977.
