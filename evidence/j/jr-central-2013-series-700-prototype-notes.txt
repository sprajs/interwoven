p. 1, opening and Series 700 features
The release dates the C1 pre-series train to September 1997 and the start of fare-paying operation to March 1999. JR Central and JR West jointly developed the Series 700. Its Aero Stream nose cut external noise and swaying. Trial results were reflected in production trains.
