pp. 8–9, Shinkansen rolling stock succession and Series N700 and N700A
The operator identifies the Series 700 as the basis of the N700, records the N700's July 2007 introduction and says that the 700 evolved from the second generation that began with the Series 300. It also dates the N700A debut to February 2013.
