A middle stone age worked bone industry from Katanda, Upper Semliki Valley, Zaire
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7725100/
Science 268(5210), pp. 553–556, DOI 10.1126/science.7725100, original abstract via PubMed.
Accessed 2026-11-17. Attributed paraphrase of the original source passages.
Primary excavation synthesis of three Katanda sites. Its approximate age is part of the original excavators' interpretation. Brooks et al. (1995) is a companion dating report for the same sites, and later assessments retain a broader chronology.

Abstract
Reports both barbed and unbarbed worked bone points in a Middle Stone Age context and fish remains at Katanda. The authors propose an age around 90,000 years or older. The abstract does not resolve specimen-level dates, preserved attachments or use.
