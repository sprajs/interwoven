Regev, Zukerman, Hitchcock, Maeir, Weiner and Boaretto, Iron Age hydraulic plaster from Tell es-Safi/Gath, Israel, Journal of Archaeological Science 37 (2010), 3000–3009, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0305440310002207, DOI 10.1016/j.jas.2010.06.023. The publisher-rendered original abstract and introduction describe two superimposed Tell es-Safi/Gath plaster surfaces with evidence of silicate-bearing hydraulic materials, requiring imported minerals under the authors’ interpretation, about 3000 years old. The introduction cites an earlier studied Kommos, Crete floor from the 17th century BCE as highly hydraulic, and c. 1700 BCE Phaistos construction material described as slightly hydraulic; these are reports of earlier researchers, not measurements made by Regev et al. on those Cretan examples. The original Kommos and Phaistos analytical works were not independently inspected. Full original article bytes and hash are not retained.
