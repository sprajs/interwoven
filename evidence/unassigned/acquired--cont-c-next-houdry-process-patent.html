Abstract

516,489. Treating hydrocarbons catalytic apparatus and processes. HOUDRY PROCESS CORPORATION. June 29, 1938, No. 19285. Convention date, Aug. 5, 1937. [Classes 1 (i) and 32] In the cracking or other transformation of hydrocarbons, particularly for the production of gasoline, by the action of a catalyst which is alternately on stream and in regeneration, the accumulation of carbonaceous deposit is limited during any " on stream " period to less than 15 grams per litre of catalyst, and all parts of the catalyst are maintained within one inch from a heat conducting surface. The depth of catalyst traversed varies between 1 and 15 feet; a formula is given whereby its optimum value can be calculated for any predetermined value of the duration of contact between hydrocarbon and catalyst. The quantity of hydrocarbon passed during each "on stream " period, expressed as liquid measure, is less than 100' per cent., preferably less than 50 per cent. of the volume of catalyst. The velocity of the reactant vapour is between 2/5 inch and 60 inches per second. The catalyst may consist' of pieces of siliceous material with or without metallic compounds, as described in Specifications 416,025 and 478,216. It should' be maintained at a temperature of 750-1,000‹F., preferably 800-950‹F. An apparatus suitable for carrying out the treatment comprises a plurality of open-ended superimposed chambers 3 each containing catalyst M supported on grids 6. and provided with heat-exchangers R embedded in the catalyst and external heatregulating coils 7. Pipes 17, 18 with valved branches 17a, 18a enable reactants to be supplied to and withdrawn from the spaces above or below the catalyst beds in any desired combination, thus offering several alternative depths of catalyst. Similar pipes 19, 20 are provided for use during regeneration. Specifications 487,785 and 503,913, [Group IV], also are referred to.
