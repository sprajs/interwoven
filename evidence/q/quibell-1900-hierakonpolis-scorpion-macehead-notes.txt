Egyptian Research Account, Fourth Memoir, London, Bernard Quaritch, 1900, with notes on plates by Petrie. Petrie Project transcription and facsimile entry.
Inspected 2026-11-17. Original excavation publication of the object, including a developed drawing. The interpretation in the plate notes is an early reading of the same carved witness cited by Manning.

pp. 9–10, note to Pl. XXVIc and Pl. XXVIc: The note reads the scene as a king inaugurating irrigation works and identifies figures working at a canal. It does not claim the depiction records transverse basin dykes or a measured canal network.
