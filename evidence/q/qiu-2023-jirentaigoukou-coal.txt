Title: Earliest systematic coal exploitation for fuel extended to ~3600 B.P.

Authors report evidence of coal collection, storage and combustion at Jirentaigoukou, Xinjiang, around 3600 BP. BP is referenced to 1950 CE; this does not rule out earlier sporadic coal use.
