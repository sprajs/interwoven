Title: The Westinghouse Automatic Air Brake
URL: https://www.asme.org/about-asme/engineering-history/landmarks/273-westinghouse-automatic-air-brake
Accessed: 2026-11-10
Passage: Landmark overview

ASME attributes automatic air-brake development and triple-valve refinement to George Westinghouse, contrasting it with the earlier direct-air brake. Its heading lists 1869 and 1872.
