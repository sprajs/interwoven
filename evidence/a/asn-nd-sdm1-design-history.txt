Origins and SDM1 design sections
The supplier dates a 16-pair cable presentation to SubOptic 2016 and full SDM1 qualification to 2019. Its architecture increases the number of fibres and combines them with repeater pump sharing and lower per-fibre power. This account supplies neither test protocols nor a route-level equipment list.
