Lane Fox Pitt Rivers, Early Modes of Navigation (1875), Frobisher Straits paragraph, attributes to Frobisher’s 1577 voyage a large open hide-covered frame boat and a smaller one-person boat covered except at the occupant’s opening, identifying the latter as a kayak. The paper gives no specific references. Settle’s inspected voyage narrative independently describes leather on wood, two sizes and one occupant, but not the smaller boat’s deck or cockpit.
