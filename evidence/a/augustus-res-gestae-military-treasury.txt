Title: Res Gestae Divi Augusti, section 17
URL: https://droitromain.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/Anglica/resgest_engl.htm
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passage: Section 17, English translation

Augustus reports a military treasury founded in the consulship corresponding to 6 CE to pay rewards after at least twenty years of service, and claims a personal transfer of 170 million sesterces. It is a self-promotional retrospective.
