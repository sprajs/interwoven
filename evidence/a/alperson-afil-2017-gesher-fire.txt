Title: Spatial Analysis of Fire: Archaeological Approach to Recognizing Early Fire
URL: https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/full/10.1086/692721
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passage: Spatial Analysis and Fire at Gesher Benot Ya‘aqov

The author interprets spatial clusters of burned flint and repeated occupation horizons as evidence for repeated controlled fire, approximately 0.78–0.79 million years ago. Phantom hearths lack constructed hearth features.
