Attributable reading notes (paraphrased).

Printed p. 10 / PDF p. 12, High-capacity transmission systems evaluated
The operator’s parent company reports that the fiber link was put in commercial service in Chicago in May 1977 while high-capacity transmission systems were still being evaluated.

Printed p. 15 / PDF p. 17, statement by Charles Marshall, Illinois Bell Telephone Company
Illinois Bell’s Charles Marshall describes the Chicago installation as a full-service lightwave installation in 1977. The annual report supplies no cutover day or circuit logs.
