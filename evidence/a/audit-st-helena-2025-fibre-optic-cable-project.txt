Printed pp. 15 and 23, Figure 1 and subsection on branch delays
The branch landed in Rupert’s Bay on 29 August 2021. Its landing station was commissioned in June 2023. By October 2023, Sure connected its existing infrastructure, giving businesses and the general public internet access over the cable before any new domestic fibre rollout.

Printed p. 23, subsection on project delays
The branch had been scheduled for completion and public access by the end of 2021. The audit identifies pandemic travel restrictions and availability of contractors among the reasons for delay.
