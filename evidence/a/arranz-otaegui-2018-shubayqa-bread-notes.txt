Research notes (paraphrase, not a copy of source)
Source: Archaeobotanical evidence reveals the origins of bread 14,400 years ago in northeastern Jordan
URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6077754/
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passages read: Site chronology and results
Bread-like remains were found in hearths dated by short-lived plant remains to approximately 14,400–14,200 cal BP.
Limitations: The remains are interpreted as bread-like; exact ingredients and preparation varied. The dates are for hearth contexts, not the global invention of bread.
