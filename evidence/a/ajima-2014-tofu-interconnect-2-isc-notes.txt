Slides 1, 3–4 and 6, introduction, comparison and system-on-chip integration
The slides contrast original Tofu developed for K and carried in FX10 with Tofu2 designed for the next machine. The comparison retains the six-dimensional mesh/torus and adds CPU-chip integration, faster links and further functions to the second version.
