Short quotation (section 3, p. 1736): “Burned flint items occur in all eight levels of Layer II-6 (Table 1).”

Research notes:
Table 1 (p. 1735) counts burnt and unburnt flint by excavation horizon. There are eight rows, with L4b separate from L4. Counts of burnt microartifacts at L2, L3 and L6 match the three flint maps in Zohar et al. (2022), Fig. 5.
Section 3 and Fig. 2 (pp. 1736–1737) show uneven distributions of burnt flint microartifacts at successive levels and differing spatial relations with unburnt flint. Burnt and unburnt clusters are distinctly separated at L2 and are nearly coincident at L6. The L5 and L6 standardized residuals are exceptions to the typical significance within burnt clusters.
Section 4 considers natural fires and argues that the localized concentrations, frequencies of burnt flint and lake-margin conditions favor hearths from recurrent hominin use. Section 5 leaves acquiring naturally ignited fire versus making it unresolved.
