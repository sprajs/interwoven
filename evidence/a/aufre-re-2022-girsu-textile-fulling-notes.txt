The chapter places an administrative textile-finishing text, ITT 2, 902+, within its discussion of Ur III records; it quotes separate allocations of oil and alkali for garments. It discusses soap as an interpretation of fuller practice generally; the excerpt itself does not state that these ingredients were combined or establish chemical composition. The chapter gives Ur III as approximately 2112–2004 BCE.
