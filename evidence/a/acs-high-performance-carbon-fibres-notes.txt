ACS dates Union Carbide’s Thornel 25 high-modulus yarn series to late 1965 and reports a 25 million psi Young’s modulus. It discusses commercial carbon fibres but does not clearly date the first sale of this particular product, nor establish continuous manufacture. A 1970 NASA memorandum gives an earlier conflicting introduction date, requiring primary verification.
