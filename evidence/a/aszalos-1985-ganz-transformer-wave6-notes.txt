Title: Centenary of the Transformer

Passage: Exhibition, early installations and pp. 13–15

Ganz’s 1885 Budapest exhibition supplied over 1,000 lamps by transformers; the 1886 Rome plant supplied city transformers. The author, from Ganz Works, retrospectively reports close to 60 power stations delivered 1885–1890.
