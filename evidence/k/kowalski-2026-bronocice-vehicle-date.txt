Title: A first toast in Kuyavia: New evidence for drinking rituals in Neolithic Europe from Sławęcinek, Poland
URL: https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/239379/1/10.1515_pz-2025-2032.pdf
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passage: Further evidence for earliest wheeled transport in Europe

Authors discuss four-wheeled wagon imagery on the Bronocice pot and report associated radiocarbon determination GrN-19612 as 3635–3370 cal BCE at 94.5% probability; interpretation of marks and dating are archaeological inferences.
