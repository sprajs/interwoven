Source: Oral Defense: How Oral Rehydration Solutions Revolutionized the Treatment of Toxigenic Diarrhea
URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7193728/
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passages read: Historical narrative and cited 1968 studies
Review reports 1968 studies in India and then Pakistan using glucose-containing fluid either orally or by intestinal infusion.
Limitations: This broad publication event is not a claim that all trials used oral administration or that this was the first use of oral fluids; individual primary trials need examination.
