Source: Evidence for food storage and predomestication granaries 11,000 years ago in the Jordan Valley
URL: https://naturedocumentaries.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/Kujit-Finlayson_2009_Granaries.pdf
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passages read: Abstract, pp. 1–2 and figures 2–5
Authors interpret Structure 4 as a raised-floor granary, with phase I construction approximately 11,300–11,200 cal BP.
Limitations: The floor and use are archaeological reconstructions; claims about ownership and storage duration are hypotheses. The date refers to phase I at one structure.
