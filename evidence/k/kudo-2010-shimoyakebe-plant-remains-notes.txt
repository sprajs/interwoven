Characterization of Plant Remains on Jomon Potteries Excavated from the Shimo-yakebe Site, Tokyo, Japan
Yuichiro Kudo, Yuka Sasaki. Bulletin of the National Museum of Japanese History 158, 1–26, author report in Japanese with English summary.
https://rekihaku.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/1828/files/kenkyuhokoku_158_01.pdf

Original dating and context report, also used in the later comparative seed study coauthored by Sasaki. This overlaps the specimen in Lee and colleagues.

Table 1, SY 37, PLD-9088; Results and English summary: A charred Glycine sp. seed from walnut midden no. 1 is reported as 4335 ± 30 radiocarbon years BP. This is the same lab code cited by Lee and colleagues. The report places the seed in the Middle Jomon; the number 4335 is a radiocarbon determination.
