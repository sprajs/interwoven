Research notes

pp. 479–481, Discussion: Cultural transmission, task differentiation, and the deep past of knots; Fig. 1E
Classifies the published Antrea knot as a sheet bend in an alternative orientation and compares similarly classified knots at Zamostje-2, Vis-1 and Satrup. The authors leave open transmission versus convergence. Their reported Antrea age of 10,522 cal BP cites Manninen et al., rather than a new radiocarbon test.
