Summary explanatory memorandum, X Telegraph, tariff table: The government’s later historical tariff table states February 1855 initial inland tariff of one rupee for sixteen words per 400 miles of telegraph line, with a revision beginning in April 1859. The book is a retrospective tariff record, not individual payment or access evidence.
