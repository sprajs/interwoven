OpenET Collection 2.0; SSEBop model description
The project describes a deployed six-model evapotranspiration ensemble. Its SSEBop implementation uses the USGS Landsat Collection 2 Level-2 land-surface-temperature science product with other ancillary data. OpenET Collection 2.0 and USGS Landsat Collection 2 are separately named releases.
