Archaeological evidence on the early metallurgy at Çayönü Tepesi
https://www.scribd.com/document/372776679/O-zdogan-1999-Archaeological-evidence-on-the-early-metallurgy-at-Cayonu-Tepesi
The Beginnings of Metallurgy, Der Anschnitt Beiheft 9, pp. 13–22
Inspected 2026-11-17. Excavators' published report inspected as a printed facsimile. The archaeological inventory is original and its discussion of annealing draws on earlier metallographic studies on site finds.

pp. 15–16, sequence and distribution of finds: Unworked malachite is reported from Round Buildings deposits. Worked native copper and worked malachite first occur clearly in Grill Buildings and basal pits. Later workshop-area finds include blanks, finished ornaments and stone, bone and obsidian tools.
pp. 16–19, distribution table and Figs. 1 and 4: The finds are inventoried by building subphase and separately counted as worked copper, worked malachite and raw malachite. Figure 4 shows a rolled worked copper bead. The report cites metallography for indications of annealing.
