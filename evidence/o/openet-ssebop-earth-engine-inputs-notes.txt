Input Collections; Landsat Collection 2 SR/ST Input Image; Example
The implementation imports Landsat Collection 2 Level-2 scenes, explicitly including Landsat 8 LC08/C02/T1_L2. The input table lists Landsat 8 surface reflectance, surface temperature and pixel quality bands. The example imports one Landsat 8 scene for an evapotranspiration calculation.
