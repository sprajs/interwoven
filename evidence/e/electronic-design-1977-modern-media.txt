Printed p. 107 / PDF p. 121, Optical Communications subsection
The article dates the Illinois Bell downtown service evaluation to 11 May 1977 and describes voice, data and video through 24 fibers linking two switching offices and the Brunswick Building. It describes the separate GTE link between Long Beach and Artesia earlier in the same subsection.
