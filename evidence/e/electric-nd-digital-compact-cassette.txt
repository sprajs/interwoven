Philips and Matsushita, Digital Compact Cassette: Matsushita and Philips Develop New Standard, date not stated, PDF pp. 1–3, https://www.cieri.net/Documenti/Altri%20marchi/Philips%20%26%20Matsushita%20-%20Digital%20Compact%20Cassette%20%28DCC%29%20-%20Matsushita%20and%20Philips%20Develop%20New%20Standard.pdf . The handout describes a stationary multichannel thin-film head, eight digital tracks and one auxiliary track per direction, PASC subband coding at 384 kbit/s, and playback of existing analogue Compact Cassettes as well as digital recordings. It contrasts rotary-head DAT and notes tape travel makes track selection slower than discs. It does not establish a global first in stationary-head digital recording.
