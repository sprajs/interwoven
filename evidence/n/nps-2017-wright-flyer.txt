Title: The Wright Flyer
URL: https://www.nps.gov/articles/wrightflyer.htm
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passage: Aircraft description, first flight and specifications

Orville flew the powered Flyer at Kitty Hawk on 17 December 1903 for 12 seconds and 120 feet; it had wing warping, rudder, elevator and a four-cylinder engine.
