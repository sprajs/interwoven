Opening paragraphs and paragraph beginning ‘Viking Link has a converter station’
National Grid reports commercial power transfer beginning on 29 December 2023 between Bicker Fen, Lincolnshire, and Revsing, Southern Jutland. It credits Siemens Energy with the electrical design, installation and commissioning on both sides and distinguishes the initial 800 MW limit from the 1.4 GW design capacity.
