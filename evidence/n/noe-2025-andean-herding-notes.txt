Research notes (paraphrase, not a copy)
Source: Hunting to herding on the Andean Altiplano: Zooarchaeological insights into Archaic Period subsistence in the Lake Titicaca Basin, Peru
URL: https://www.foragerarchaeology.org/papers/Noe2025.pdf
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passages read: Discussion, comparison with Telarmachay
The authors report Wheeler’s proposal of management by approximately 6000 calibrated BP, based on faunal age and dental morphology.
Caveat: The date and managed status are archaeological interpretations reported by a later synthesis; hunting and herding are difficult to separate, and distinct domestication centers remain possible.
