Title: US Announcement—July 1955: Soviet announcement of Sputnik
URL: https://www.nasa.gov/history/sputnik/14.html
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passage: TASS announcement, October 5, 1957

NASA reprints the TASS statement that a Soviet satellite was placed in orbit; it describes a spherical satellite with mass 83.6 kg and two transmitters. Introductory headnote dates launch to October 4.
