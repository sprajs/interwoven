The Office of Naval Research funded Whirlwind in the 1940s. After ONR sought to reduce support in 1951, the Air Force became the major sponsor through SAGE; by 1953 core memory had replaced Whirlwind memory. Funding relates to the overall Whirlwind project, not a separate core-memory grant.
