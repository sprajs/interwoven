Quick Facts; March 18, 2013 First Light; The Instruments
Landsat 8 flew OLI and TIRS, separate reflective/multispectral and thermal sensors. First light was acquired on 18 March 2013. The Landsat 8 overview explicitly names the demonstrated EO-1 ALI approach as the source for OLI design.
Short excerpt: “Landsat 8’s first light satellite image, acquired on March 18, 2013”
