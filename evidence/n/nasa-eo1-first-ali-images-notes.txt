First three article paragraphs
NASA's contemporaneous report confirms ALI acquired and returned images of Alaska. It describes the displayed panchromatic comparison with Landsat 7 and the finer pixel size of that ALI band. The dates in the migrated paragraph are poorly typeset, so the exact ALI acquisition day is based on the instrument developer's account.
