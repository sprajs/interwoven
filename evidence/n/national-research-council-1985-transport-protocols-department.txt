Attributed research note. National Research Council, “Transport Protocols for Department of Defense Data Networks,” RFC 942, February 1985, https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc942.txt, printed pp. 25–26. The report describes the 1 January 1983 ARPANET cutover from NCP to TCP, prior parallel testing and maintenance of operational capability during the transition. It reports continuing traffic on the ARPANET component in the mid-1980s. The account does not establish that every individual host had completed conversion on the cutover date.
