Printed p. 3 places al-Muqaddasi's geographical treatise around 985 CE and reproduces an English translation, attributed in footnote 8 to Basil Anthony Collins, of his account of Ahwaz in Khuzistan. The translated account reports wheels turned by the stream, called na'ura, followed by raised water channels feeding town reservoirs and some gardens. The precise relation of those channels to those wheels is an inference made explicit by another historian, rather than wording unambiguously established by this excerpt alone. Neither the medieval Arabic manuscript nor the Collins edition was independently inspected. The author's date is approximate and this is not an observed first installation.
