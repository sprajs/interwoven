Research notes (paraphrase, not a copy of source)
Source: Dating rice remains through phytolith carbon-14 study reveals domestication at the beginning of the Holocene
URL: https://www.pnas.org/content/pnas/114/25/6486.full.pdf
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passages read: Abstract and discussion, pp. 6486–6489
Rice phytoliths at Shangshan are dated approximately 9400 cal BP; morphology suggests incipient domestication. Interpretation of domestication and precision of phytolith dating remain debated.
Limitations: The date is approximate and refers to sampled site material, not a dated invention or completed domestication. Wild, transitional and domestic interpretations are debated.
