北京东胡林遗址植物遗存浮选结果及分析 (Plant remains unearthed at the Donghulin site in Beijing: discussion on results of flotation)
Zhijun Zhao, Chaohong Zhao, Jincheng Yu, Tao Wang, Tianxing Cui, Jingning Guo. Kaogu (Archaeology) 2020 no. 7, pp. 99–106, Chinese original presented with the ResearchGate item for the 2021 English translation (DOI 10.1515/char-2021-0015).
https://www.researchgate.net/publication/356299065_Plant_remains_unearthed_at_the_Donghulin_site_in_Beijing_discussion_on_results_of_flotation

Original report on charred remains from flotation. Several authors overlap with the Yang et al. starch study, but the specimens and analyses are distinct.

pp. 100–101, Tables 2–4: Report of 14 charred specimens assigned morphologically to foxtail millet (Setaria italica), one assigned to broomcorn millet and 11 to green foxtail (Setaria viridis).

pp. 103–104, chronology and caution about displacement: Four deposit units yielded the foxtail millet grains. Four pooled grains from one unit were too small for the attempted direct AMS date. A cork-tree seed from a deposit also containing charred foxtail millet was directly dated as BA07888, 8885 ± 55 radiocarbon years BP, calibrated to 8240–7820 BCE at 95.4%. This is a context date for one millet-bearing unit.
