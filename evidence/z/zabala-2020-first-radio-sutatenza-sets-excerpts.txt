Excerpts and reading notes, 2026-11-17

p. 15, Los inicios, first paragraph
“ensambló en Sutatenza (Boyacá) el primer transmisor de la emisora”
Salcedo assembled the first transmitter in Sutatenza in 1947.

p. 15, Los inicios, final paragraphs and images 1–2
Names Irzón as an early receiving locality and describes the use of fixed-frequency receivers there. Image 1 identifies the first transmitter in Archivo ACPO 1–8, album 8, photo 4. Image 2 reproduces a receiver photograph from Álvaro Sánchez, Escuelas Radiofónicas, Ministerio de Educación Nacional, 1951, p. 41.
