Characteristics of the short rachillae of rice from archaeological sites dating to 7000 years ago
Yunfei Zheng, Guoping Sun, Xugao Chen. Chinese Science Bulletin 52, 1654–1660 (2007), DOI 10.1007/s11434-007-0258-1; publisher PDF.
https://www.sciengine.com/doi/pdfView/62b25ca4e17b44ae8e354942bdae586e

Original morphological analysis of rice grains and husks from excavated material, distinct from the sediment microfossils examined by Zong and colleagues.

pp. 1655–1657, Fig. 2 and Table 1: The authors assign 70 of the 120 short rachillae they examined from Kuahuqiao to wild type and 50 to a japonica type, and infer early rice cultivation. Morphological categories and samples do not identify individual fields or exact generations of plants.

pp. 1657–1658, Fig. 3: The team separately reports examples of wild-type and japonica-type rachilla markings in Shangshan and Xiaohuangshan material. Their rice material and morphology differ from the dated Shangshan silica samples analysed by Zuo and colleagues.
