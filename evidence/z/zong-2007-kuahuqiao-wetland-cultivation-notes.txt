Fire and flood management of coastal swamp enabled first rice paddy cultivation in east China
Yongqiang Zong, Zhongyuan Chen, James B. Innes, Chun Chen, Zhanghua Wang, Hui Wang. Nature 449, 459–462 (2007), DOI 10.1038/nature06135; author-posted publisher text.
https://www.researchgate.net/publication/5945483_Fire_and_flood_management_of_coastal_swamp_enabled_first_rice_paddy_cultivation_in_east_China

Original palaeoecological study of an excavation-face sediment sequence and associated archaeological contexts.

p. 459 and Table 1: The authors place their studied cultural sediments in a modelled interval of about 7700–7550 cal BP. This interpretation concerns one excavated sequence.

pp. 460–461, Fig. 2: Pollen, algal and fungal indicators, diatoms and microscopic charcoal are used to infer sustained wetland rice cultivation. Clearance and later maintenance are interpretations of changes in vegetation and fire indicators. Low-level brackish inflow is observed indirectly; a protective barrier is hypothesised.
