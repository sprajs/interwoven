A Southern Song commemorative account. It is a separate composition from Li Dao's annals, but the textual dependence of its account of the takeover is unestablished.

Juan 4, Account of the Yizhou Jiaozi Office (益州交子務記)
鐵錢重而劵作主户貧而劵病……天聖元年十一月戊午詔音俞焉
The account connects the bills to heavy iron cash and the crisis to failures by the households behind the bills. It explains Xue Tian's case for official administration and records approval on the wuwu day of the eleventh month of Tiansheng 1.
