Northern Song institutional compilation preserving earlier Tang material. The dated passage transmits a Tang fiscal memorial and its disposition.

Juan 89, Coinage (泉貨), Yuanhe 7, fifth month
七年五月。……伏請許令商人于戶部度支鹽鐵三司。任便換見錢。……從之。
In the fifth month of Yuanhe 7 (812), Wang Shao, Lu Tan and Wang Bo requested that merchants be allowed to exchange cash through the three fiscal offices. The record concludes that the request was granted.
