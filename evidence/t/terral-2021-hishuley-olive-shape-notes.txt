The Shape Diversity of Olive Stones Resulting from Domestication and Diversification Unveils Traits of the Oldest Known 6500-Years-Old Table Olives from Hishuley Carmel Site (Israel)
https://www.mdpi.com/2073-4395/11/11/2187
Agronomy 11(11), 2187. Publisher HTML, DOI 10.3390/agronomy11112187.
Inspected 2026-11-17. Geometric morphometric comparisons using ancient stone samples from the same Hishuley Carmel structures and a broader modern reference collection. Galili, Terral, Barazani and Dag also coauthored the original 2021 preservation study.

Materials and Methods, section 2.1; Discussion and Conclusions, Fig. 7: The outline analysis finds ancient stones resembling both presumed wild and modern cultivated forms. Some more complex morphotypes are interpreted as signs of selection. This does not identify the source of the olives or the curing material.
