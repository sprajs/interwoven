Financial improvement and continuing operation; High-speed railway route and stations
The bureau dates the first opening to 5 January 2007 and lists the seven stations of the opening section.

Core electromechanical system; Rolling-stock specifications
The bureau identifies Taiwan Shinkansen Corporation as the supplier of the core system and the Tokaido–Sanyo Shinkansen as the electromechanical reference. It identifies the Series 700 as the design basis for the 12-car 700T and specifies adaptation to Taiwan’s route and geographical conditions.
