Taiwan High Speed Rail names the N700ST and unveils its livery
2025-08-20 operator release
https://www.thsrc.com.tw/ArticleContent/e1e25e6a-4139-44d4-ac30-3f9724f08310
Inspected 2026-11-17. Research paraphrases of the original source.

Paragraphs 1–4
The operator names the N700ST as a localized N700S-based design under manufacture. It reports tailoring of interior, functions and livery, including an increase in spaces for wheelchair users from four to six relative to its existing type. The naming convention and orange-and-black livery deliberately continue the present 700T fleet's identity.
