First N700ST main-line test reaches 170 km/h
2026-11-16 operator release
https://www.thsrc.com.tw/ArticleContent/69301a4b-e742-4695-b719-823f9f3342dc
Inspected 2026-11-17. Research paraphrases of the original source.

Paragraphs 1–3
THSRC reports that the first N700ST trainset reached the day's target of 170 km/h on its 16 November 2026 first main-line trial between Yanchao and Chiayi. The test checked interaction with track and overhead power. It recalls arrival on 15 August and plans public operation starting on 1 July 2027.
