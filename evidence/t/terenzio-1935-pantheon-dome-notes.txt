Pantheon
Enciclopedia Italiana (1935), pp. 212–214, publisher’s digital transcription
https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/pantheon_(Enciclopedia-Italiana)/

Alberto Terenzio directed the 1929–1934 restoration at the Pantheon. His entry records observations of the exposed structure made during that campaign. The cited descriptions concern the built fabric of the dome.

p. 213, paragraph beginning “La cupola, impostata sull’ultimo cornicione interno”
Quoted excerpt: da tufo e scoria vulcanica leggerissima, sempre a ricorsi alternati
Terenzio describes horizontal courses in the vault. The lowest zone uses brick fragments, the next alternates tuff and brick, and the upper zone alternates tuff and very lightweight volcanic scoria. He describes the construction materials and their arrangement without assigning the quarry or linking the builders to an earlier project.
