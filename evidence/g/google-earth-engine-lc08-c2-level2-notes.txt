Description; Bands table
The distributor's own hosted image-collection entry identifies Landsat 8 OLI/TIRS as the source of calibrated reflectance and land surface temperature. SR_B1 through SR_B7 are reflectance outputs. ST_B10 is the land surface temperature band. This catalogue describes a subset of global Level-2, not the first public release.
