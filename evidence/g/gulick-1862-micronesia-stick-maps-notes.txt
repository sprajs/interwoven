Printed p. 304 reports “maps by which they retain and impart knowledge regarding the direction and relative distances of the various groups”. Gulick describes straight and curved sticks representing currents or waves, with islands located at some intersections. He reports a restriction on disclosure imposed by chiefs and describes an unnamed informant. The description refers broadly to the Marshall Islands; the paragraph that follows dates the writer's first landing on Ebon to December 1859. Nothing specifies where or when every chart was seen, the source of Gulick's hydrodynamic gloss, a named chart class, or a chart use while underway.
