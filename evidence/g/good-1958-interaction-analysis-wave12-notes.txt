pp. 361–364, §§1–4: factorial contrasts via successive dimension-wise matrix operations.
pp. 367–370, §§9–11: analogous dimension-wise operations for multidimensional discrete Fourier transforms.
pp. 370–371, §12: for mutually prime component lengths, index permutations convert the one-dimensional DFT into the corresponding multidimensional DFT. The author identifies one permutation with “the solution of the Chinese remainder problem” (p. 370). The printed index example uses 24 = 3 × 8.
p. 371, §13: the same reduction handles a modulus with coprime factors in a multidimensional transform.
p. 370, §12 and p. 372 references: cites Good's own 1951 finite Abelian-group paper for expressing a multidimensional transform as a one-dimensional one. The 1958 passage uses the converse to reduce arithmetic. The 1958 first-page receipt notice gives August 1957 without identifying the contents of the received draft.
