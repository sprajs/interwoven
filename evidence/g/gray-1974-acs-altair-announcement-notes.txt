Locator: November 1974, COMPUTER KITS, Altair 8800 Computer Kit

The November 1974 ACS Newsletter volume III number 10 describes a forthcoming Popular Electronics article on the MITS Altair 8800 kit, 8080 processor and anticipated kit pricing. This notice does not itself establish completed shipment or sale.
