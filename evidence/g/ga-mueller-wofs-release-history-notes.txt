Historical challenge – floods in Australia; Mapping surface water using satellite data
Existing flood studies largely concerned populated areas. The National Flood Risk Information Project commissioned in 2011 called for a nationally consistent historical water product. Geoscience Australia developed the automated water classifier and summarised the fraction of clear observations in which water was detected.
