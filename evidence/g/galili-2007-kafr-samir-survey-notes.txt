Galili, Rosen and Boaretto, Haifa, Kafr Samir, Hadashot Arkheologiyot 119 (10 July 2007).

Inspected 2026-11-17, including Figs. 3–5.

The 1991–1995 surveys were run by the IAA Maritime Archaeological Unit under G-26/1992 and G-21/1994 (site map references NIG 19616/74442 and OIG 14616/24442). The report names T. Sagiv for survey photography, M. Rasovsky for treatment of organic material, and E. Boaretto of the Weizmann Institute for radiocarbon dating. The survey's plant finds were transferred to Israel Museum laboratories in Jerusalem for treatment. The report does not publish a museum accession or present custody.

In winter 1994 the team recorded three fragments in a 0.90 m by 0.55 m pit, 2.3 m underwater and approximately 65 m west of the paved findspot of the earlier bowl. Soft gray clay, dozens of olive stones and braided twig pieces are reported. The Hebrew gives twig diameters of 3–5 mm (the English shows a unit typo). Fig. 4 identifies a possible base and two probable wall pieces. Alternate pair-twining is visible in the conserved fragments and rendered in Fig. 5.

The report assigns sample RT 4689 to the braiding and gives 5000–4730 cal BCE. It identifies no specific fragment sampled, and gives no uncalibrated measurement, pretreatment, calibration curve or probability. The team identified the vessel as a possible pressing basket and located a distinct crushed-olive installation several dozen metres south. No residue or use-wear test is reported for the three fragments.
