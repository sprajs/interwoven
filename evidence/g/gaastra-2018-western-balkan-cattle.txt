Title: Gaining traction on cattle exploitation: zooarchaeological evidence from the Neolithic Western Balkans
URL: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/antiquity/article/abs/gaining-traction-on-cattle-exploitation-zooarchaeological-evidence-from-the-neolithic-western-balkans/4D7976886FE66AF19A956ADA45FAA376
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passage: Abstract

The study interprets sub-pathological cattle-foot changes from eleven Neolithic western Balkan sites as occasional light traction, during a wide 6100–4500 cal BCE range. The abstract alone does not assign this use to a particular site or narrow date.
