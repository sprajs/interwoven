Source: Early production of table olives at a mid-7th millennium BP submerged site off the Carmel coast
URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7838305/
Accessed: 2026-11-09

The authors interpret crushed olives and installations at Kfar Samir (7500–7000 BP) as oil production and whole olives in structures at Hishuley Carmel (around 6600 BP) as preserved table olives. Both are archaeological interpretations; wild or cultivated trees are not settled.
