Printed pp. 12–15: Joel's childhood training at a Rongelap reef, incomplete after displacement associated with nuclear testing; 2005 Waan Aelõñ in Majel collaboration; model instruction supplemented practice in perceiving movements. Genz describes Isao Eknilang's Rongelap teaching and Thomas Bokin's knowledge from Kapinmeto (including Ujae). Pp. 29–32: rough westerly sea prevented Joel from confidently identifying wave patterns on the outbound leg. On Ujae, Bokin explained his approach by “weaving the meto through his sitting mat” (p. 30). Joel went back to Kwajalein with both Eknilang's wapepe and Bokin's meto in mind. Feeling diminished swell in the lee of atolls was a practical cue.
