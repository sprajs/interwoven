§§2.3 and 3.1, and §6 Conclusion
The JR Central authors date the Series N700's train-draft cooling of the converter to 2007. They say that the N700S built on that accumulated technology by combining the cooling architecture with silicon carbide devices and six-pole motors.

Abstract and §§4.1–4.2
Smaller underfloor equipment gave the N700S designers more flexibility for different train lengths and freed space for battery-powered self-movement during a loss of power.
