Title: Gödel’s Incompleteness Theorems
URL: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/goedel-incompleteness/
Accessed: 2026-11-09

Sections 1, 4 and 5: the 1931 paper concerns formal incompleteness and unprovability of consistency. Gödel originally treated system P and specified extensions; later general statements rely on later advances. The article was published in January 1931.
