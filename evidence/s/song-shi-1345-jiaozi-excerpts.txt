Yuan official history compiled more than three centuries after the Yizhou takeover. The opening inference has no specified chain of transmission and the surrounding narrative may share earlier Song records with other histories.

Juan 181, Food and Finance III, opening of Huizi (會子)
會子、交子之法，蓋有取於唐之飛錢。……轉運使薛田、張若穀請置益州交子務，以榷其出入，私造者禁之。
The compilers suggest that huizi and jiaozi drew from Tang flying cash, using the qualified 蓋. The section recounts failures to meet obligations and credits Xue Tian and Zhang Ruogu with requesting a government office and a ban on private issue.
