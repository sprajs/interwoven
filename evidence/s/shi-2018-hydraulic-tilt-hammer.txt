hammer is a kind of pestling machine made of stone and another change from mortar and pestle 1 . The pestle mainly depends on the strength of the hands and the gravity of the pestle itself, so it has a low efficiency. Later, the treadle-operated tilt hammer was invented and given the name of Jian Dui 践碓 (in Chinese Jian 践 means to tread). In the changing years of two Han Dynasties(around the beginning years of A.D.), the philosopher Huan Tan 桓谭 wrote the following words in his book Xin Lun 新论, "The advantage of the treadle-operated tilt hammer is ten times as productive as the pestle by hands because it gets the help from the weight of the laborer" 2 . The treadleoperated hammer is the most widely used in history and was used in some rural areas in China. This paper tells the kind of higher efficiency of the tilt hammer in ancient China, which was driven by hydraulic power. It can be divided into three types: a battery of hydraulic tilt hammers, the spoon tilt hammer and the ship tilt hammer.
