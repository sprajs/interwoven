Introduction and Improving Train Performance
The operator's retrospective identifies the 1964 line and links its dedicated track and automatic train control to safety and frequent operation. It describes the N700 as introduced July 2007 and the N700A as its February 2013 successor, built using the N700 as a basis and using results from the N700 pre-series train tests.
