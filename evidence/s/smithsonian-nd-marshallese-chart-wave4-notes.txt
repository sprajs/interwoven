Title: Stick Navigation Chart
URL: https://collections.si.edu/search/detail/edanmdm:nmnhanthropology_8552873?print=yes
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passage: Collection date and object notes

The museum documents a Marshallese chart collected in 1940–1959 and interprets the object as a pre-voyage teaching tool depicting swell interaction and islands; it was not a real-time navigation instrument.
