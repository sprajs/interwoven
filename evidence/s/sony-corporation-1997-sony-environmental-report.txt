Sony states it began producing and marketing lithium-ion batteries in 1990. Its later official accounts and other histories give 1991 as commercialization. The source does not name a buyer, date a priced offer or distinguish promotional samples from paid commercial shipments, so the discrepancy is retained instead of inferring a 1990 first sale.
