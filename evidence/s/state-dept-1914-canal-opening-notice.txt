Title: Panama Canal opening notice, July 31, 1914
URL: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1914/d17
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passage: Circular of July 31, 1914

The State Department circular announced opening to commerce from 15 August 1914 for vessels requiring no more than 30 feet of water; this is an advance notice rather than proof of transit.
