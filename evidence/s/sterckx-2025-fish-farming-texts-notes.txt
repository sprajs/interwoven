Fish Farming in Pre-modern China: A Study and Translation of Two Texts
Roel Sterckx. T’oung Pao 111, 353–396, DOI 10.1163/15685322-11103003, version of record.
https://brill.com/view/journals/tpao/111/3-4/article-p353_4.pdf

Original textual analysis and translation of surviving witnesses to historical fish farming; contextual comparison to Nakajima et al. cites their own dataset.

pp. 366–369, 372–373: Traces the fish farming text attributed to Fan Li, whose earliest transmitted version survives in the sixth-century CE Qimin yaoshu. The attribution and the title in a later catalogue do not date the work to the historical Fan Li.
