Characterization of a fully depleted CCD on high-resistivity silicon
Richard J. Stover, Mingzhi Wei, Yan J. Lee, David K. Gilmore, et al.
1997-04 | https://www.researchgate.net/publication/260660401_Characterization_of_a_fully_depleted_CCD_on_high-resistivity_silicon
Inspected 2026-11-17. Conference paper, Proceedings of SPIE 3019, DOI 10.1117/12.275174, full text displayed in an author-uploaded copy. The Lick Observatory team's characterization includes original measurements on the Berkeley device. All nine authors of the 1996 design report also coauthored this paper, which reports separate Lick-led testing.

Abstract, §2 and references [1]–[2]
The characterization paper identifies LBNL as designer and fabricator, reports tests of the 200 × 200, 300 μm CCD and attributes it to previous Berkeley p-i-n detector work. It cites the 1996 CCD design and the 1989 fabrication article separately.

§4, Observatory Testing
The team tested the back-illuminated CCD at the Lick Observatory 1 m reflector on 4 December 1996, obtaining two images of part of the Orion Nebula.
