Fairchild Semiconductor, advertisement for the CCD101, Electronics, 24 May 1973, printed pp. 43–45, https://www.worldradiohistory.com/Archive-Electronics/70s/73/Electronics-1973-05-24.pdf. The advertisement describes a 500-element linear self-scanning charge-coupled image sensor and offers it as available for prototyping at $1,200. This is an offered product, not proof of a completed transaction or the first market offer. The scanned issue was inspected through provider text; no complete local scan or original-byte hash is claimed.
