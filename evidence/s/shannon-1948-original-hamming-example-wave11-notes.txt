Inspected 2026-11-10: printed pp. 379 and 418 (PDF pp. 1 and 40).
The original July 1948 issue header is on printed page 379. Section 17, printed page 418, presents a seven-symbol error-correcting example and explicitly attributes its method to R. Hamming. The example therefore appears in the original issue, not only in the later corrected reprint. This does not establish the date of Hamming’s private work or all constructions in his 1950 paper.
