Science History Institute, “Julius Lothar Meyer and Dmitri Ivanovich Mendeleev,” https://www.sciencehistory.org/education/scientific-biographies/julius-lothar-meyer-and-dmitri-ivanovich-mendeleev/, section “Who Got There First?”, inspected 6 November 2026 through the web provider. The institute describes Meyer’s 1864 textbook arrangement of 28 elements into six families, organized using atomic weight and valence, with a blank for an undiscovered element. It describes the later 1870 atomic-volume paper and acknowledges the subsequent Meyer–Mendeleev priority dispute. The original 1864 textbook was not independently inspected in this pass. This note does not establish all predictions or settle priority.
