§1.4 (pp. AB-4–AB-6) and §§2.1, 2.10 (pp. AB-8, AB-10) — The appointed expert panel describes the demonstration at Bedok Water Reclamation Plant and its treatment train: microfiltration, aromatic polyamide thin-film composite RO and ultraviolet disinfection. The report says Bedok was selected because more than 95% of its feed was domestic wastewater, in keeping with the pilot’s source-control policy. The panel reports that the demonstration started in May 2000 and had shown consistent production after two years. The cited URL is a third-party host of the original 2002 panel report.
