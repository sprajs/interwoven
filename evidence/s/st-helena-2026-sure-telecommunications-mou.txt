Opening, funding and Sure chief executive’s statement
Government and Sure signed a memorandum for a domestic broadband and mobile upgrade to use Equiano capacity better. The operator aimed to begin deploying in early 2027 and see the first customer benefits by mid-2027. These dates were targets.
