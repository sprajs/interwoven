Primary X-ray fluorescence comparison of archaeological obsidian and geological samples. Shares Sahle with the 2013 artifact study. The obsidian measurements are distinct evidence at assemblage scale. They do not identify each of the 2013 study objects.

Abstract and source assignments, pp. 302–309
Of 26 Gademotta Formation artifacts sampled, most are assigned to the local Worja source. A minority may come from Bora or remain undetermined. Source signatures overlap for some samples. Earlier analyses from Kulkuletti involved a less certain provenance for some artifacts.
