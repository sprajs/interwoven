Inspected 2026-11-17. Selected passages paraphrased.

pp. 43–44, design of demonstration plant and Fig. 1 — The 1998 study evaluated whether reclaimed municipal effluent could supply industrial customers and supplement Singapore's raw water for potable use. At the Bedok Water Reclamation Plant demonstration, microfiltration preceded aromatic polyamide thin-film composite reverse osmosis and ultraviolet disinfection.
p. 46, Major Suppliers — The authors identify Hydranautics as the supplier of the reverse-osmosis units at the demonstration project. They do not identify an element model.
p. 46, The Future and Table 5 — The demonstration plant was commissioned in May 2000; the first full-production plants followed at Bedok and Kranji in early 2003. The authors date the first delivery of NEWater to the Bedok and Kranji surface-water reservoirs to 21 February 2003.
