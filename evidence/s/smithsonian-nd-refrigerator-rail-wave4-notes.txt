Title: Refrigerator Car Model
URL: https://americanhistory.si.edu/collections/object/nmah_1211872
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passage: Description

The museum says rail refrigerator cars using block ice were used from the 1860s and perishable-food rail distribution increased in the late 1880s. The depicted model dates to about 1905.
