Attributed partial research note. Science Museum Group, “Model of the periodic system of de Chancourtois,” collection object 1925-647, https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co13134/model-of-the-periodic-system-of-de-chancourtois-model, inspected 6 November 2026. The museum describes de Chancourtois’s 1862 paper arranging elements by atomic weight on a helical cylinder, with analogous elements aligned approximately vertically. The physical model documented by the collection was made by the museum in 1925; it is not the 1862 object. This institutional description does not by itself establish missing-element predictions or resolve competing priority claims. Only this short source-identified note is retained; the original 1862 paper was not inspected. The museum licenses its textual descriptions under CC BY 4.0.
