Attributed research note, not a reproduction. F. L. J. Sangster, “The bucket-brigade delay line,” Philips Technical Review 31 (volume labelled 1970), no. 4, pp. 97–110, https://www.worldradiohistory.com/Archive-Company-Publications/Philips-Technical-Review/70s/Philips-Technical-Review-1970.pdf. Pages 97–99 explain the earlier analog shift register in which each stage uses a capacitor and transistor and charge is passed by clocking transistor paths; p. 109 proposes applying this architecture to image arrays. The article cites Sangster and Teer's 1969 IEEE paper. This is an earlier charge-transfer architecture, not proof that the proposed imaging device was a CCD.
