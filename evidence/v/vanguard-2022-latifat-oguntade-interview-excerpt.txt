Latifat Adepeju Oguntade describes her father's work. This is family testimony about credits and NEK Video Links, with no claim by her to have observed the shoot.

Answer to Could you share how your journey as an artiste began?
“written and directed by my dad- Hammed Adesola Oguntade, aka Hamlilo.”
She names Aje Ni Iya Mi and identifies NEK Video Links as the production company. She recounts her father's running a troupe for stage work.
