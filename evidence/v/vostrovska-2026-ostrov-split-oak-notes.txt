Multi-proxy analysis of an Early Neolithic wooden well in a pastoral landscape in eastern Bohemia
Praehistorische Zeitschrift, final paginated article (online 2024)
https://doi.org/10.1515/pz-2024-2040

Original Ostrov find and plank observations, based on an assemblage separate from Altscherbitz. Willy Tegel co-authored both site studies, and the Ostrov ring series was cross-dated against the Saxon oak chronology. Article text read at https://www.academia.edu/175380587/Multi_proxy_analysis_of_an_Early_Neolithic_wooden_well_in_a_pastoral_landscape_in_eastern_Bohemia.

§3.1 Trace evidence, table 1, figs. 6–8; §3.2 Dendrochronology and radiocarbon dating
The authors identify radial splitting in individual horizontal oak planks installed in grooved posts, alongside one tangentially split plank. Dendrochronology dates the felling of the trees used for the original horizontal planks to 5256–5255 BCE. Other pieces and repairs have different dates.
