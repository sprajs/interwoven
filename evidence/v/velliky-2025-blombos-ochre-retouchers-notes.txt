Primary analysis of ochre artifacts. Mourre et al. (2010) describe a different object class, silcrete artifacts, and share one author, Christopher S. Henshilwood. The archaeological specimens were never paired across the studies.

Archeological background; Figure 2
The age estimates are of the archaeological phases. Still Bay phases M1 and M2 upper are dated to approximately 77–73 ka BP. The authors also discuss pre–Still Bay pieces, with alternative age descriptions depending on context or dating method.

The ochre retouchers; Results, Table 1 and Figure 3
The authors interpret wear on Still Bay ochre specimens CB.200 and CFB-CFC.1566 as consistent with pressure flaking. The study reports seven ochre retoucher specimens in total, including pieces in earlier deposits, some with wear also interpreted as consistent with pressure flaking. It notes that pressure-flaked products have not yet been identified in the studied pre–Still Bay sample.
