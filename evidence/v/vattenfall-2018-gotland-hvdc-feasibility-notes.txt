Section 2.3, pp. 10–11
Vattenfall reports that Gotland 1 entered service with mercury-arc valves in 1954. The converter stations added thyristor equipment in series with the mercury-arc equipment in 1970, raising line capacity to 30 MW. The original cable and terminal equipment were decommissioned and dismantled in 1986.
