Lúcia Hussak van Velthem, Mulheres de cera, argila e arumã: princípios criativos e fabricação material entre os Wayana, Mana 15(1) (2009), pp. 213–236, DOI 10.1590/S0104-93132009000100008. Full Portuguese HTML inspected at https://www.scielo.br/j/mana/a/mm9TTjTxFg4dMRWNfhWXCjQ/?lang=pt. The introduction discusses learning to make a woven tipiti and the need for a working constriction; note 2 describes the Wayana tumkëi as a cylindrical arumã-fibre basket for squeezing grated cassava, and note 4 describes extensible weave techniques. The article locates the Wayana described near the Paru de Leste River in northern Pará and was published in 2009; its appearance provides an upper bound rather than an exact fieldwork year. The article reports present practice and does not establish continuity or descent from the separate Hispaniola witness. Retained note only, since the source licence is noncommercial.
