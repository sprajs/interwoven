Title: Chariots in the Eurasian Steppe: a Bayesian approach to the emergence of horse-drawn transport in the early second millennium BC

Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passage: Grave 8 and conclusion

Grave 8 at Kamennyi Ambar-5 has wheel pits, horse remains and harness-associated artefacts; Bayesian model gives 1950–1880 cal BCE at 95.4% confidence. A radiocarbon date is not an invention date.
