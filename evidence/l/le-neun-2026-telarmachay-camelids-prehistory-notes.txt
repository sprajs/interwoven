§2.2 and Table 1, §4 and Figs. 5, 7–8
Talus morphology changes in vicuña-like camelids in layer IV. The article interprets changed mobility as possible management intensification between modeled phase boundaries of 6.4–6.0 and 5.8–4.5 ka BP. The isotope results show no major change in diet or environment.

Abstract and Conclusion
The reported bone morphology does not establish a shift to alpaca or llama phenotypes. The authors discuss protective herding as a possible alternative to captivity.
