Title: When did the first underground railway open?
URL: https://library.ltmuseum.co.uk/portal/Default/en-GB/RecordView/Index/108
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passage: Answer

Museum states that the Metropolitan Railway opened to the public on 10 January 1863, between Paddington and Farringdon Street, and reports 40,000 passengers on the first day.
