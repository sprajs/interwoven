LONGi Solar releases new solar module Hi-MO2, starting a new era of high efficiency mono PERC bifacial technology
2017-04-24 | Manufacturer announcement dated 24 April 2017
https://www.longi.com/en/news/6818/
Original manufacturer announcement on its English-language site.
Inspected 2026-11-17

Opening paragraph and Hi-MO2 product and production paragraphs
The 19 April unveiling occurred at SNEC in Shanghai. Hi-MO2 was built around monocrystalline bifacial PERC cells, with a glass rear package admitting light and prior low light-induced degradation technology from Hi-MO1. LONGi reported that the 365 W version had reached mass production by the time of the announcement.
