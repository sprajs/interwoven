Attributed partial research note, with English paraphrase of an article in German. Bernd Lemke, “Geschichte der Herzschrittmacher-Therapie in Deutschland,” Herzschrittmachertherapie + Elektrophysiologie 35 (2024), section “Erste Herzschrittmacher-Implantationen in Deutschland,” https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10924011/ , doi:10.1007/s00399-024-01010-4. The article reports that Sven Effert placed a 12 September 1961 clinic order with Heinrich G. Ulrich, the German distributor of Medtronic, for the Chardack–Greatbatch device subsequently implanted at Düsseldorf. It cites a facsimile in Weber’s 2018 dissertation, which was not independently inspected. The article distinguishes alternative personal recollections of procurement and does not establish the first worldwide sale or that this unit was the 1958 Swedish prototype. The original article is CC BY 4.0; this cache is an attributed partial note, not a reproduction of the full article.
