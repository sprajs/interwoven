Tiansheng 1 (1023 row), month 11 beginning xinmao (辛卯)

In the row 宋仁宗天圣元年, month 11 starts 16 December 1023 (Julian), xinmao. The following wuwu day is 27 days later, 12 January 1024 (Julian). The Chinese Text Project table for Tiansheng 1, month 11 lists the same wuwu day.
