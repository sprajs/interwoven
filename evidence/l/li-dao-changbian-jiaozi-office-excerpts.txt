Southern Song retrospective annals. Li Dao compares sources and gives precedence to the Chengdu ji on the disputed roles of officials.

Juan 101, Tiansheng 1, eleventh month, wuwu (戊午)
初蜀民以鐡錢重私為券謂之交子以便貿易……廢交子不復用則貿易非便但請官為置務禁民私造……戊午詔從其請始置益州交子務
The account links private bills to the weight of Sichuan iron cash, records payment failures and litigation, and says Xue Tian and Zhang Ruogu recommended official administration and a ban on private issue to preserve the convenience for trade. The court accepted and established the office on the wuwu day.
