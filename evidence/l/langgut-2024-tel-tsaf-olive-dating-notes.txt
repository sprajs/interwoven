The core area of fruit-tree cultivation: central Jordan Valley (Levant), ca. 7000 BP
https://doi.org/10.1080/01916122.2024.2347905
Palynology 48(4), 2347905. Publisher record and version of record transcribed on Academia.edu.
Inspected 2026-11-17. Same lead researcher and Tel Tsaf Area C context as the 2022 study. Table 2 adds a direct olive assay and reproduces the earlier fig date. Inspected copy at https://www.academia.edu/121002056/The_core_area_of_fruit_tree_cultivation_central_Jordan_Valley_Levant_ca_7000_BP_Langgut_2024_PALYNOLOGY.

Section 3.1 and Table 2: Tel Tsaf Area C olive branch Beta-657404 yields 7160–6950 cal BP at 68.2% probability. Fig twig Beta-585311 yields 6960–6860 cal BP at the same probability and is carried over from Langgut and Garfinkel (2022).
Section 3.1, Fig. 3: The author interprets the olive charcoal beyond the tree's natural range as cultivation. The Carmel coast and Galilee are proposed as possible earlier areas. The summarized evidence does not identify a source orchard, a route or the people who moved the planting material.
