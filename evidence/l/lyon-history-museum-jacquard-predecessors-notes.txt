The Lyon history museum dates Jacquard’s development to 1801 and describes earlier Bouchon, Falcon and Vaucanson mechanisms as elements combined in the Jacquard mechanism. The Deutsches Museum dates introduction to 1805. These accounts may use different milestones; their exact relation requires further study.
