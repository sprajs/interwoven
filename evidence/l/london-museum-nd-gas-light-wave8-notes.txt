Title: How gas lamps illuminated London

Passage: Gas lamps and company sections

The museum describes gas lighting on Westminster Bridge in 1813 and roughly 50,000 London gas lamps with 300 miles of pipes by the 1820s; it dates the company’s foundation to 1812.
