Sample chapter p. 1, Summary: Historical Development — Loeb dates his and Sourirajan's anisotropic cellulose acetate development to 1959–1960, describes the selective skin and porous substructure, and distinguishes their achievement from Reid's lower-flux cellulose acetate films. He also distinguishes the later composite approach in which the skin was added to a separate porous support.
