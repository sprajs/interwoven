High flow porous membranes for separating water from saline solutions
https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/84/02/7d/b78345f0d595d0/US3133132.pdf
Inspected 2026-11-17. Selected passages paraphrased.

Front matter and columns 1–2 — US patent 3,133,132, filed 29 November 1960 and granted 12 May 1964, identifies Loeb and Sourirajan of Los Angeles as the inventors and the Regents of the University of California as assignee. The invention addresses the difficulty of sustaining appreciable water flux while desalinating saline water under pressure.
Columns 3–6, claim 6 — The working film described is cellulose acetate; claim 6 specifies that material. The reported design advances practical membrane flux over prior porous films.
