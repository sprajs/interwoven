Source: Oral fluid therapy of cholera among Bangladesh refugees
URL: https://iris.who.int/bitstream/handle/10665/329813/whoseajphv1i1p105.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1
Accessed: 2026-11-09

The authors report oral rehydration delivered in 1971 by teams with relatives and paramedical workers at Bongaon, India. Of 3,703 admitted to the whole centre, 135 died; care included oral and intravenous therapy as appropriate. These observational outcomes do not isolate an effect of oral therapy.
