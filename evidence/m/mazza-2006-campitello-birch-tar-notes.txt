Accessed 2026-11-17. Attributed paraphrase of the original source passages.
Primary excavation report with chemical analyses of residues. The 2019 comparison refers back to these same two objects for Campitello, and independently analyzes an artifact from the Dutch North Sea.

Sections 3, 7 and 8, Figures 4–6
Two flakes bear residue at the quarry. One was found near the elephant remains and the other beneath a rib. Dating is based on geological context and associated fauna. The authors place the finds in the late Middle Pleistocene and infer a cold interval before oxygen isotope stage 6.

Section 5, p. 1315
Infrared spectroscopy and gas chromatography–mass spectrometry were used to characterize the preserved residue. The authors identify triterpenoids consistent with birch tar.
