Attributed research note, not a reproduction. MIT Computation Center, The Compatible Time-Sharing System: A Programmer's Guide, first edition, 1963, printed pp. v–vi, 5–6, 23–24 and 45, https://people.csail.mit.edu/saltzer/Multics/CTSS-Documents/CTSS_ProgrammersGuide_1963.pdf . The guide describes multiuser operation compatible with FMS programming, protected supervisor and user operation, and per-user named disk-file directories with files retained between sessions. It names BBN's independently developed PDP-1 system as another then-operating time-sharing system. The guide documents the 1963 configuration, not necessarily the 1961 demo. Pages inspected through web rendering; incomplete direct download was not retained as evidence, so no original-byte hash is claimed.
