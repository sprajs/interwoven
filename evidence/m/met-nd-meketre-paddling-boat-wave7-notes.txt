Title: Model Paddling Boat
URL: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/544211
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passage: Object description

The Met interprets a paddled northbound model and a sailing return model in Meketre’s tomb as an Abydos funerary pilgrimage; they represent ritual travel, not a documented real individual trip.
