Landsat-8 Operational Land Imager Design, Characterization and Performance

Source date: 2014. Read 2026-11-17.

Section 2, Landsat-8 History
Instrument-team paper describes ALI's pushbroom demonstration and its focal-plane modules as the starting point for OLI modules. Ball developed OLI under its NASA Goddard contract.

Sections 2–3, history and instrument description
Discusses Landsat continuity and OLI's different design for visible, near-infrared and short-wave infrared measurements. TIRS is a separate instrument.
