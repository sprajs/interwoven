The excavation of Building 1, North Area
Çatalhöyük Research Project, 1996 Archive Report

Original excavation report for Building 1.

Phase 1, Space 71 and fig. 1
The excavator reports curved wall plaster and heavily reworked floors east of oven F39. These traces suggest a ladder by the oven and access from the roof. The ladder itself was not identified.
