Printed pp. 461–462: the article's opening identifies the 1819 Paris edition. Inner pages alternate Jan. and 1820 headings.
Printed pp. 489–491: the unsigned review says the writers have put some of Laennec’s cardiac diagnostic signs “to the test of experience” and gives examples of recently observing those signs. It leaves the examination dates and sites unstated.
Printed p. 494 footnote: “The Editor having procured some cylinders from Paris, has engaged a workman in London to make them”. The note names Mr. Alnutt of Piccadilly, gives a price of four shillings and says models may be viewed at the editor’s residence. This is a published offer and account of physical transfer. The article leaves the Paris supplier and precise transfer date unnamed.
