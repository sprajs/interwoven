Attributed research note, not a reproduction. C. Mohan, Don Haderle, Bruce Lindsay, Hamid Pirahesh and Peter Schwarz, “ARIES: A Transaction Recovery Method Supporting Fine-Granularity Locking and Partial Rollbacks Using Write-Ahead Logging,” ACM Transactions on Database Systems 17(1), March 1992, printed pp. 94–99, 109–110, 136–140 and 150, https://www.csd.uoc.gr/~hy460/pdf/aries.pdf ; readable renderings of the article were also inspected at https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs345d-01/rl/aries.pdf and https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~15849g/readings/mohan92.pdf . The method combines write-ahead logging, redo that repeats missing history, compensation log records and page log-sequence numbers to support fine-granularity locks and partial rollback. The authors describe redo-only compensation records chained by UndoNxtLSN, in contrast to earlier systems that could undo compensation records and log compensation for them; they say System R used shadow-page recovery with selective redo and no compensation records. They report implementation to varying degrees in IBM OS/2 Extended Edition Database Manager and other program products by 1992; this does not identify each released recovery feature or a first customer. Write-ahead logging and crash recovery were earlier techniques.
