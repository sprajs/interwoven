pp. 26–29, Modeling the Pantheon, models 5 and 6 and maximum-tension table
Quoted excerpt: light, gradational concrete density
The full-geometry uncracked model with graded densities yielded lower maximum circumferential (hoop) tension than the corresponding uniform-heavy uncracked model. The comparison concerns the model of the whole structure and does not isolate the effect of crown scoria. It uses idealized wall geometry and support conditions and provides calculated stresses.
