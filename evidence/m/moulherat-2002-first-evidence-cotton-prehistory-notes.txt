First Evidence of Cotton at Neolithic Mehrgarh, Pakistan: Analysis of Mineralized Fibres from a Copper Bead
Journal of Archaeological Science 29(12), 1393–1401. DOI 10.1006/jasc.2001.0779. Original article as served in the web PDF reader.

Research notes

pp. 1393–1396, Introduction and Description of the Beads, Figs. 2, 4, 7–9; pp. 1398–1400
The original article identifies grave MR.84.03.158 and copper bead MR.84.03.158.01b as Period I and reports the then-current estimate of the first half of the sixth millennium BCE. Reflected-light microscopy and SEM identified mineralised threads as Gossypium sp. Morphology does not distinguish a particular modern species or domesticated stock. Copper corrosion preserved the threads.
