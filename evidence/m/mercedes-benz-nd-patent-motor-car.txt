Title: Benz Patent Motor Car: The first automobile (1885–1886)
URL: https://group.mercedes-benz.com/company/tradition/company-history/1885-1886.html
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passage: 1885–1886, first automobile and long-distance journey

Company history describes Benz car completed 1885, patent application 29 January 1886, and July public outing. Bertha Benz and sons used an improved car on a reported 180 km round trip in August 1888.
