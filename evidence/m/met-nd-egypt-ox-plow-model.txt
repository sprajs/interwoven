Title: Model of a Man Plowing
URL: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/544255
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passage: Object description and artwork details

The museum dates the Egyptian model approximately 1981–1885 BCE and interprets it as a farmer with a plow pulled by two oxen. The object is a depiction, not proof of a particular field operation.
