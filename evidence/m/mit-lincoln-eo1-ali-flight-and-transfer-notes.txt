Source date: 2017-04-12. Read 2026-11-17.

Opening and Inaugural scan
The Advanced Land Imager built at Lincoln Laboratory flew aboard the NASA EO-1 one-year validation mission launched on 21 November 2000. Its inaugural scan of Earth was on 25 November 2000 and included Sutton, Alaska.

Instrument design and transfer discussion
The line-of-detectors pushbroom instrument collected Landsat-like measurements. The laboratory delivered ALI in 1999. Its technology was transferred to industry and the Landsat 8 Operational Land Imager is based on its design.
Short excerpt: “The Landsat 8 instrument, the Operational Land Imager, is based on the ALI design”

Farewell
Lincoln Laboratory reports the EO-1 retirement in 2017 and dates the end of spacecraft operation to 30 March. The EO-1 mission and archive had already curtailed normal data acquisitions earlier that year.
