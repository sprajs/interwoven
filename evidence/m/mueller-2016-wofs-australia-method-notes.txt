Water observations from space: Mapping surface water from 25 years of Landsat imagery across Australia
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rse.2015.11.003
Source date: 2016. Read 2026-11-17.

Introduction; Section 2.1 Landsat archive; Section 3 Methods
The underlying archive is the 1987–2014 Landsat 5 and Landsat 7 imagery over Australia, processed to approximate 25 m pixels and organised into time-series tiles. The team applied a decision tree to estimate water presence per scene/pixel, then summarised those observations. The product was publicly accessible at the time of writing.
Short excerpt: “The core dataset is the Australian archive of Landsat-5 and Landsat-7 data from 1987 to 2014.”
