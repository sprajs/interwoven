pp. 3–4, Fig. 2 and Discussion: The dating and duration of Mehrgarh Period I
The reported revised envelope for Period I is 5250–4650 cal BCE. The model separately estimates a cemetery onset of 5223–4914 cal BCE and end of 4769–4679 cal BCE. The grain of those estimates is the cemetery sequence. No match to grave MR.84.03.158 was identified in the published Figure 2.
