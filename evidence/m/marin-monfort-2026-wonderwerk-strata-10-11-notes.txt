Primary analyses of small mammal fossils from Beaumont's Wonderwerk excavations, including a previously studied taphonomic collection. Berna et al. (2012) and this paper share site investigators. The new analyses largely concern a different material sample from the earlier micromorphological study, and the methods within this study assay some of the same fossils.

Wonderwerk Cave, Figure 1d and Table 1
Excavation 1 Stratum 11 sample is from lithostratigraphic unit 7 at the base of a reversed-polarity interval, bracketed by the Olduvai and Jaramillo subchrons at 1.79–1.07 Ma. The authors discuss a possible age nearer the older bound without resolving it. Stratum 10, unit 4a, is listed with two possible age correlations, approximately 0.75 or 1.0 Ma.

Results, Tables 2–3, and Conclusions
Some small mammal fossils in Strata 10 and 11 carry signatures of heating under infrared spectroscopy and luminescence. Both analyses agree on the 32 selected white or grey Stratum 11 samples. They are part of a targeted sample of 42 fossils from that stratum, not a prevalence estimate. The authors interpret localized burning deep within the cave in an occupation context as evidence consistent with fire brought in by hominins.
