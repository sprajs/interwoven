Research notes (paraphrase, not a copy)
Source: Fermented beverages of pre- and proto-historic China
URL: https://www.biomolecular-archaeology.com/wp-content/uploads/2010/03/PNASChina.pdf
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passages read: Abstract, Methods, discussion and note 10
The paper samples vessels across reported phases from approximately 7000–5800 BCE and interprets a mixed fermented beverage; phase labels and dates are not entirely consistent.
Caveat: Ingredients and fermentation are inferred from combined chemical and archaeological evidence; exact fruit and method are uncertain. The paper gives inconsistent subperiod labels for some vessels, so a broad combined range is retained rather than attributing a specific positive residue to 7000 BCE.

Independent review, 2026-11-09: Methods p. 17594 gives periods 7000–5500 BCE; note 10 p. 17598 gives 7000–5800 BCE. These conflict, and the combined broader range is retained pending chronological audit. Sixteen denotes source vessels, not necessarily positive identifications per ingredient.
