Source: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/312290
Accessed: 2026-11-09
The current Met catalogue identifies object 1979.206.86 as a cast-brass commemorative royal head by Benin brass-casting guild artists. Its date field is early fifteenth to mid-sixteenth century. The description discusses royal ancestral altars and artists working with patrons. This broader catalogue interval differs from the circa-1550 date in the earlier educators publication. Neither dates the origin of brass casting or the guild.
