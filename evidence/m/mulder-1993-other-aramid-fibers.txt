Source: Karel F. Mulder, “The Other Aramid Fibers”, in Manmade Fibers: Their Origin and Development, 1993, chapter pp. 337–360; section “A HS/HM fiber”, online author-uploaded rendering https://www.researchgate.net/publication/232557442_The_Other_Aramid_Fibers
Access: relevant chapter text was inspected in the author's online rendering. The original 1971 Russian publication cited there was not retrieved; no original PDF bytes are retained.
Mulder reports that Kudryavtsev's VNIIV group produced a high-strength/high-modulus heterocyclic-aromatic fiber in 1969 and published properties in 1971; the fiber was called Vniivlon and later improved and renamed SVM. It describes a chemistry different from PPTA, and comparable high-strength applications were contemplated. These reports must be compared alongside DuPont's PPTA liquid-crystal-spinning route; no claim is made that PPTA invented high-strength aromatic fiber worldwide or exceeded this predecessor.
