Mandl 2023, printed p. 10, reports the Gurgler Eisjoch birch-branch snow ring and a 3790–3690 calibrated BCE estimate, citing Steiner, Marzoli and Oeggl 2016. This is a material-date estimate; it does not prove the date of a particular walk or document the first snowshoe.
