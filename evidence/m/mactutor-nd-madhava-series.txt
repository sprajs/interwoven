Source: Madhava of Sangamagrama
URL: https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Madhava/
Accessed: 2026-11-09

Biography: Madhava’s mathematical writings are lost; later Kerala authors attribute series to him. Jyesthadeva’s Yuktibhasa dates to around 1550 and describes a series for an arc, equivalent in modern notation to arctangent expansion. Dating is approximate and attribution is mediated.
