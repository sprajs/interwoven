Source: Length of Very Sheer Linen Cloth
URL: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/545138
Accessed: 2026-11-09

The museum dates the woven linen sheet to ca. 1491–1473 BCE, records warp and weft selvages and evidence of ancient repair. It estimates months of work but does not date weaving separately from its object chronology.
