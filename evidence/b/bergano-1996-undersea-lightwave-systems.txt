Optical Amplifiers and Their Applications, paper TO8, abstract
The AT&T author reports deployment of erbium-doped fibre amplifier systems in both oceans and the TAT-12/13 North Atlantic ring with two transatlantic legs and two interconnects. Each fibre carried a single 5 Gb/s channel. The digital abstract has corrupted typography in the line dating the final segment. A dated independent report is used to settle the year.
