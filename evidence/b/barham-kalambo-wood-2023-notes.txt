Accessed: 2026-11-09

The structural element is from BLB5, not BLB4. Luminescence dating of its sediment cluster gave 476 ± 23 thousand years, described by the authors as a minimum. With a fading correction the estimate is older; the uncertainty is one standard deviation, not a demonstrated occupation interval. Function and hominin species are uncertain.
