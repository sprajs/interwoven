Title: Microstratigraphic evidence of in situ fire in the Acheulean strata of Wonderwerk Cave
URL: https://www.pnas.org/content/pnas/109/20/E1215.full.pdf
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passage: Abstract and discussion

The authors report burned bone and ashed plant remains in cave occupation sediments around 1 million years old; no constructed combustion features were identified, and human control is an inference, not direct observation.
