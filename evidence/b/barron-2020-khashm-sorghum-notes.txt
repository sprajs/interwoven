Source: Snapshots in time: MicroCT scanning of pottery sherds determines early domestication of sorghum in East Africa
URL: https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10114710/1/Snapshots%20in%20Time_Sorghum_final%20ms.pdf
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passages read: Abstract, chronology and discussion
The site yielded mixed sorghum morphologies; the authors report approximately 73% non-shattering among identifiable specimens and a 3700–2900 BC contextual chronology.
Limitations: The reported chronology uses broad one-sigma charcoal dates, potentially affected by old wood. The location and timing of initial cultivation are unknown.
