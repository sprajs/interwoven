pp. 79–82, §§3–4, figs. 1, 3 and 5, fragment No. 10
Quoted excerpt: in well compacted, alternating courses parallel to the flat upper and lower surfaces of the vault
The authors document yellow tuff and brown scoria as alternating coarse aggregate courses in fragment 10, and describe distinct fine ingredients in its mortar. A 1932 excavation photograph records the fragment, attributed to a minor aisle on the northwest side.
