Attributable reading notes (paraphrased).

Printed pp. 1745–1747 / PDF pp. 29–31, §§II–III and Figs. 1–2
Bell Laboratories stacked twelve adhesive-sandwich ribbons of twelve graded-index Western Electric glass fibers each into a protected cable. The linear arrays eased fiber handling and group splicing. Four of sixteen manufactured ribbons were discarded and the selected twelve had fiber breaks.

Printed p. 1756 / PDF p. 40, §VII
The authors describe the follow-on Chicago cable as using just two ribbons while keeping the same design and undergoing evaluation with live customer traffic.
