Pages 3–4, Antrea entry: A fishing net of twined willow bast, with surviving knots, bark floats and stone sinkers, was recovered in Karelia, present-day Russia. The authors give 8400–8300 cal BC on the basis of reported radiocarbon and sediment ages. This is a secondary synthesis of the find and dating.
