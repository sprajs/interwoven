Attributed research note, not a reproduction. C. Gordon Bell, J. Craig Mudge and John E. McNamara, Computer Engineering: A DEC View of Hardware Systems Design, 1978, printed pp. 140–141, https://bitsavers.org/pdf/dec/_Books/Bell-ComputerEngineering.pdf . The account describes BBN's PDP-1 time-sharing system as operating from September 1962 with five terminal users, a protected supervisor, restricted mode and timer. BBN therefore predates CTSS's 1963 community-use milestone in protected time-sharing; memory protection itself is not attributed to CTSS as a unique advance. This does not assess every earlier worldwide file system. Passage inspected through web rendering; no original-byte hash is claimed.
