David L. Bates, “Cotton-Spinning in Northampton: Edward Cave’s Mill, 1742–1761,” Northamptonshire Past & Present, volume 9 number 3, 1996–97, printed pp. 237–251, https://www.northamptonshirerecordsociety.org.uk/pdf/npp/volume-9/npp-v9-n3.pdf.
Inspected printed p. 240: no actual machines survive and the machine details are sparse; the 1758 patented construction used single roller pairs with the bobbin drawing, while the earlier patent described two pairs. Printed p. 250 discusses the Northampton factory’s commercial spinning and mechanical complications. The document does not itself prove that the earlier operating mill used the differential-pair rather than single-pair system.
