p. 19, early chronology; p. 20, note 8
“El 28 de septiembre de 1947 se realiza la primera transmisión cultural”
Gives a different first cultural broadcast day from the 2020 library bulletin. It reports early radio-school experiments in both Sutatenza and Tibirita and identifies Sutatenza’s parish priest José Ramón Sabogal as a supporter.
