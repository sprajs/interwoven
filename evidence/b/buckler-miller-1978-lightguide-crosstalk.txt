Printed pp. 1765–1768 / PDF pp. 49–52, §IV
The authors measured the installed Franklin–Wabash and Franklin–Brunswick links from optical distributing frames. They give the change from twelve to two twelve-fiber ribbons, the ten connectorized segments and array splices. The paper shares Buckler with the cable manufacture report but presents measurements on the receiving installation.
