Inspected locations: Abstract and Results, BD 1 chronology and flaking analysis.

The study describes flaked-stone cores and flakes at Bokol Dora 1 and uses the chronology to place the studied assemblage around or older than 2.58 million years. The analysis identifies percussive flaking and compares the assemblage with other early stone-tool sites. Later scholars contest the precise earliest-Oldowan age; this comparison relies only on a well-established ancient percussive-flaking tradition, not the priority of the Bokol Dora claim. The article does not establish that particular flake tools were used with or without handles.
