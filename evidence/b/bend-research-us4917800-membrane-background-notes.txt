Functional, photochemically active, and chemically asymmetric membranes by interfacial polymerization of derivatized multifunctional prepolymers

Inspected 2026-11-17. Selected passages paraphrased.

Background of the Invention, discussion of US 3,133,132 and US 4,277,344 — Independent inventors in membrane research distinguish the original Loeb–Sourirajan thin selective membrane from Cadotte's later advance in thin selective membranes, which uses interfacial polymerization to form a supported selective layer. They identify Morgan's work on interfacial polymerization as a separate input to Cadotte.
