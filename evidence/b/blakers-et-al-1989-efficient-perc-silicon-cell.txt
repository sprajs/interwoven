p. 1363, facsimile on the two slides titled First paper PERC solar cell (1989)
The paper names the authors and the Solar Photovoltaic Laboratory at UNSW, Kensington. It describes a passivated emitter and rear cell and reports independently verified conversion efficiency up to 22.8%. The paper gives manuscript receipt on 9 May 1989, acceptance on 18 July and issue publication on 25 September. The reproduced section diagram depicts an insulating rear oxide and discrete rear contacts.
