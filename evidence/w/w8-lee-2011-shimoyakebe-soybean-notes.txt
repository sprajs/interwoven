Source: Archaeological Soybean (Glycine max) in East Asia: Does Size Matter?
URL: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0026720
Accessed: 2026-11-09

The researchers compare soybean seed sizes and identify large Middle Jomon specimens at Shimoyakebe around 5,000 calibrated years before 1950, interpreting enlargement as evidence consistent with selection. Size is an imperfect proxy for domestication, and the finding does not establish a single point of origin.
