Accessed 2026-11-10. Paraphrased research notes.
First commercial mission to Patna, letter V, pp. 76–77: The 1914 edition transcribes Robert Hughes’s 14 September 1620 letter from Patna to Agra, recording about 2,000 rupees received against prior bills, while other sums were accepted and anticipated. The 3 September letter discusses local hundis and risk of bankruptcy. The archive manuscript was not inspected.
