The Evolution of Time Measurement, Part 2: Quartz Clocks
Research notes, accessed 2026-11-09

Printed pp. 43–44 describe a 1927 paper presented by J. W. Horton and W. A. Marrison describing a quartz-referenced 50 kHz clock, and the development of quartz oscillators as frequency standards. It distinguishes the quartz oscillator from the later clock mechanism.
