Ellen Rosand, Opera in Seventeenth-Century Venice: The Creation of a Genre, discussion of Andromeda, pp. 70–71. Interprets Bariletti’s 1637 libretto: Ferrari’s company largely financed staging with some help; identifies Madalena Manelli as the title-role singer and Francesco Manelli as composer. Rosand notes the named cast yields seven singers rather than the six mentioned in Bariletti’s earlier description. Inspected 2026-11-09.
