Watch, W.A., 1571
Research notes, accessed 2026-11-09

The Met catalogues a 1571 watch by W.A., attributed to Antwerp or possibly Ghent. It describes a mainspring, fusee, verge escapement, balance and hog’s-bristle regulator; the gut line is missing and the verge and balance may be replacements. The catalogue rejects a confident identification of the maker as Walter Arsenius.
