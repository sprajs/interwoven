This company anniversary history says Göransson visited Bessemer’s London workshop in spring 1857, after London correspondents Hoare Buxton had offered a part of their newly acquired Swedish patent; Bessemer undertook to send equipment and an expert to Edsken. It says the agreement was concluded, equipment installed that autumn and production attempted from November 1857. It is a retrospective account by an interested company, and does not by itself establish the precise patent share or later commercial success.
