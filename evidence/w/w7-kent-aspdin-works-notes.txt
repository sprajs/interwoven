Aspdin’s kiln, monument record TQ 67 SW 77
https://heritage.kent.gov.uk/Monument/mke1703
Accessed 2026-11-09.

Kent’s heritage record identifies the site as Parker and Wyatt’s earlier Roman-cement works, acquired by William Aspdin in 1846, with partners Maude and Jones. It states the later 1850s works contained nine bottle kilns and produced a cement called Nine Bottle Cement. These are site-wide retrospective counts, not annual production or an exact 1846 kiln count.
