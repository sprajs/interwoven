Sehasseh and colleagues, Early Middle Stone Age personal ornaments from Bizmoune Cave (2021).
URL: https://www.science.org/doi/pdf/10.1126/sciadv.abi8620?download=true
Accessible full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8457661/
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Paraphrased: The 33 shell finds include 32 from 4c and one from 4a; most perforations are partly natural, with some further modification and use-wear interpreted as ornaments. The overlying flowstone in 4a is 102,980 ± 9,620 years (2σ). A separate speleothem in 4c is about 142 ka, and layer 4c luminescence dates are markedly younger and inconsistent with U-series dates. All dating is contextual and stratigraphic.
