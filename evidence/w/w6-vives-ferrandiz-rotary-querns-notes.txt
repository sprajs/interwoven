Source: Saddle querns and rotary mills from the Iron Age site of La Cervera
URL: https://una-editions.fr/saddle-querns-and-rotary-mills-from-la-cervera/
Accessed: 2026-11-09

The authors date early rotary querns in northeastern Iberia to approximately 500–450 BCE, including Els Vilars; they describe the latter as mid-fifth century and possibly imported from the coast. The general account identifies grain milling and household-sized querns; neither a world origin nor actual use of that particular example is established.
