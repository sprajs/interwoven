CERN/IT/97/5, “Current Cable Systems”, TAT-12 and TAT-13 paragraph
The CERN network engineer records that TAT-12/13 entered service in 1996. The two cables operated as one mutually backed-up system using erbium-doped fibre amplifiers between the United States, England and France. Williams describes this as an overview by a non-specialist drawing on multiple sources.
