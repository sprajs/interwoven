Source: Kwii.aang and Da.axiigang, spruce-root hat
URL: https://americanindian.si.edu/exhibitions/infinityofnations/northwest-coast/196110.html
Accessed: 2026-11-10

The Smithsonian dates the Haida hat to approximately 1900, attributes its weaving to Kwii.aang (Isabella Edenshaw) and painting to Da.axiigang (Charles Edenshaw), and presents family testimony on weaving. This one object does not date the origin of the wider craft.
