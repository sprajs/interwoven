The federal expert report describes Hans Eggenberger’s 1922 campaign in Appenzell Ausserrhoden, a petition with 3,480 signatures, cantonal approval and iodized salt distribution. It also describes earlier work by Otto Bayard and a later national recommendation; signatures are not consumers or coverage.
