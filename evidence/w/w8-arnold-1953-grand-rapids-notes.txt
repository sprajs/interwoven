Source: Effect of Fluoridated Public Water Supplies on Dental Caries Prevalence
URL: https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/71858/cdc_71858_DS1.pdf
Accessed: 2026-11-09

Age-group increments of decayed, missing or filled permanent teeth per child between ages six and nine were 1.781 in 1951 versus 3.123 before fluoridation, about 57 percent. Authors interpret these as mainly reflecting first permanent molars. This observational figure is not a 57-percent reduction or individual effect.
