Source: High-Altitude Archaeology Uncovers Earliest Evidence of Potato Consumption
URL: https://graduatedivision.ucmerced.edu/node/4021
Accessed: 2026-11-09

The university account reports potato consumption evidence at Jiskairumoko, Peru, dated between approximately 3400 and 2200 BCE. Its report describes the team’s 2016 study; the remains are archaeological interpretations, not direct proof that cultivation originated at this site.
