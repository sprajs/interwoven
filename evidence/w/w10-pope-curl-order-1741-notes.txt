Pope v Curl: entry from the Court’s Book of Orders
https://www.copyrighthistory.org/cam/tools/request/showRepresentation.php?id=representation_uk_1741d
Accessed 2026-11-10. Paraphrased research notes.
On Friday 5 June 1741 the court ordered a temporary injunction restraining Curll and agents from printing, publishing and vending the specified Pope letters pending an answer and further order. The order reproduces allegations, not proof of final judgment or payment.
