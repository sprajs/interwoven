The Old Furnace at Darby Ironworks, Coalbrookdale
https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/heritage-gateway/results/record/2f000900-da89-446c-af1a-55ae6fb05a9d/MSA453
Accessed 2026-11-09.

The aggregated heritage record describes the pre-existing 1638 charcoal furnace, Darby’s 1708 lease, nearby ironstone, and coke-fuel iron smelting in 1709. Waterwheel marks are archaeological observations but their exact period is not established. Avoid claiming 1709 volume, financial return or global first.
