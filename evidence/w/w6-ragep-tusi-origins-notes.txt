Title: The origins of the Ṭūsī-couple revisited
URL: https://zenodo.org/records/3928498/files/Instruments%20Observations%20Theories.pdf?download=1
Accessed: 2026-11-09

Pages 229–236 discuss the rectilinear couple in the Ḥall and a later manuscript colophon giving its completion as late October 1245 in Tūn. Later versions and disputed historical transmission are distinct.
