WHO, Statue commemorates smallpox eradication (2010).
URL: https://www.who.int/news/item/17-05-2010-statue-commemorates-smallpox-eradication
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Paraphrased notes: WHO traces the smallpox vaccination approach to Jenner in 1796 and describes vaccination and surveillance in the global eradication campaign. It reports the Assembly declaration of 8 May 1980 and estimates programme costs during 1967–1980 at US$300 million, with about two thirds borne by endemic countries. The account is retrospective and does not state a price base.
