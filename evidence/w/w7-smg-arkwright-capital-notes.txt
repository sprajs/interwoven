The museum account says Samuel Need and Jedediah Strutt invested £500 in January 1770 and became partners. The partnership commissioned conversion into a horse-powered mill, which entered operation in December 1772. It identifies a later Cromford site with supplies of water and labour. This is a retrospective institutional account, not the partnership agreement.
