Attributed research note, not a reproduction. Bert Whyte, “Behind the Scenes: Tales from the Strip,” Audio, April 1983, printed p. 12, https://www.worldradiohistory.com/Archive-All-Audio/Archive-Audio/80s/Audio-1983-04.pdf . An indexed rendering of the contemporary trade-show report was inspected; direct retrieval returned 403. The report describes Sharp’s compact-cassette CX-3 digital recorder with a stationary 18-track head, 14-bit quantization at 44.1 kHz, and two-channel stereo. It says no price or marketing date had yet been provided. The passage does not establish compatibility with previously recorded analogue cassettes. Its description precedes a later 1991 magazine recollection that dates a CX-3 prototype to 1985. An indicative uncompressed audio-sample rate calculated from the report is 14 × 44,100 × 2 = 1,234,800 bits/s, before error correction and overhead; it is not the measured rate written to tape. No original-byte hash is claimed.
