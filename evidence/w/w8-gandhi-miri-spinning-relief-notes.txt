The Wheel of Fortune: Spinning as Famine Relief
https://www.gutenberg.org/files/41954/41954-h/41954-h.htm
Accessed 2026-11-09

The published account describes spinning at Miri near Ahmednagar, with wheels lent due to poverty and made by local carpenters. It reports two Rs 100 donations, six annas per pound and 29 wheels running. These are project claims, not independently audited general wages or famine outcomes.
