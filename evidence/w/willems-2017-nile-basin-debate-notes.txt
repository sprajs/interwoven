The Analysis of Historical Maps as an Avenue to the Interpretation of Pre-Industrial Irrigation Practices in Egypt
https://doi.org/10.14361/9783839436158-010
The Nile: Natural and Cultural Landscape in Egypt, pp. 255–344. Publisher PDF at https://www.inlibra.com/document/download/pdf/uuid/486029aa-9cbf-38b8-83f4-dffa90a07aa9?page=1&toc=5723285.
Inspected 2026-11-17. Historical-map and archaeological analysis. Early hydraulic interpretations on the cited pages discuss Butzer and Schenkel. The separate Fifth Dynasty relief is cited to Willems (2012), pp. 1101–1107.

Section 1, pp. 259–260 and n. 12: Schenkel disputed that the Scorpion macehead depicts an irrigation canal. The authors cite a scene in Niuserre's separate Fifth Dynasty sun temple as showing a dyke around a flooded basin and distinguish early local basins from later extensive networks.
