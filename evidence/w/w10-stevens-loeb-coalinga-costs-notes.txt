Reverse osmosis desalination costs derived from the Coalinga pilot plant operation
Research notes, accessed 2026-11-10

The 1967 abstract reports a 5,000-gallon-per-day Coalinga pilot supplying a supplement to drinking water from June 1965. It reports $1.94 per thousand gallons for the combined experimental and production unit and separately gives projected costs for hypothetical production-only plants. The abstract does not state a consumer tariff or precise accounting interval.
