The researchers infer northern Quadra Island gardens existed at least 3500 years ago from below-wall shells and relative sea level; old-shell effects and later modification complicate wall chronology. For two deposits the paper reports older radiocarbon/sea-level results than previous OSL dates of about 1200–1900 years, with OSL methodology under review. Kee k us (Elizabeth Harry) told a historian in 1993 of maintaining a Waiatt Bay wall during her lifetime.
