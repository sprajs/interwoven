Kolmogorov’s contributions to the foundations of probability
Source: https://glennshafer.com/assets/downloads/articles/article68.pdf
Locator: Introduction and historical discussion, pp. 1–4
Kolmogorov’s 1933 Grundbegriffe gave a measure-theoretic axiomatization of probability.
The 1933 German original built on earlier work in measure theory and probability; later English translations are not the original publication.
