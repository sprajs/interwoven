NV-10-H Injection moulding machine, with instruction card
https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co9913
Accessed 2026-11-09

The museum dates a Nene Valley Tool Company injection-moulding machine to 1930–1940 and reports later use by Emitome to make hearing aids. The catalogue does not date that use, quantify output or specify the polymer.
