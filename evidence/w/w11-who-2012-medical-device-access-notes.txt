WHO dates Jaipur Foot development to 1968, reports free provision under BMVSS from 1975 and 59 limbs fitted in its first year. It reports Aurolab producing nearly 35,000 lenses in 1992 at roughly US$10 each. The counts are not patient counts and the cost is not necessarily a patient charge; a separate overview states US$5 on an unclear basis.
