30,000-Year-Old Wild Flax Fibers
Research notes, accessed 2026-11-09

The authors describe plant fibres in Dzudzuana Cave, Georgia. Unit D includes fibres they describe as spun and a twisted thread; they report its contextual occupation interval as 32–26 ka radiocarbon BP, equivalent to 36–31 ka calibrated BP. Dates are based on associated material, not direct fibre dating. Identification as flax is disputed in a later technical comment.
