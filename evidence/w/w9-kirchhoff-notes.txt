Chemical analysis by observation of spectra
Source: https://www.chemteam.info/Chem-History/Kirchhoff-Bunsen-1860.html
Locator: Opening paragraphs; sodium and lithium sections
Kirchhoff and Bunsen described qualitative chemical analysis using characteristic bright spectral lines, focusing on alkali and alkaline-earth metals.
Earlier spectral observations preceded this paper. The English excerpt is a later presentation of the 1860 German work.
