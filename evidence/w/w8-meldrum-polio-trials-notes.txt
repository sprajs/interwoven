Source: A calculated risk: the Salk polio vaccine field trials of 1954
URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1114166/
Accessed: 2026-11-09

The historian reports 623,972 children in vaccine-or-placebo groups and over a million in observed-control groups in the 1954 US field trials, sponsored by the National Foundation for Infantile Paralysis. Trial designs differed; the count is not the number vaccinated or a population adoption rate.
