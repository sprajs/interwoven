Historical context: the account dates the larger Tuskegee gift to 1912, states that Washington persuaded Rosenwald to designate $2,800 for an equal-match six-school experiment, and dates a later $30,000 commitment to 1914. A 1912 gift or arrangement does not establish the completion dates of all six buildings.
