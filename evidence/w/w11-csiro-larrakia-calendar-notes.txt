The calendar names Gulumoerrgin/Larrakia contributors Lorraine Williams, Judith Williams, Maureen Ogden, Keith Risk and Anne Risk. It credits CSIRO science co-design in 2012 and knowledge sourced from Larrakia and Limilngan elders in 1989–1994. The notice reserves community ownership and control and restricts reproduction; this note records only attribution and dating metadata.
