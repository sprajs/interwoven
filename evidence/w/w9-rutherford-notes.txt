The scattering of α and β particles by matter and the structure of the atom
Source: https://www.lnl.infn.it/wp-content/uploads/Articolo_Rutherford_1911.pdf
Locator: pp. 669–671, §1 and notes
In the May 1911 paper Rutherford used Geiger–Marsden observations and an intense concentrated atomic charge to account for large-angle scattering.
The credited observations date from 1909 and 1910. A February 1911 oral account is mentioned. The model predates the neutron and modern nuclear theory.
