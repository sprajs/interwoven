Hsu interprets the Chunyu Yi memoir in chapter 105 of the Shiji as an early extensive account of pulse-based diagnostic reasoning and dates composition conventionally around 86 BCE while warning of complex textual history. Only the publisher’s introductory excerpt was inspected, not the complete translation or a contemporary manuscript.
