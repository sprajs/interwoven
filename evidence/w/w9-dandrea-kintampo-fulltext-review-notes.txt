D’Andrea et al. (2007), pp. 688–689, table 2: one cowpea cotyledon from B6B horizon 4 was dated by AMS to 1830–1595 calibrated BCE at 95.5% confidence. The authors identify 16 fragmentary specimens as domesticated cowpea; only three were complete enough for limited measurement, and morphology is compared with domesticated and wild forms. This is evidence from one site, not a demonstrated origin. Selected passages inspected.
