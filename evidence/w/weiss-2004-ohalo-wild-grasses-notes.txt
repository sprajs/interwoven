Source: The broad spectrum revisited: Evidence from plant remains
URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC470712/
Accessed: 2026-11-09

Ohalo II, about 23,000 calibrated years BP, yielded more than 90,000 plant remains. The authors interpret the assemblage as evidence for gathered wild grasses and cereals; grains cluster around a grinding stone.
