Armillas’s reconstruction estimates more than 120 square kilometres of reclaimed swamp, excluding islands, and more than 9,000 hectares of productive soil after subtracting one quarter for canals and pools. It is a reconstruction using aerial evidence, not a measurement of annual cultivated area. He interprets the regular pattern as evidence for higher-level planning, while the timing of the plan remains uncertain.
