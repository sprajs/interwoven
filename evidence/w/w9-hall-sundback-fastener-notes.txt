How Gideon Sundback Perfected the Zipper
https://www.invent.org/blog/inventors/gideon-sundback-zipper
Accessed 2026-11-09

The institutional history reports that New York tailor Robert J. Ewig used Sundback hookless fasteners for money belts in 1917. It describes later uses and a manufacturing machine separately; it does not establish earlier invention of all slide fasteners.
