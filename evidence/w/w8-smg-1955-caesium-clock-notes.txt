Caesium Atomic Clock, 1955
Research notes, accessed 2026-11-09

Catalogue object 1962-59 is a caesium clock built by Louis Essen and J. V. L. Parry at the National Physical Laboratory, Teddington in 1955. The museum expresses its accuracy as one second in 300 years; this is an equivalent rate, not an observed 300-year operation.
