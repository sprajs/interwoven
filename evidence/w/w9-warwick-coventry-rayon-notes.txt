Artificial Fibres History: Courtaulds and the Rayon Boom
https://warwickshireias.org/artificial-fibres-industry/
Accessed 2026-11-09

The regional industrial history describes Courtaulds’ acquisition of viscose patents in 1904, the Coventry factory in 1905, nine operating spinning machines by late 1906, local uses for the yarn and nearby transport and fuel. Retrospective account; these facts do not establish world priority.
