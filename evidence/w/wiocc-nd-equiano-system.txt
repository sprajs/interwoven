Design, Technology, Global Reach and Lagos Landing Station
WIOCC describes the constructed cable as having 12 fibre pairs and pair-level optical switching. It lists the Portugal to South Africa route with Lagos, Lomé, Swakopmund and Rupert’s Bay among the landings. WIOCC describes independent control of the terminating equipment and its own fibre pair.
