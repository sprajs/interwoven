Nassarius kraussianus shell beads from Blombos Cave: evidence for symbolic behaviour in the Middle Stone Age
Research notes, accessed 2026-11-09

The repository abstract reports 39 perforated shell beads from M1, dated approximately 75 ka by luminescence, and two from M2, provisionally approximately 78 ka. Microscopic wear is interpreted as evidence that shells were strung and worn. The M1 date belongs to the archaeological context; it is not a direct date for each bead.
