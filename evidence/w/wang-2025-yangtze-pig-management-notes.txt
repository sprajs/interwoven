Source: Early evidence for pig domestication in the Lower Yangtze, South China

Passages read: Abstract and discussion
The authors interpret dietary and dental evidence from both sites as indicating a mixture of managed and wild or free-ranging pigs.
Caveat: Human management and domestic status are inferred; the ranges cover multiple contexts and do not date a single origin or prove independent domestication.
