Source: Searching for the origins of African rice domestication
URL: https://antiquity.ac.uk/projgall/murray
Accessed: 2026-11-09

Three early rice samples from Dia have calibrated intervals of 799–413, 785–411 and 791–413 BCE. Most rice remains lacked diagnostic husks, so the domestic status of each early grain is not established; the broader assemblage is interpreted as consistent with African rice cultivation.
