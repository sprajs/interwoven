The historian describes the 14 September 1968 People’s Daily report from Jiangzhen Commune and subsequent national promotion. Production brigades financed cooperative stations, and the chapter estimates 87 percent of production brigades had such stations by 1983. That denominator is brigades, not people or patients; coverage and services varied.
