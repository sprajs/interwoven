Eugene Wong and Karel Youssefi, “Decomposition: A Strategy for Query Processing,” University of California Berkeley technical report UCB/ERL M574, 15 January 1976, §7, printed pp. 21–24 / PDF pp. 23–26, https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED118131.pdf . Before System R's 1979 paper, INGRES decomposed multivariable queries, formulated a cost recurrence, and studied cost estimates for tuple substitutions. Its operational January 1976 implementation used relation size as a simplified choice rule; more detailed cost estimates were proposed for experiment. This is not evidence of the identical implemented System R subset-enumeration architecture.
