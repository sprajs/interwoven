Coventry City of Culture 2021: Unravelling a history
https://blog.sciencemuseum.org.uk/coventry-city-of-culture-2021-unravelling-a-history/
Accessed 2026-11-09

The Science Museum dates the Courtaulds artificial-silk factory in Coventry to 1905 and describes the British Nylon Spinners factory producing nylon yarn in Britain in 1941. Viscose rayon is regenerated cellulose, distinct from synthetic nylon.
