Title: Gideon v. Wainwright, 372 U.S. 335
URL: https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/372/335/
Accessed: 2026-11-09

The Court decided on 18 March 1963 that Gideon, charged with a Florida noncapital felony, had a right to appointed counsel because he could not afford one; it overruled Betts. This is a legal ruling, not evidence of effective counsel everywhere.
