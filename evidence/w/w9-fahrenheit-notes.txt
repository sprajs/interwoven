Evolution of the Thermometer, 1592–1743
Source: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Evolution_of_the_Thermometer/Chapter_4
Locator: Chapter IV, Fahrenheit papers and scale discussion
Fahrenheit’s 1724 papers described thermometry using a scale referenced to a cold mixture and human body heat.
This is a later historical reconstruction, not the original paper. Modern fixed-point definitions should not be projected onto every early instrument.
