Title: $80 Million Loan for Japanese Railways
URL: https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/965701586270216549/pdf/Announcement-of-Eighty-Million-US-Dollars-Loan-for-Japanese-Railways-on-May-2-1961.pdf
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passage: Press release, May 2 1961, pp. 1–2

The World Bank announced a loan equivalent to US$80 million to Japanese National Railways for its new Tokaido line, amid a 1961 projected cost of $548 million. The loan is funding, not construction spending or total final cost.
