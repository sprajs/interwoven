The Arrangement of Field Experiments
Source: https://pdixon.stat.iastate.edu/stat571/readings/Fisher%201926.pdf
Locator: Journal of the Ministry of Agriculture 33, pp. 503–513, discussion of random arrangements and blocks
Fisher’s 1926 article connected random plot arrangements and blocking with estimation of experimental errors in field comparisons.
Random allocation and comparative experiments had earlier precedents. The article does not establish a first invention of randomization.
