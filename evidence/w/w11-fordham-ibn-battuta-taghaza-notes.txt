Ibn Battuta describes reaching Taghaza after a 1352 departure, rock salt extracted in slabs by enslaved workers under Massufa ownership, imported food, and prices of eight to ten mithqals per salt load at Iwalatan. The translated travel narrative is retrospective and its price is a reported local figure.

Fordham identifies this as H. A. R. Gibb’s translation and edition published in 1929. The narrative was recorded after the 1352 journey.
