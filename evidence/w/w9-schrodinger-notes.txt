Quantisierung als Eigenwertproblem: Erste Mitteilung
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdf/10.1002/andp.19263840404

The first 1926 paper replaced a quantization prescription with an eigenvalue problem and applied it to the nonrelativistic unperturbed hydrogen atom.
The first communication is not the later time-dependent equation or proof of equivalence to matrix mechanics. DOI ending 40602 is the fourth communication.
