LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models, version 1
Research notes, accessed 2026-11-10

Version 1 submitted 17 June 2021 lists seven authors. It freezes pretrained weights while training low-rank adapter matrices, applying the method to Transformer models and citing the 2017 Transformer paper. The version-1 PDF https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.09685v1 was also inspected; affiliation is not evidence of funding.
