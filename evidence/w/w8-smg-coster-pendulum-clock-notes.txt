Early pendulum clock by Salomon Coster, c.1657
Research notes, accessed 2026-11-09

The catalogue identifies object 1980-108 as a Coster clock made at The Hague in 1657–1659, and reports Huygens made a successful pendulum clock in December 1656. It describes adaptation of spring-driven verge clockwork and Huygens giving Coster sole rights the following year.
