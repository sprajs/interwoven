Radar apparatus used by R. Watson Watt in 1935

Object 1957-90/1, description
The museum describes a receiver previously used for NPL ionospheric research and the 26 February 1935 aircraft reflection demonstration using a BBC transmitter. Its reported distance is not proof of range measurement.
