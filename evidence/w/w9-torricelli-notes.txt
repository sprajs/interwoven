Letter to Michelangelo Ricci concerning the barometer
Source: https://webserver.lemoyne.edu/giunta/torr.html
Locator: Letter dated Florence, June 11, 1644; paragraphs on air and mercury
Torricelli explained a mercury column through the weight of surrounding air and considered an instrument for atmospheric changes.
The letter witnesses work already underway, not an invention date. He reported difficulty isolating atmospheric variations from temperature effects. This English translation derives from a later edition.
