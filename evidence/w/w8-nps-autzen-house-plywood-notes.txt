The National Register nomination states that a rotary veneer plant began operating at Portland Manufacturing in early 1902, and that the firm experimented with panels of rotary-cut Douglas-fir veneers in 1904 before their 1905 exposition display. Its history cites Thomas Autzen’s 1953 company account. This supports a rotary-cut veneer prerequisite without proving that the 1905 exposition introduced plywood globally.
