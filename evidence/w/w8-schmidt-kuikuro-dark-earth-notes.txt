Source: Intentional creation of carbon-rich dark earth soils in the Amazon
URL: https://www.alice.cnptia.embrapa.br/alice/bitstream/doc/1156858/1/Intentional-creation-of-carbon-rich-dark-earth-soils-2023.pdf
Accessed: 2026-11-09

The researchers, including Kuikuro authors, describe intentional soil enrichment observed in a present-day Kuikuro village and compare it with ancient dark-earth sites. Similarities are interpreted as evidence for past management, but do not directly date the beginning of the modern community practice or prove all ancient soils had the same origin.
