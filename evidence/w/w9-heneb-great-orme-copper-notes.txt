Great Orme Bronze Age copper mines, GAT1694
https://archwilio.org.uk/her/chi3/report/page.php?watprn=GAT1694
Accessed 2026-11-09

The heritage record describes copper-ore workings at Great Orme and charcoal from a passage with a reported 1410–1070 cal BC range, plus stone and bone tools nearby. The date is a sample proxy and later disturbances occur elsewhere at the mine; exact mine origin and continuous use are not established.
