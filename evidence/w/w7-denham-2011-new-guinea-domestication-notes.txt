The review describes a regionally complex history of banana domestication and cultivation inferred from phytoliths and features at Kuk. It discusses evidence suggestive of westward dispersal from the New Guinea region, while noting the lack of direct archaeobotanical verification for parts of the proposed route; no direct link from the individual Kuk assemblage is established.
