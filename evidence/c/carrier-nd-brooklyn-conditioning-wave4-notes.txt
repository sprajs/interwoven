Title: Invention of Air Conditioning
URL: https://www.carrier.com/us/en/history/invention-of-air-conditioning/
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passage: Early air conditioning questions

Company history describes Carrier’s 1902 design at Sackett & Wilhelms, for humidity problems in multicolor printing. It reports a 55% humidity target and well water in 1902, supplemented by ammonia compressor in 1903. Its priority and performance claims are retrospective.
