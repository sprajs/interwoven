Research notes

pp. 44–45 (PDF p. 32), testing of residues from the Wanggou urn burials
The team examined fifteen samples from W12 and W20 together. Carbonised fabric in W12 showed two structures, a coarser plain weave and a finer crossed-warp weave tentatively described as leno. The ELISA result identifies the latter as silk. The report does not provide the calibrated date of W12.
