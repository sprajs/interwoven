Attributed research note, not a reproduction. Donald D. Chamberlin and colleagues, “A History and Evaluation of System R,” Communications of the ACM 24(10), October 1981, printed pp. 632–635 and 638–641, https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs245/win2020/readings/system-r-history-evaluation.pdf . The authors describe Phase Zero (1974–75), the multiuser optimizer built 1976–77 with weighted CPU/I/O cost and SQL joins, and experimental actual use in 1978–79 at the San Jose laboratory and other user sites. These were experimental, not supported commercial installations. The article does not individually date each partner's first inventory query.
