America’s last Linotype newspaper

The Saguache Crescent newspaper in Saguache, Colorado, is a family run newspaper, from the 1800s, and also the last American newspaper to be produced using a Linotype typesetting machine.

Since the purchase of a brand new Linotype machine back in 1921, the family have been printing it’s local weekly news the same way ever since. Owner and editor, Dean Coombs, has passionately continued the tradition and shows no signs of stopping any time soon.
