The museum says the collected writings include a postface written around 1234 that records printing 28 copies of Sangjeong gogeum yemun using movable metal type. The museum attributes this practice to Goryeo. We did not inspect the original Korean text or underlying historical witness. The date is that attributed to the postface, not a separately dated copy of the printed edition.
