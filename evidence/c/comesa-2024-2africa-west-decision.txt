Overview of the 2Africa West Cable System, paragraphs 18–20
The Commission describes SDM1 supplied by Alcatel Submarine Networks and the design ceiling of 16 fibre pairs. The decision describes the project design, not its final route-by-route commissioning. Its underlying transaction description draws in part on submissions by the parties.
