Thin-Film Composite Reverse-Osmosis Membranes: Origin, Development, and Recent Advances
https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/bk-1981-0153.ch021
Inspected 2026-11-17. Selected passages paraphrased.

Abstract — The inventors describe the Loeb–Sourirajan membrane as a thin selective surface integrally supported by a thicker porous layer. They discuss separately forming the selective and supporting layers as an approach to improving that design, recount work at North Star and FilmTec, and name NS-300 and FT-30 among the resulting composite membranes. Only the publisher-indexed abstract was inspected.
