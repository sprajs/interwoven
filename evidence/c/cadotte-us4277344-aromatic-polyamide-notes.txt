Interfacially synthesized reverse osmosis membrane
https://patents.google.com/patent/US4277344A/en
Inspected 2026-11-17. Selected passages paraphrased.

Patent face, abstract and claim 1 — US 4,277,344 was filed 22 February 1979 and issued 7 July 1981. It describes aromatic polyamide reverse-osmosis membranes synthesized by interfacial polymerization, with the thin separating layer on a porous support in the composite design.
