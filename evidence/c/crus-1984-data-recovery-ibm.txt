Attributed partial research note, not a reproduction. R. A. Crus, “Data recovery in IBM Database 2,” IBM Systems Journal 23(2), 1984, printed pp. 178, 182–183, https://www.bitsavers.org/pdf/ibm/IBM_Systems_Journal/232/ibmsj2302H.pdf. The primary article describes DB2 write-ahead logging, page identifiers, undo/redo and compensation log records before ARIES. Earlier DB2 therefore already used compensation records; ARIES is not asserted to invent logging or the general record type.
