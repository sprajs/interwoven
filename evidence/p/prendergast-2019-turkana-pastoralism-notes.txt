Passages read: Introduction and archaeological context
The review of archaeological evidence describes herding and fishing near Lake Turkana at the beginning of the Pastoral Neolithic around 5000 BP.
Caveat: The date is a broad regional approximation rather than a dated first adoption event; genetic, archaeological and cultural pathways of herding transmission are incompletely resolved.
