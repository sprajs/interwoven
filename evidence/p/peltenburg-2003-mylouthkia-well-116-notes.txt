Chapter 11, §11.1, pp. 83–84, table 11.1 and fig. 11.3
Two samples from Well 116, fill 124 (OxA-7460 and AA-33129), and one from fill 123 (AA-33128) are short-lived cereal remains. The author summarises the dates as c. 8600–8200 cal BCE. This dates material in the fill and provides an approximate latest bound for the digging. The shell dates are excluded from the chronological argument. The official ASOR-hosted 2001 report gives earlier calibration of the same three seed samples.
