Abdominal Imaging with Contrast-enhanced Photon-counting CT: First Human Experience
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4820083/
Source version: Radiology 279(1), pp. 239–245, online 3 February 2016. DOI 10.1148/radiol.2016152601.
Inspected: 2026-11-17

Primary NIH Clinical Center study, coauthored by an engineer from Siemens. Reports the exact enrollment period and the research use of the hybrid detector architecture.

Abstract, Materials and Methods; Materials and Methods, Subjects
Fifteen volunteers were prospectively enrolled from 2 September to 13 November 2015. The study obtained both PCD and EID images.

Materials and Methods, Imaging System
The scanner combines one energy-integrating detector and one photon-counting detector on a dual-source system.

Submission history
The authors submitted the results on 24 November 2015. This is a conservative upper bound on the demonstration, separate from the recruitment date.
