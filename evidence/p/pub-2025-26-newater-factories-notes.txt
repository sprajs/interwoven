p. 2, report period; p. 13, Development of Changi NEWater Factory 3 — Report covers April 2025 through March 2026. PUB identifies four existing NEWater factories at Kranji, Ulu Pandan and Changi, distinguishes the decommissioned Bedok plant, and reports that the replacement Changi 3 construction contract was awarded at the end of 2025. It schedules completion for 2028 and identifies membrane bioreactor filtrate and reverse osmosis with flow reversal as intended design features.
