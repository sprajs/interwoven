Passages read: Archaeobotanical evidence and Fig. 3
The authors date pertinent feature fills to 6950–6440 cal BP; rare phytoliths match M. acuminata and occur in an agricultural context.
Caveat: Cultivation is inferred from associated features and diagnostic plant remains, not a direct observation of planting; the chronology and species identifications remain subject to archaeological interpretation.
