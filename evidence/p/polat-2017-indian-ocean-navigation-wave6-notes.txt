Title: Kamal, an instrument of celestial navigation in the Indian Ocean, as described by Ottoman mariners Piri Reis and Seydi Ali Reis
URL: https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/download/article-file/659838
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passage: Indian navigation and Kitabü’l-Muhit section

Scholar interprets Seydi Ali Reis’s 1554 text as describing an instrument used by Indian navigators to measure stellar altitude. Terminology, origin and structure are disputed; publication day and individual voyage are not established.
