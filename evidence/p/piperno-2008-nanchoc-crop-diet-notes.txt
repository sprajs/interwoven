Research notes (paraphrase, not a copy)
Source: Starch grains on human teeth reveal early broad crop diet in northern Peru
URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2604935/
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passages read: Abstract, site context and discussion
The authors place a diverse food-production system by approximately 8000 radiocarbon years BP, approximately 8600 calibrated years BP.
Caveat: The authors infer the wider farming system from multiple classes of evidence; starch in teeth is evidence of consumption, not by itself proof of local cultivation or independent domestication of every plant.
