Title: A Picture of New-York in 1846: Hudson River Steam-boats
URL: https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/public/gdcmassbookdig/pictureofnewyork01rugg/pictureofnewyork01rugg.pdf
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passage: pp. 104–105, extracts dated September and October 1807

The guide reproduces a September 1807 advertisement for the North River steamboat listing a seven-dollar Paule’s Hook–Albany fare, and an October 2 report of a departure with ninety passengers; these are reported offer and passenger counts.
