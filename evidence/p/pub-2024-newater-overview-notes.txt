NEWater’s Journey and NEWater Usage; page last updated 2 October 2024 — PUB confirms that the Bedok NEWater Factory and visitor centre closed on 31 July 2024. NEWater is primarily used in industrial and cooling applications. In dry periods it is also blended into reservoirs, from which water is treated at waterworks before distribution.
