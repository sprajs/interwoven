Title page and §5.2, p. 30 — UCLA Water Resources Program final report from July 1981. The preceding high-flux, salt-rejecting cellulose acetate membrane development by Loeb and Sourirajan and improvements by Manjikian and colleagues led to the Coalinga pilot test for supplementing drinking water. The pilot used tubular cellulose acetate membranes.
