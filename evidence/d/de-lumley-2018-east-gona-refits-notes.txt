Primary refitting analysis of the existing East Gona 10 assemblage. Some excavators and material overlap with the earlier Gona studies. Source-gravel selection discussion uses earlier geological research, while refitting provides a new object-level observation.

Introduction and site stratigraphy; Conjoining and refitting lithic sets from EG 10
The artifacts overlie an intermediate cobble conglomerate regarded as a probable raw material source. East Gona 10 includes trachyte and rhyolite cobbles with relatively few visible crystals. Physical joins among flakes include seven knapping sets, one of 14 pieces, which the authors interpret as evidence of sequential working of individual cobbles.
