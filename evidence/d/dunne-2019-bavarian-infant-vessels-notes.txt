Source: Milk of ruminants in ceramic baby bottles from prehistoric child graves
URL: https://research-information.bris.ac.uk/files/219986105/Accepted_Ruminant_animal_milk_in_ceramic_baby_bottles_from_European_prehistoric_child_graves_1_.pdf
Accessed: 2026-11-09
Passages read: Abstract, archaeological contexts, results
Three small vessels from child graves in Bronze and Iron Age Bavaria yielded residues consistent with ruminant dairy.
Caveat: The oldest sample is from a cremation and may contain contaminants; the evidence is not direct observation of feeding or population-wide practice.
