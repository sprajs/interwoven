Dahmen and Ochsendorf 2012, printed p. 450, describe northern Syrian unbaked-earth domed dwellings as used since the 1700s and describe a continuous helicoidal spiral masonry construction without centering. Their account does not separately establish when that precise spiral procedure began. These earth, mortar and foundation structures compare with the snow-block material and winter travel shelter; no transfer or chronological priority between the spiral methods is established.
