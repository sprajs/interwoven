Attributed research note. DARPA, “ARPANET,” https://www.darpa.mil/news/features/arpanet, “DARPA Solution” section, inspected 6 November 2026. The agency’s retrospective prose says the original ARPANET was formally decommissioned in 1990. An accompanying agency infographic gives 1989 as the network’s end, so the page is internally inconsistent about the earlier closure date. The prose does not assert that later Internet services or all host use ended.
