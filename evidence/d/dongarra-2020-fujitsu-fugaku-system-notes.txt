Report on the Fujitsu Fugaku System

Innovative Computing Laboratory technical report ICL-UT-20-06, 22 June 2020
Opened 2026-11-17. Paraphrased reading notes.

Pp. 7–8, Tofu Interconnect Network and node/link comparison
Dongarra identifies original Tofu and Tofu2 as prior interconnects on K and Fujitsu PRIMEHPC FX100. He compares these with the deployed Fugaku design, including a move from a separate network controller in K to CPU-chip integration, and the TofuD change from four to two signal lanes per link relative to Tofu2. His technical comparison closely tracks Fujitsu’s 2018 publication, so the report is not treated as independent corroboration of design inheritance.

Pp. 1–2, Overview and Systems History
The report locates Fugaku at RIKEN in Kobe and describes it as the Japanese post-K project, with the RIKEN/Fujitsu collaboration and HPC infrastructure context. It reports first-quarter 2020 access during installation and does not specify the work users performed.
