High-gain erbium-doped traveling-wave fiber amplifier

Optics Letters 12(11), pp. 888–890, author affiliations, History and abstract
Bell Labs scientists reported 22 dB gain on a 1.53 μm optical signal in erbium-doped single-mode fibre at room temperature. The catalogue gives submission on 18 June, acceptance on 12 August and publication on 1 November. The experiment had already been performed by publication.
