Research notes

pp. 72–75, especially pp. 72–74 and Figs. 1–2 (same PDF pages)
The complete eyed bone needle came from Main Chamber lithological horizon 11.2. Microscopy found polish and other use traces; the authors infer use of a fine thread from the eye, but no thread survived with this artefact. They report a lower-limit radiocarbon result for the middle of Main Chamber layer 11, separate from the dated bone in the East Chamber.
