Pp. 42–43: the report describes a fiber link connecting a Dorset police computer to video terminals after lightning damage, and says information is transferred to supervising inspectors. It separately reports that a six-station telephone system aboard USS Little Rock was in its third year of operation; it names LEDs, photodetectors, fiber bundles and pulse-position modulation. A separate photograph caption on p. 43 discusses Corning Corguide, specified at up to 20 dB/km; it does not identify the Little Rock cable. Installation year and individual calls are not specified in the inspected passage. A year bound prior to 1976 is inferred from the ordinal duration, not directly dated.
