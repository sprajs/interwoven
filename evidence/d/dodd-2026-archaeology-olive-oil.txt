The section on pre-Roman presses describes a fourth-century BCE olive oil production facility at Sant’Antonio Ferrandina with a lever and mobile weights and stone beds. This is an earlier, spatially separate press architecture used for a different crop, not evidence of technical descent, comparative yield or a shared woven constrictor.
