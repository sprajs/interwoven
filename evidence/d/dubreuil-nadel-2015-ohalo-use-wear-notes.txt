Source: The development of plant food processing in the Levant: insights from use-wear analysis of Early Epipalaeolithic ground stone tools
URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4614720/
Accessed: 2026-11-09

Use-wear and residue analysis of the Ohalo II slab support grinding or pounding non-oily plant materials. The authors consider the evidence for intensive or routine flour production weak and interpret flour production as possibly sporadic.
