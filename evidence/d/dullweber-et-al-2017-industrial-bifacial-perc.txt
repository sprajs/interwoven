pp. 46–47, Introduction and Figure 1; p. 54, reference [1]
The paper cites Blakers et al. 1989 for the parent PERC design. Bifacial PERC+ retains the processing sequence of industrial PERC but uses a patterned aluminum rear grid that exposes rear area to light. The rear contacts remain local on the passivated silicon surface.

pp. 50–51, Commercial bifacial PERC+ modules and Table 2
The authors identify LONGi among the suppliers of commercial glass–glass bifacial PERC+ modules, listing its LR6-60PD as an example.
