p. 1891, Fig. 5 and p. 1892, Table 1 CA-09-71: "Cotton boll in house floor". The published 2σ interval is 6278–5948 cal BP. The rendered article text has different lab labels and uncertainties in the prose and Table 1, with agreement on the site and calibrated interval.
pp. 1890–1891: Peanut remains occurred in both Las Pircas and Tierra Blanca contexts, and additional quinoa-like chenopods were recovered from later sites. The authors interpret Tierra Blanca as a transition to farming with managed irrigation and a wider crop range.
p. 1892, Table 1: QSN-1 Canal 3 (Beta-154127), 6279–5936 cal BP; QSN-1 Canal 2 (Beta-182966), 5038–4833 cal BP. The lab codes and conventional ages are shared with Dillehay et al. 2005. The 2007 table lists both under Tierra Blanca and gives different calibration ranges from the 2005 report.
