P. 221 (PDF p. 1): Dubois examined 66 case manuscripts from September 1816 to July 1819 in lot b, folder I of the Fonds du Musée Laennec, Nantes. Interns wrote the notes, often citing Laennec and sometimes incorporating his handwritten corrections. The seventh case, Nicolas Chaudron, is dated December 1817 and is the first record of mediate auscultation within this manuscript sample.
P. 222 (PDF p. 2): Dubois describes repeated examinations, recording signs over time and comparing them with autopsy findings. He gives a case dated 1818 in the series to illustrate this practice.
