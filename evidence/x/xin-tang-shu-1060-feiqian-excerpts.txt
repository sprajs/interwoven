Northern Song official history of the Tang. The narrative was compiled after the transactions it describes and can share earlier records with Tang huiyao.

Juan 54, Food and Finance IV, paragraph beginning 時商賈至京師
委錢諸道進奏院及諸軍、諸使富家，以輕裝趨四方，合券乃取之
Merchants coming to the capital deposited cash with circuit liaison offices and other public or private intermediaries. They travelled with light baggage and drew their cash by matching tallies. The punctuation of the intermediary list varies between transcriptions.

Juan 54, Food and Finance IV, paragraph beginning 自京師禁飛錢
請許商人於戶部、度支、鹽鐵三司飛錢，每千錢增給百錢，然商人無至者。復許與商人敵貫而易之
The fiscal officials proposed flying cash through the Revenue, Expenditure, and Salt and Iron offices after the capital ban. The history says that no merchants came under the initial offer of an additional hundred per thousand. It then records permission to exchange at par.
