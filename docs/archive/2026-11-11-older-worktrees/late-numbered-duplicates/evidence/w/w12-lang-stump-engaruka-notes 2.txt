The researchers interpret check-dam construction and captured alluvial sediments at Engaruka, together with evidence for variable irrigation. They associate the wider landscape with the 15th–18th centuries; no exact construction date of each wall is established, and reasons for abandonment and climatic resilience remain uncertain.
