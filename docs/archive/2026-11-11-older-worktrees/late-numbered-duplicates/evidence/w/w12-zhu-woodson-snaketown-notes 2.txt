Source: Reconstructing Ancient Hohokam Irrigation Systems in the Middle Gila River Valley, Arizona, United States of America
URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6182576/
Accessed: 2026-11-10

The authors reconstruct Snaketown-area canal expansion using Woodson’s period chronology, describing a canal in 450–650 CE and enlargement during 750–850 CE. Hydraulic outcomes are model estimates based on assumptions, not observed ancient flows; coordination arrangements remain uncertain.
