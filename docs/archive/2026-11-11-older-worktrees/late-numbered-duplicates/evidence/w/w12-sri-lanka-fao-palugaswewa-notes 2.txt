The 2017 proposal describes Palugaswewa as a branched network with downstream reuse, maintained by two farmer organizations with departmental supervision. It identifies four tanks under the Palugaswewa group. This is a contemporary witness and does not date the system’s origin or the modern organizations to antiquity.
