# Case selection and coverage decisions

Initial selection recorded 5 November 2026, before integrated synthesis.

## Selection logic

The programme seeks contrasting mechanisms, not a list of celebrated inventions. The initial table below is retained as a dated planning record; the completed expansion and review-driven additions follow it. The first eight assignments deliberately span material transformations, ancient public systems, knowledge transmission, industrial production, agriculture/households, health, transport and digital systems. Researchers choose bounded cases for evidential quality, chronological/regional contrast, and potential to challenge simple explanations.

This design over-samples cases with inspectable scholarship and explicit debates. It also intentionally over-samples negative and delayed outcomes. Neither success rates nor the relative frequency of mechanisms can be estimated. Multiple episodes may share sources, settings or interventions; they are not independent observations. Counts of cases, claims, URLs and agents describe work completed, not evidential strength.

## Candidate explanations fixed before synthesis

These are questions, not conclusions:
- Does whole-use cost explain choices better than acquisition price, and when does liquidity dominate either?
- Does reliable service depend on named, observable complements and maintenance arrangements?
- Do regional contrasts implicate factor prices, ecology, institutions or task differences?
- Does the chooser capture gains while operators, workers or non-users bear costs?
- Do standards and distribution matter independently of technical performance?
- Do trust, norms, learned practices and bodily fit explain differences after material constraints are considered?
- Can old, hybrid or less technically capable systems remain useful or regain use?
- Can deployment increase without improved welfare, inclusion or sustained use?

## Initial first-wave choices (historical planning record)

| Area | Initial coverage | Why retained / next test |
|---|---|---|
| Prehistoric material systems | Ceramics and metallurgy across several regions | Challenges universal stage ladders; preservation and dating remain difficult |
| Ancient engineering | Roman water/machinery, qanats, China, South Asia, Inka transport | Distinguishes artifact feasibility, operating systems and maintenance |
| Medieval/early modern networks | Paper/printing and Indian Ocean navigation | Tests language, market, institution and diffusion narratives |
| Industrial production | Steam/water, textiles, electrification, Britain/India/China/Japan | Rich rival causal literature; Britain is not the default causal template |
| Agriculture and household labour | Andes/Bali/Gambia/Ghana/India plus modern programmes | Explicit distribution, ecology, risk and recurring-use tests |
| Health/public goods | Inoculation, vaccination, water, antisepsis, penicillin, malaria, ORT, prescribing | Separates efficacy, uptake, mandate, coverage and social effects |
| Mobility | Roman/Inka roads, India/Ghana rail, electric vehicles, containers | Large-system coordination and distribution; bicycles/aviation still thin |
| Digital | Web, computers/phones, India education access, Kenya payments, VR | User examples receive bounded, dated comparators; adoption is not one metric |
| Military/state procurement | Present indirectly, no dedicated treatment yet | Requires direct treatment of coercion, strategic demand and technical standardisation |
| Precision, repair and skill | Complements in several strands, few focal cases | Needs cases where tacit skill and maintenance are observed directly |
| Oceania and Indigenous agency | Not yet adequately covered | Navigation, adaptation and local expertise should not appear only as deficits |
| Finance, administration and measurement | Mostly contextual | Candidate focal cases: credit/accounting, standard time, administrative infrastructure |
| Psychology, identity and accessibility | Often hypothesised; limited identification | Needs direct studies and historically variable behaviour rather than universal needs |
| Modern energy/semiconductors | Little direct coverage yet | Important omissions; choose based on additional discriminating evidence, not topical fashion |

## Inclusion and exclusion transparency

An attractive story is insufficient for inclusion as a strong claim. Famous 'firsts', suppression myths and technological lock-in anecdotes require source checks. If only an abstract is accessible, the supported claim must remain at abstract scope. If no meaningful denominator exists, qualitative uptake is labelled qualitative. A case can remain because it exposes uncertainty; it need not yield a tidy explanation.

A future edition could use systematic search protocols for a narrow mechanism or domain, multilingual archival work, or a deliberately matched sample. This broad edition instead documents its purposive choices and uses independent criticism to alter them.

## Completed expansion and inference changes

| Added strand | What is actually included | Why it changes the comparison | Residual limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| ENE: energy/manufacturing | PV, reactors, planar/DRAM silicon, lithium-ion manufacture, Chinese e-bikes | Separates production from domestic adoption; model/input sensitivity and cross-generation limits challenge a single learning story | Failed entrants, finance/cancellation and human contribution are incompletely observed |
| POL: state/administration | Tokugawa guns, Yuan money, Qing telegraph, French metrication | Adds permissions preserving niches, transaction alternatives, redistributed weather risk and rules/use gaps | Some institutional accounts are abstract-only; military procurement as a whole is not covered |
| OCE: Oceania/Indigenous systems | Voyaging, Tae Rak, Martu, Kuk, Māori kūmara | Adds agency, governance, disabling of operating conditions, contemporary recovery and unresolved interruption | These communities do not represent all Oceania; archaeology and experiments do not supply adoption denominators |
| SKL: precision/repair | Chronometer copying, seasonal clocks, armory gauges, automotive/appliance repair, TEA lasers | Failed contacts/rough components constrain tacit-knowledge and precision-only stories; tools and ownership constrain entry | Small historical samples; selected repair testimony lacks reliability/safety and livelihood panels |
| PSY: behaviour/accessibility | Fertilizer timing, seaweed learning, Malawi communication, hearing aids, distinct Bihar/Zambia bicycles | Identifies some packages while retaining realistic latent-mechanism rivals; actual access and continued use differ | Disability representation is not established by the bicycle evidence; Zambia abstract-versus-pooled-table interpretation remains unresolved; final tables uninspected |
| CTX: targeted adverse textile case | Post-1949 Guanzhong household production | Adds cash/ration and unpaid-work pathways beside earlier hybrid inputs; no backward projection | Same Eyferth lineage as IND-C07; retrospective local evidence, not national uptake |
| GHA: targeted actor/labour supplement | Cocoa roads/haulage, northern public-road recruitment, farmer feeder roads | Adds African initiative with unequal entry and contested compulsion; maintenance can respond to use | Connected source lineage; contextual transport series not reconciled or spliced |

These additions were chosen to challenge specific omissions and inference, not to fill a numerical quota. OCE, SKL, PSY, ENE and POL were commissioned after synthesis1 exposed gaps. CTX and GHA were commissioned after independent scope/power review named credible missing pathways. Neither reviewer acceptance nor structural completeness is inferred from an author's self-check.

## Current coverage matrix

| Dimension | Covered with inspectable cases | Still weak or missing |
|---|---|---|
| Chronology | Early ceramics; ancient/medieval materials and systems; early-modern networks; industrial change; twentieth-century public/manufacturing systems; recent dated use/interventions | No continuous history of every domain; prehistoric subsistence technologies and many intermediate craft lineages |
| Geography | Europe, East/South/Southeast/West Asia, several African regions, Americas, Australia/Pacific | Most countries and communities; Arctic/circumpolar systems; original-language and archival breadth |
| Outcome contrasts | Invention versus deployment, mixed/persistent use, niche/failure/reversal, legal versus practical uptake, production versus local demand | A representative denominator of failures or normal low-friction adoption; comparable diffusion rates |
| Technical constraints | Energy/materials, tolerances, instruments, repair, process/yield and supply | Long-term reliability/safety testing; complete engineering reconstruction of all systems |
| Economic constraints | Prices, risk, credit, scale, contracts, tariffs, alternative tasks | Comparable cost series and welfare accounts; failed enterprise/individual transition panels |
| Human/institutional | Skill transfer, incentives, attention/interpretation, identity, body/service fit, standards, land/authority, coercion, colonial relations and governance | Universal psychological effects; direct ordinary-user voices in many older settings; representative disabled/nonparticipant outcomes |
| Labour/value capture | Incumbent/cohort contrast, bargaining, gendered entry, unpaid work, assets/tools and debt | Verified present-day human advantage, earnings, accessible careers and a general displacement remedy |
| Graph/history | Scoped contribution/transmission/necessity records with provenance; full dated navigation; explicit cross-case comparisons | A continuous globally connected dependency history. Missing links remain research questions rather than invented arrows |
