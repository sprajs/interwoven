# Technologies in use: a revised comparative account

Synthesis 2, 5 November 2026. This revision integrates the materials, ancient engineering, knowledge networks, industrial, agricultural, medical, transport, digital and labour investigations with energy/manufacturing, state administration, Oceania, precision/repair and behavioural/accessibility research. Two targeted additions—post-1949 Chinese household textiles and Ghanaian road enterprise/labour—test omissions identified by independent reviewers. The collection is purposive, unevenly observed and not a representative sample of technological history. The [coverage inventory](../data/COVERAGE_INVENTORY.md) gives the generated record counts; counts are not independent confirmations.

The main result is a set of **bounded causal distinctions and locally tested mechanisms**, not one general law of adoption. Some propositions reject a categorical mistake; some have an intervention or dated process supporting a local mechanism; others remain plausible accounts with serious surviving rivals. Those are different evidential achievements. [SYN-K10–K22](../data/CLAIM_LEDGER.md) preserve these distinctions and the underlying claim IDs; [SYN-X01–X08](../data/COMPARISONS.md) make comparison limits explicit.

## What changed after criticism

The first synthesis placed useful emphasis on tasks and operating arrangements, but its labels sometimes treated a source limit or counterexample as a positive causal test. This revision separates them. Hero’s description does not document productive load; that silence is not observed non-adoption. Chinese movable-type niches refute impossibility, yet also fit a serious script-sensitive cost model. They therefore do not rule out script-related cost as part of relative uptake. “The system matters” remains a research instruction, not an explanation selected by all these cases.

The new histories also change the candidate mechanisms. An older practice can persist because an industrial substitute is inaccessible and its time costs are shifted to someone else. State rules can preserve specialised use, disable an existing system, or redistribute exposure while improving an average. Teaching and repair can be essential work in a particular configuration without providing an accessible, well-paid occupation. Direct behavioural experiments often identify an intervention package more convincingly than the psychological motive attached to it. These are substantive limits on the initial account, not additional illustrations of a single thesis.

## 1. Separate the thing, the service and its consequences

A meaningful historical unit specifies a configuration, task, population, interval and contemporary alternatives. Invention, feasible operation, reliable manufacture, deployment, repeated use and displacement are distinct stages. Welfare is another outcome, distributed among people who may not choose the technology.

Hero’s steam sphere is a transmitted description; Barbegal is archaeological evidence of productive machinery with interventions over time. Neither provides an empire-wide adoption denominator. A household smartphone need not be controlled by a child at lesson time. Delivered stoves do not establish sustained use or exposure reduction. In the hearing-aid trial, measured hearing benefit, logged use, satisfaction and keep intention diverged. A legal metric deadline does not date universal household practice. These observations demand different measurements, rather than one success/failure label. [ANC-C01–C02](../contributions/ANC/report.md); [DIG-C04](../cases/DIG-C04.md); [AGR-C07](../cases/AGR-C07.md); [PSY-C04](../cases/PSY-C04.md); [POL-C04](../cases/POL-C04.md).

Production is also distinct from local adoption. Chinese PV manufacturing could expand while most output was exported. Completed reactor overnight-cost series omit financing and cancelled projects. A reconstruction’s working smelt is evidence about that experiment, not the rate, recipe or economics of ancient production. These are material limits on what an observed number can explain. [ENE-K03/K08](../contributions/ENE/report.md); [MAT-C04](../cases/MAT-C04.md).

The graph consequently preserves scoped local relations, including uncertain transmission and specific requirements. It does not yet establish a continuous dependency history across all eras. Chronology and comparisons provide additional routes through the collection, separately from causal arrows. Shared words such as “waterpower,” “precision” or “knowledge” do not make two historical configurations the same node.

## 2. Persistence can mean adaptation, exclusion or shifted work

Material properties are task-dependent. Resilient sheet, cutting edges, sound and appearance need not favour the same alloy. Printing methods likewise serve different runs and workflows; commercial manuscript copying can supply selections rather than defective identical copies. Steam can supplement water, electricity can be generated from steam, and machine-spun yarn can support hand weaving. These observations reject a universal replacement ladder. They do not establish that each surviving technique was optimal or freely chosen. [MAT-C02/C05/C06](../contributions/MAT/report.md); [NET-C02–C03](../contributions/NET/report.md); [IND-C03/C07/C08](../contributions/IND/report.md).

The stronger challenge is post-1949 Guanzhong. Eyferth’s retrospective village investigation records households selling cloth coupons while continuing home production because purchasing factory cloth required scarce cash. Women supplied unpaid textile work alongside collective agricultural obligations. Durability and social uses also mattered, and some women reported wider social gains. The result supports access and labour-allocation pathways without identifying their causal shares or reducing everyone to a passive victim. It does not extend those later institutions backward into the earlier hybrid-yarn episode. Both episodes use different sections of the same article, so they are not independent replications. [CTX-C01; CTX-K02–K08](../contributions/CTX/report.md).

Lead-acid e-bikes add another constrained comparison: the contemporary lithium alternative had higher upfront battery cost, while policy changed the available transport choices. The continued older component does not show that users were indifferent to weight, life or pollution. Tanaka’s elaborate seasonal clock, reported as one completed object without a buyer, conversely shows why superior demonstration cannot establish a scalable product. [ENE-C05](../cases/ENE-C05.md); [SKL-C02](../cases/SKL-C02.md).

A better test asks what happens when people can obtain equivalent services from the substitute and operators can refuse the older work. Without that contrast, observed coexistence cannot distinguish task fit, scarcity, obligation and exclusion.

## 3. Costs and constraints belong to particular actors

Acquisition price omits recurring inputs, finance, time, uncertainty, repair and coordination. Those categories are a way to keep accounts, not a scalar whose invisible weights can be inferred from adoption. A purchaser’s cheaper service can consume an operator’s unpaid time or impose costs downstream.

The Ghana insurance experiment provides a comparatively strong local intervention: insurance-only offers increased farm investment, while cash-only aggregate responses were smaller under the tested contracts. Cash still affected some inputs; packages were not equal-sized changes to abstract constraints. Risk, expected future liquidity, basis risk and contract experience remain relevant. The result cannot rank insurance above cash across places or policies. Kenya’s fertilizer trials similarly distinguish a profitable small-dose configuration from the full recommended package; higher yield alone did not determine returns. [AGR-C06](../cases/AGR-C06.md); [PSY-C01](../cases/PSY-C01.md).

In Jahaly-Pacharr, changed water control and household income coexisted with altered rights to rice and demands on women’s labour. Refusal was constrained not only by household authority but by project enforcement. Carney’s 1990 episode reports low-yield arrears, plot seizures and reallocation, with conditional recovery of rights the following season. This is a dated sanction process, not evidence that every household was permanently evicted. Technical and input problems also prevent a labour-conflict-only explanation. [AGR-K07–K09/K25](../contributions/AGR/report.md).

Ghana’s transport history adds African initiative and unequal access. Chiefs and other actors helped finance roads; owner-drivers and hire-purchase supplied routes into haulage; farmers requested and built feeder connections. Land, debt, compulsory work and uneven alternatives shaped these activities. Northern public-road histories distinguish paid compulsion, changes towards hired recruitment, residual obligations and locally initiated unpaid work. Payment alone does not prove freedom to decline, and unpaid work alone does not prove coercion. The competing archival interpretations are preserved. [GHA-C01–C03](../contributions/GHA/report.md).

These additions change the adoption question from “Was there demand?” to “Whose demand could become a funded decision, who supplied the work, and what alternatives could each person refuse?” They do not establish one effect size for institutions, colonialism or labour prices.

## 4. Institutions change uses, alternatives and distribution

Institutions do not have one signed effect. Specific permissions can maintain gun use in peacetime Tokugawa niches. Yuan legal monetary unification can coexist with different transaction practices. French metric law can retain a base system while accommodating familiar subdivisions, and implementation can lag legislation. A religious commission can support printing without proving unrestricted permission everywhere. These are particular arrangements, not properties of an undifferentiated state, religion or culture. [POL-C01/C02/C04](../contributions/POL/report.md); [NET-C05](../cases/NET-C05.md).

Tae Rak offers an adverse comparison: drainage completed despite recorded opposition curtailed the water conditions for Gunditjmara trapping. Later restoration used a negotiated water setting that could reactivate many complexes but excluded Muldoons at that level. Neither cessation nor restoration can be described adequately as an individual efficiency choice. Martu burning likewise operates with customary rights and responsibilities; describing local foraging benefits without governance would omit a documented part of the arrangement. [OCE-C02–C03](../contributions/OCE/report.md).

A network can improve one measure while distributing risk differently. The Qing telegraph study estimates lower rice-price maxima following office access, alongside greater sensitivity to weather in connected regions and heterogeneous changes in volatility. Its access design does not establish that everyone could afford telegrams or that all households gained. Similarly, the Chinese prescribing audit gives a direct response to expected dispensing location, with patient scripts probing additional explanations; it does not supply a national prevalence estimate or a complete system-reform counterfactual. [POL-C03](../cases/POL-C03.md); [MED-C08](../cases/MED-C08.md).

## 5. Learning, precision and maintenance need specific tests

Knowledge is carried through people, records, tools, material supply and testing. K1 was a named copy of a sea watch, not demonstrated inexpensive serial manufacture. Armory gauges required maintained references; an interchangeability claim for one rifle cannot be transferred to every musket. Japanese clockmakers adapted mechanisms to seasonal time rather than simply waiting for equal hours. Tanzanian garages used apprenticeship, personal tools, manuals, modules and improvisation in different combinations. [SKL-C01–C05](../contributions/SKL/report.md).

The TEA-laser evidence is especially useful because it includes unsuccessful contacts and successful rough components. Early copies were associated with contact with successful builders, but Collins did not claim written-only replication impossible. Changing power units, materials and build variables leave an exclusively tacit-knowledge account underdetermined. The successful rough-electrode examples challenge strict precision as a prerequisite for every workable configuration; they do not show that precision is unhelpful or that the blocked laboratory could succeed with the same substitution. Hōkūleʻa’s revival adds apprenticeship, institutional support, expressed cultural purposes, volunteer sacrifices and a severe operational failure; a successful voyage does not itself measure a Pacific-wide revival effect. [SKL-C06](../cases/SKL-C06.md); [OCE-C01](../cases/OCE-C01.md).

Manufacturing cost histories also resist a single “learning curve” explanation. PV decompositions assign materially different shares to plant size. Battery attributions depend on what the model calls R&D or learning. DRAM experience does not transfer reliably across every product generation. Procurement can support an early niche and common specifications, but scale, redesign, equipment imports, skilled transfer and demand selection must be distinguished. A model decomposition is not an independent intervention on each contribution. [ENE-C01–C04](../contributions/ENE/report.md).

Maintenance remains important without becoming a residual explanation for every failure. Odisha’s stove intervention included instruction and local support, yet use and condition changed; those mediators were not separately randomized. HAPIN achieved supported use and reduced exposure without a statistically distinguishable birth-weight benefit, limiting the claim that every clinical null reflects poor use. Malaria recurrence requires separate timing of provision, ecology and detected resistance. A Ghanaian feeder road deteriorating after exports stopped is compatible with demand changing maintenance, rather than maintenance failure causing all demand loss. [AGR-C07](../cases/AGR-C07.md); [MED-C06](../cases/MED-C06.md); [GHA-K10](../contributions/GHA/report.md).

Adequate provision must be defined before observing an outcome. Otherwise “the missing complement” becomes whatever would have made the case succeed. The [causal framework](CAUSAL_FRAMEWORK.md) separately defines an outcome interaction, a necessary input, a supported package and an adoption decision; none automatically establishes the others.

## 6. Human behaviour is evidence to investigate

People’s expressed purposes can include comfort, identity, care, autonomy and cultural obligation. Those statements matter without becoming fixed universal preferences. A testable account names a prior belief or constraint and an observation that distinguishes it from a competing explanation.

The behavioural investigations repeatedly limit a strong psychological reading. Early fertilizer offers changed use, but did not uniquely identify present bias or produce persistent uptake after the offer ended. Seaweed summaries supplied interpretation as well as attention. The Malawi experiments show that peer effort, communication and uptake can respond to rewards and contact structure; some unrewarded arms also produced communication and knowledge gains. Measured gendered credibility differences did not establish a precise pooled adoption penalty. Hearing-aid benefit, stigma, service fit and keeping the device differ, while the trial’s price comparison was sequential rather than randomized. Bihar and Zambia bicycle studies identify distinct packages and outcomes; a nominal-fee arm being significant where a free arm is not does not establish a difference between them or psychological ownership. [PSY-C01–C05](../contributions/PSY/report.md).

These results do not make beliefs or social relations irrelevant. They narrow the inference to what was measured and changed. A promising next study separates attention from computational help, contact from credible demonstration, or future liquidity from commitment. Relabelling every null as habit or distrust would make the account impossible to disprove.

## Surviving rivals and the observations that could separate them

| Question | What the current evidence supports | Realistic rival still alive | Discriminating next evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Why limited movable-type uptake? | Task-specific niches; no categorical script impossibility | Script-sensitive fixed/search costs can predict those same niches | Independently reconstructed run sizes, costs and choices across comparable projects |
| Why continued household textiles? | Hybrid inputs in an earlier period; later cash/access and unpaid-work pathways | Durability, social obligations and unequal household alternatives may coexist | Equivalent-service choices after access changes, including operators’ refusal and control of proceeds |
| Why a stove health null? | Package outcomes, changing use and distinct exposure/clinical links | Design, outside exposure, task fit, physiology and demand feedback | Predefined adequacy plus separately measured or varied mediators and endpoints |
| Why intervention uptake changes? | Local offer/summary/incentive/package effects | Liquidity, computation, credibility and access can mimic a named psychological mechanism | Direct arm contrasts with mechanism-specific predictions and pre-outcome measurements |
| Why production cost falls? | Bounded process histories and model decompositions | Output selection, redesign, input prices and attribution rules | Failed entrants/cancelled projects, independent process measures and sensitivity across definitions |
| Does new work compensate displacement? | Some incumbent losses, later-cohort resilience, bargaining and concrete entry barriers | Demand conditions and selection, unequal access and unpaid work | Linked trajectories, net returns, ownership and credible alternatives for excluded people |

These are research designs, not findings promised in advance. More heterogeneous cases alone would not identify the missing causal shares.

## The digital examples and future human roles

Pocket internet is a plausible part of smartphone usefulness, but the acquired surveys do not isolate it from software, tariffs, distribution, price, network reach or ergonomics. UK device importance varied by age and US households often combined devices. The assertion that most laptops cost less than most smartphones remains unestablished without a date, market, transaction-price population and contract treatment. Ofcom’s UK 2023 adult gaming question reports minority VR-headset use; it is neither global ownership nor retention, industrial usefulness or a current verdict. The prolonged-work experiment concerns a specified setup. [DIG-C02/C03/C06; DIG-K24](../contributions/DIG/report.md).

Historical work in repair, teaching, coordination and adaptation motivates investigation of future roles. It does not establish that those tasks will remain human, scarce, accessible or well paid. Telephone automation illustrates the difference: adverse linked-incumbent outcomes coexisted with no measured overall employment reduction for subsequent young female cohorts in the study population. Maintenance jobs were heavily gendered; some incumbent moves do not establish general access. Container bargaining could protect particular workers while leaving others outside the arrangement. [LBR-C01](../cases/LBR-C01.md); [MOB-C06](../cases/MOB-C06.md).

The [future-role analysis](FUTURE_ROLES.md) therefore separates **contribution, entry and value capture** and applies adverse cases to each candidate. No contemporary opportunity is validated by this edition. Its strongest prospective result is a better evidence requirement: identify the payer, alternative, assets/skills/rights needed, distribution of unpaid time, feasible refusal, and a scenario under which the role disappears.

## Scope of the conclusion

This account explains some local decisions and rules out several broad substitutions of evidence for inference. It cannot estimate the frequency or relative importance of mechanisms across history. English-accessible scholarship, surviving elite/administrative records, celebrated controversies and research-intensive regions shape the selection. Archaeological survival, retrospective interviews, administrative categories, observational estimates and experiments have different uncertainties. Source editions and connected datasets are recorded rather than counted as independent votes.

The [chronological/global map](GLOBAL_MAP.md) and [selection register](SELECTION_REGISTER.md) make the achieved coverage inspectable. The [uncertainty register](UNCERTAINTY_AND_NEXT_RESEARCH.md) names unresolved claims, languages, populations and designs. The stopping boundary is a coherent, independently criticised edition with explicit residual tests—not exhaustion of all technology or all history.
