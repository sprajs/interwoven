# Rejected, qualified and still-open hypotheses

Revised synthesis 2. “Rejected” below means the stated strong version fails the inspected evidence; it does not imply every weaker or local version is false. Most cases are purposively selected, so their frequency cannot estimate how often a mechanism operates.

| Hypothesis | Disposition and decisive distinction | Evidence route | What remains open |
|---|---|---|---|
| A described principle proves operational deployment | Reject the inference: source description and observed deployment are different evidence; silence alone is not observed failure | ANC-C01 Hero; MED-C05 penicillin | Which specific barriers prevented a particular deployment |
| A harder/newer material universally replaces an older one | Reject universal ordering: task-dependent properties and coexistence | MAT-C02/C05; MAT-K15 | Relative prices, supply and performance for particular objects |
| Agriculture is a universal prerequisite for pottery | Reject: securely contextualised forager pottery | MAT-C01 | Exact origins, transmission and prevalence |
| Roman slavery made productive machinery impossible | Reject absolute incompatibility: productive mills and enslaved hydraulic upkeep | ANC-K07 | Marginal effects of labour prices or institutions on particular investment decisions |
| Ancient steam was a nearly completed industrial revolution | Unsupported: no documented productive load in the inspected passage and no identified scalable pathway | ANC-C01 | Capabilities of historical reconstructions and lost applications |
| One cause—high wages, cheap coal or colonialism—settles industrialisation | Not established by this corpus; competing mechanisms and units remain | IND-C01–C08 | Relative contributions under specific counterfactuals |
| Chinese script makes movable type impossible or fixes one method for every task | Reject the categorical rule: documented type niches | NET-C02; NET-K08 | Script-sensitive cost models can explain niches and low relative uptake; their causal contribution remains unresolved |
| Ottoman rulers' blanket printing ban explains a civilisation's delay | Not established by inspected documentary trail; specific permissions and earlier printing matter | NET-C05 | Local restrictions, costs, customers, enforcement and omitted records |
| More output per hectare demonstrates a superior farming system | Reject denominator substitution: labour, rotation, water and control differ | AGR-C01/C03/C04 | Full social and intertemporal accounting |
| Poor farmers do not invest chiefly because they lack cash | Qualified: insurance-only offers increased investment without cash grants; cash-only responses were smaller under the tested contracts | AGR-C06 | Generalisation, basis risk, unsubsidised demand and welfare |
| A cheap stove that works in the laboratory will yield sustained health gains | Reject sufficiency: delivery, use, exposure and health are separate links | AGR-C07 | Which constraints dominate each setting and endpoint |
| Appliance purchase mechanically reduces a fixed amount of housework | Reject fixed-task assumption: output, standards, exertion and division of labour change | AGR-C08 | Device-specific causal effects and unpaid-work distribution |
| Laws or mandates establish population adoption | Reject measure substitution: obligation differs from reach, provision and compliance | MED-C02 | Enforcement and recipients' experiences |
| Doctors prescribe antibiotics because patients demand them | Qualified by direct incentive contrast in one Chinese city | MED-C08 | Other populations, actual outcomes and system-wide financing responses |
| Successful disease suppression remains secure without recurrent provision | Reject sufficiency; ecological and operational reversals require continuing measurement | MED-C06 | Relative causes of particular resurgences |
| Lower transport cost or higher trade means everyone benefits | Reject welfare inference: district output, public finance, land and worker outcomes differ | MOB-C03/C06 | Complete distribution and counterfactual accounting |
| A failed enterprise proves its underlying technology had no viable use | Reject: early electric service failures coexist with later specialised fleets | MOB-C05 | Alternative financing/coordination counterfactuals |
| Technology displacement erases all of its spatial effects | Reject: Ghanaian railway towns persisted after rail decline, with later road access | MOB-C04 | Relative roles of assets, coordination and omitted geography |
| Smartphones displaced computers because pocket internet outweighed everything | Consistent with some task evidence but causal ordering unestablished; substantial coexistence | DIG-C02/C03 | Matched task experiments and separated attribute effects |
| Most laptops cost less than most smartphones | Unestablished, not disproved: population/date/weighting/contract definition missing | DIG-K24 | A defined, dated transaction-price distribution |
| VR is a global adoption failure | Overbroad: bounded UK2023 gaming minority-use evidence; specialised use and retention not settled | DIG-C06 | Current global tasks, active use, retention and comparative value |
| Device ownership equals meaningful access | Reject: shared control, service interruption and task fit matter | DIG-C03/C04/C05 | How interventions change actual sustained capability |
| New work automatically benefits displaced individuals | Reject: incumbent/cohort outcomes and bargaining differ | LBR-C01; MOB-C06 | Access to entry, transition costs and value capture |
| Trust, status or habit can explain residual uptake without direct evidence | Methodologically inadequate: requires beliefs/behaviour independent of observed adoption | AGR-C06; DIG-C03; NET-C05 | PSY experiments and interviews provide direct evidence, while latent motives remain partly unidentified |


| Older practice persists because users freely prefer its performance | Not established: later Guanzhong cloth access and unpaid work supply another pathway; useful durability/social functions can coexist | CTX-C01 versus separately dated IND-C07 | Equivalent-service choices when substitutes are affordable and operators can refuse |
| More cumulative output is a general cause of lower cost | Not established: cost-model/input sensitivity, mixed nuclear trajectories and limited cross-generation DRAM spillovers | ENE-C01–C04 | Failed projects, selection, process changes and comparable causal contrasts |
| Institutions have one sign for technical uptake or welfare | Reject common-sign inference: licensed niches, partial restoration, rules versus use and redistributed network risk | POL-C01–C04; OCE-C02/C03 | Specific enforcement, alternative arrangements and complete distribution |
| Expert contact guarantees successful replication | Reject universal sufficiency: some visits were followed by failed attempts; this does not establish contact necessity or rule out written-only success | SKL-K16 | Materials, instrumentation, attempt selection and independent reliability tests |
| Strict electrode precision is necessary for every workable TEA configuration | Reject that universal requirement: reported rough profiles worked in other configurations; the blocked laboratory's configuration-specific requirement remains unresolved | SKL-K17 | Hold the rest of the configuration fixed while varying verified geometry; no claim that precision is useless or sufficient |
| A behavioural intervention identifies its named psychological mechanism | Qualified: early offers/summaries/incentives/packages change multiple channels; direct arm comparisons and prior measures matter | PSY-C01–C05 | Present bias versus liquidity; attention versus interpretation; contact versus credibility |
| A significant paid arm and nonsignificant free arm prove ownership motivation | Reject statistical inference: difference between arms must itself be tested | PSY-K19 | Adequately powered mechanism-specific contrasts |
| Payment proves voluntary work; unpaid work proves compulsion | Reject classification shortcut: recruitment, sanctions, purpose and refusal matter | GHA-C02/C03 | Road-level payroll, actual worker choice and competing archival interpretations |
| Indigenous practice is either untouched continuity or lost knowledge | Not established by these cases: teaching, governance, mixed economies, interrupted use and contemporary recovery differ | OCE-C01–C05 | Local/community-led evidence and specific chronological transmission pathways |

All case routes resolve through the [case library](../cases/README.md) and [claim ledger](../data/CLAIM_LEDGER.md). The central methodological rejection is **sufficiency without scope**. It does not make price, performance, beliefs, institutions or human needs irrelevant; it demands a defined mechanism and an observation that could show it wrong.
