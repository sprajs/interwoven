# Dated case routes

Overlapping editorial routes, not precise invention dates or causal links. A case can appear in several routes; read its period and episode interval. Archaeology, reconstructions, publication dates and operating episodes are not interchangeable.

All 88 cases have at least one route. Route membership is editorial navigation, not an additional historical claim.
