# PSY-C02 — Learning which seaweed input to observe in Nusa Penida

**Period:** 2006–2008; main field experiment 2007–2008. **Region:** Indonesia; Nusa Penida, Bali. **Domain:** aquaculture; learning; attention.

Farmers’ independently elicited knowledge differs by input dimension. A trial-plus-explanation package changes practices, but limited attention is not cleanly separated from computation, credibility or learning delay.

[Read the substantive research dossier](../contributions/PSY/report.md).

## Stages

**feasibility:** Existing seaweed farming; trials vary pod size and, in one arm, spacing.

**deployment:** 117 farmers participate in compensated on-plot experiments followed by personalised summaries.

**diffusion:** Only short-run within-sample input changes measured; no population spread estimate.

**non-adoption/niche:** Trial participation before summaries produced little detectable change; spacing practices largely persisted.

## Episode

**technology configuration:** Existing line-grown seaweed with pod size and spacing as distinct adjustable inputs; 65 sorting trials and 52 weight trials.

**task:** Choose productive planting inputs on a farmer’s own plot.

**population:** 489 baseline respondents from a 2006 census of about 2,706 farmers in seven villages/24 hamlets; 117 randomly assigned trials; 232-person follow-up frame with 231 then 221 located.

**interval:** Baseline June–December 2007; trials July 2007–March 2008; follow-ups April–May and July–August 2008, with summaries May–June.

**contemporary alternatives:** Continue customary cutting/spacing; Learn by observing raw trial measurements; Use personalised summary and discussion

**adoption measure:** Enumerator-measured pod weights/distances and self-reported changed techniques. Table IV contains 684 person-wave observations, not 684 independent farmers.

**observation limits:** Short follow-up and selection of active existing growers; paper’s projected income gains are not sustained realised income measurements.

## Actors

**chooser:** Farm operator chooses subsequent inputs; assignment governs offer of researcher-managed trial.

**payer:** Researchers provide trial inputs, guarantee a return floor and small time gift; farmer bears routine operating effort.

**operator maintainer:** Farmer and field team plant/weigh trial lines; subsequent cutting and cultivation remain farm tasks.

**affected nonchooser:** Household labour and buyers potentially affected; their outcomes not separately observed.

**ability to refuse:** All approached trial farmers participated; voluntary context with compensation. Refusal by unpaid helpers or household members not measured.

## Labour

**tasks:** Cutting, attaching, sorting, measuring and tending seaweed; summary interpretation adds a knowledge task.

**entry barriers:** Existing farming plots/lines and practical knowledge; no new occupational entrants studied.

**bargaining ownership:** Farm-level decisions observed; ownership and sale of output not tested as bargaining mechanisms.

**transition:** Two short follow-ups; no occupational migration or sustained skill-premium evidence.

**distribution:** Input-dimension and trial-type heterogeneity; no robust gender/wealth distribution of livelihood gains established.

**evidence limit:** Labour cost of precise cutting and time spent interpreting evidence were not isolated experimentally.

## Mechanisms and counterevidence

Candidate/supported mechanisms: selective attention hypothesis; interpretable feedback; computation assistance; experience-dependent beliefs

Summary arrives later for all treated farmers, with no separate randomisation of timing/content. Measured pod-size effect about 7g is imprecise; explanation can change perceived returns as well as attention.

## Evidence links

- [PSY-K04](../data/CLAIM_LEDGER.md#psy-k04): Among 489 surveyed Indonesian seaweed growers, 86% could not state their current pod size and 87% could not state an optimal size, whereas most could state spacing; these beliefs were elicited before uptake outcomes and alongside observed farm practices.
- [PSY-K05](../data/CLAIM_LEDGER.md#psy-k05): In the seaweed trial, input changes were more evident after personalised summaries than after trial participation alone; measured pod-size phase differences were significant, but the approximately 7g post-summary treatment effect itself was imprecise.
- [PSY-K06](../data/CLAIM_LEDGER.md#psy-k06): Limited attention is a plausible account of the seaweed input-specific pattern, but explanation also reduces computational demands and can alter credibility; the evidence does not isolate attention as the sole mechanism.
