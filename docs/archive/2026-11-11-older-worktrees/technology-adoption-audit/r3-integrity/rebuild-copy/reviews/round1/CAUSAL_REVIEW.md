# Round 1 independent adversarial causal review

Reviewer: independent causal strand. Date: 5 November 2026. Decision: **retain the bounded historical distinctions; request targeted revisions to the causal tests and labels.** No P1 finding is warranted within the explicitly provisional scope. Three P2 repairs and three P3 clarifications follow. This is not endorsement of a general predictive complements theory.

## Scope and independence

I reviewed only the immutable round-1 snapshot as the programme evidence: 59 cases, 210 claims, 129 source records, and its synthesis, framework, hypothesis register, future-role document, methods and decisive case/claim records. All 169 files listed in MANIFEST.json matched their SHA-256 values when checked. I did not read the other round-1 reviewers' reports. Planned energy, Oceania, administration, psychology and repair work contributes no evidence to this assessment.

The numbered range SYN-K10–K18 contains eight actual records; there is no SYN-K15. I assessed all eight. I independently inspected original research on Ghanaian insurance, Indian stoves, Chinese prescribing, Massachusetts water/sewerage and telephone automation, plus the omitted printing-cost model by Angeles and the Mali credit experiment. The final published stove paper and an AT&T archival working paper extend the snapshot's access. Versions, locators and limits are recorded in [CAUSAL_SOURCES.json](CAUSAL_SOURCES.json); actionable findings are in [CAUSAL_FINDINGS.json](CAUSAL_FINDINGS.json). No raw-data replication, original-language archival collation or representative literature search was performed.

## Central verdict

The defensible result is that the selected histories defeat several unrestricted equivalences: invention is not deployment; equipment delivery is not sustained beneficial use; coexistence is not evidence of optimality; aggregate employment is not the experience of displaced incumbents. The synthesis usually states these boundaries well. A genuinely universal proposition can be defeated by one appropriate counterexample, so purposive selection does **not** invalidate every negative conclusion.

The stronger proposition—system/task/actor-specific complements distinguish adoption from non-adoption—has not been established across the library. The categories can absorb almost any outcome unless inputs, task, actor, time and a competing prediction are fixed independently of the result. The local experiments do discriminate among some accounts. Their success does not confer that property on every historical comparison. Calling a constraint part of “the system” adds no explanatory content to a result already explained by prices, design, coercion, demand, selection or changing alternatives.

This is partly acknowledged at SYNTHESIS.md:9–13,97–99 and CAUSAL_FRAMEWORK.md:25,50,54,60–62. Those acknowledgements should be retained. The repairs concern places where a particular test, rejection or label still implies more discrimination than its evidence supplies. I would not demand an estimated universal adoption model or treat the absence of one as an error in this working edition.

## Evidence-strength distinctions

These tiers describe the **inferential route**, not the importance or truth of a proposition.

| Tier | What it can establish | What it cannot establish by itself |
|---|---|---|
| A: identified local intervention contrast | A randomized offer/script/package effect, or an observational causal estimate conditional on its design assumptions | The causal contribution of every bundled component; transportability to another population |
| B: independently supported mechanism or interaction | Measured physical trade-offs, an outcome interaction, or process evidence that discriminates against a specified rival | Universality, causal shares across the library, or human wage/entry effects |
| C: compatible historical/process evidence | Sequence, coexistence, documented arrangements and plausible pathways | That an observed mediator caused rather than followed demand or success |
| D: logical, measurement or source-entailment result | Invalid inference from one outcome to another; a well-observed counterexample to an absolute necessity | A positive explanation of uptake. Source silence is not automatically a counterexample |

It is possible to be highly confident in a Tier D distinction and uncertain about its associated causal explanation. The current synthesis records often attach one medium-confidence, mechanism-supported label to this mixture.

## Prioritized findings and repairs

### C01 — P2: The printing exception does not discriminate against the serious script-cost rival

**Locations:** analysis/HYPOTHESES_AND_NEGATIVE_CASES.md:13; data/claims.json:5142, NET-K08.inference_limits and test.strongest_rival/discriminating_observation; related SYN-K11 at data/claims.json:5727 and SYNTHESIS.md:27. NET-C02's narrower assertion that script does not determine every task choice is defensible.

The hypothesis register says Chinese characters alone explain limited *relative uptake*, then treats task-specific niches as a rejection. NET-K08 tests a different, much stronger rival: identical script predicts identical method choices across tasks. A cost explanation need not make that prediction. Angeles's model explicitly explains block predominance **and** movable-type genealogies and imperial encyclopedias through script-sensitive costs, pages and print runs. His manuscript uses observed output choices and uncertain historical calibration; it is a serious compatible rival, not a causal proof of script dominance. [Angeles, January 2016 author version, pp. 11–22, especially 18–21](https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/120520/13/120520.pdf).

**Why the existing caveat is insufficient:** admitting that character costs “can still” matter does not repair an allegedly discriminating observation that the rival itself predicts. The rejection of strict impossibility survives; the rejection of a relative-cost account does not.

**Replacement:** “Chinese script did not make movable type impossible. Its contribution to relative uptake remains unresolved: script-sensitive fixed and operating costs, print-run requirements, reusable blocks and patronage can jointly predict the observed niches.” Keep the rigid all-tasks determinism only as an explicitly narrow rejected claim, not the strongest scholarly rival.

**Acceptance:** update the register and NET-K08 together; include Angeles or an equally explicit rival; mark the comparison underdetermined unless independent workshop/project evidence differentiates predictions. Useful tests compare similar texts and print runs with measured composition, storage and reprint costs, or exploit changes in type costs within the same market. Do not count model calibration to the same historical niches as new independent corroboration.

### C02 — P2: Stage distinctions and unavailable evidence are being promoted into evaluated mechanism tests

**Locations:** data/claims.json:5686, SYN-K10.causal_status and test.discriminating_observation; analysis/HYPOTHESES_AND_NEGATIVE_CASES.md:7; SYN-X01.evidence_comparability; related SYN-K11/K12/K14 composite classifications. METHODS_ADDENDUM_V2.md:54 already supplies the right rule about absence and shared predictions.

SYN-K10's test asks for downstream use “unestablished or constrained.” Those are evidentially different. Hero's transmitted description can establish the limits of a source; it cannot reject actual ancient deployment without an argument that traces would survive and be found. The Indian stove trial does provide a directly observed downstream contrast, but it estimates the delivered programme. It does not turn Hero into an observed negative case or identify a shared missing-input mechanism.

The broader classification problem is that “these measurements are not equivalent,” “the proposed universal implication fails,” and “mechanism M caused outcome Y” are stored under the same evaluated/mechanism-supported presentation. The accurate prose caveat does not travel reliably when a record is filtered, summarized or used in the graph.

**Replacement:** split the bounded measurement implication from the empirical mechanism claim. For SYN-K10, record the former as descriptive/source-entailment; preserve randomized stove findings as their own intervention evidence; mark particular mediation claims underdetermined. Replace “unestablished or constrained” with “directly observed lack of the specified downstream outcome, or independently measured constraints; documentation gaps are only source limitations.” Do not mechanically downgrade genuinely causal clauses in SYN-K12 or SYN-K16.

**Acceptance:** each synthesis record makes clear which proposition was tested, against which rival and on which actual observations. No source silence counts as observed non-adoption. If the schema remains unchanged, put this distinction explicitly in the statement and test fields and choose causal_status for the actual asserted proposition rather than the strongest constituent source.

### C03 — P2: The operational complementarity test conflates joint inputs, interaction and necessity

**Locations:** analysis/CAUSAL_FRAMEWORK.md:43,54; application to SYN-K10–K14 and MED-K09. This is a repair to the framework, not a claim that every dependency edge is erroneous.

“A joint change produces a task benefit unavailable from either input alone” is not sufficient to distinguish complementarity from simple addition. If A adds two units and B adds three, the joint five-unit output is unavailable from either alone even though neither raises the other's marginal contribution. Conversely, one input can have a positive independent effect and still be complementary to another. Availability of a substitute for an input does not, by itself, refute an interaction in the specified configuration.

The framework correctly separates necessity, helpfulness, substitutes and co-occurrence in paragraph 54, but the table does not operationalize that distinction. It also needs to separate **complements in producing a benefit** from **a complement causing adoption**. An improved joint output does not establish why a particular chooser invested.

**Replacement:** “For a specified outcome, scale, actor and interval, an input is complementary when its causal marginal benefit is greater with the other input than without it. State separately any bounded necessity, threshold or substitutable route. A successful bundle alone cannot identify the interaction.” For two binary inputs, the relevant comparison is Y11 − Y10 − Y01 + Y00, with outcome direction and scale declared. Historical engineering can supply bounded process evidence when that contrast cannot be estimated; it must not be presented as the estimate.

**Acceptance:** name at least one fully worked example and one non-example. MED-K09 can illustrate an estimated health interaction; the stove package illustrates unidentified component contributions. For each central application distinguish the outcome of interest—trial, continued use, output, health or profit—and date the alleged inputs before that outcome. This repair can be completed without new experiments by narrowing the claim.

### C04 — P3: Make maintenance adequacy explicit before using the case to test an explanation

**Locations:** data/claims.json:5809, SYN-K13.causal_status and test.mechanism_chain; underlying AGR-K18 at data/claims.json:501; MED-K16 at data/claims.json:4037. SYNTHESIS.md:55–63 is more careful than the unqualified arrows in the record and should not be strengthened.

The final stove paper reports that the intervention already included instruction and local promoters, and later repair/rebuilding coincided with renewed ownership/use. These were not separately randomized interventions. Hence “support was present” cannot automatically mean sufficient support, but “support proved insufficient” cannot be inferred simply from an unwanted endpoint either. Poor task fit can reduce use and incentives to maintain; physical deterioration can also reduce use. Both arrows remain possible. [Hanna, Duflo and Greenstone, 2016, pp. 86–87,94–95](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/sites/default/files/research-paper/52%20Up%20in%20Smoke%20AEJ2016.pdf).

The malaria review explicitly does not validate each causal attribution. In Sri Lanka, a planned transition to consolidation, surveillance, population movement and later resistance require a dated account. Recurrence after reduced control is informative, but a later detection date for resistance is not itself proof of its biological onset. The snapshot already acknowledges much of this. [Cohen et al., 2012, “Weakening of the malaria control programme” and “Discussion”](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1475-2875-11-122).

**Why this is a clarification, not a P2 causal error:** AGR-K18 and SYN-K13 already mark mediation underdetermined, and a physical operating requirement can be supported without identifying its historical effect size. I do not treat that acknowledged limitation as a new defect. The next use of these cases as a test needs pre-outcome adequacy criteria; otherwise failure can always be assigned to a newly specified deficient complement. This is an operational research recommendation, not a claim that the reported stove or malaria results are wrong.

**Replacement:** “These cases document changing operating conditions alongside changing outcomes. They motivate maintenance mechanisms, but do not isolate a common maintenance effect. Stove offer effects are identified; the causal contributions of repair, task fit and use remain unresolved. Malaria chronology constrains some sole-cause stories without assigning causal shares.” Prefix unresolved arrows with “hypothesized.”

**Acceptance:** retain the existing underdetermination flags and clarify which arrows are proposed. Before claiming that this comparison identifies a maintenance contribution, supply a dated contrast that changes repair/provision while independently measuring demand, condition and service, or keep the conclusion at compatibility. Define adequacy before the outcome; include a case with the named provision but no endpoint improvement. Keep first detection distinct from onset in MED-K16. This does not block the current cautious historical account or require new trials.

### C05 — P3: “Insurance dominates cash” invites a constraint ranking the experiment does not supply

**Locations:** analysis/HYPOTHESES_AND_NEGATIVE_CASES.md:16; SYN-K14; AGR-K15. SYNTHESIS.md:35 and AGR-K15's limitations are appropriately narrower.

The Ghana paper itself says the cash and insurance treatments are not directly comparable in intensity. It also explains that future capital/saving constraints can make insurance valuable. Thus risk and liquidity are not always separable rival worlds. The insurance-only response is stronger evidence against an exclusively binding current-cash account than a policy ranking by treatment size. [Karlan et al., 2014, pp. 599–601,646](https://www.atai-research.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/Agricultural-Decisions-after-Relaxing-Credit-and-Risk-Constraints.pdf).

A useful omitted challenge is the Mali trial: cash grants increased investment in the no-loan sample, while returns differed for selected non-borrowers. It is not a refutation of Ghana; it shows why the sample and selection rule must be known before naming the binding constraint. Its gross-profit measure excludes some costs and should not become total welfare. [Beaman et al., 2023, pp. 1595–1598](https://poverty-action.org/sites/default/files/2025-03/Selection%20into%20Credit%20Markets.pdf).

**Replacement:** “Insurance-only offers increased investment despite no cash grant; cash-only responses were smaller in this sample. The comparison supports a risk channel under the tested contracts without ranking risk and liquidity constraints across settings.” **Acceptance:** remove “dominates,” retain the package and state-contingency qualifications, and place Mali in the challenge queue rather than counting it as a matched replication.

### C06 — P3: “Employment recovery” slips from a cohort comparison to an implied transition

**Location:** analysis/FUTURE_ROLES.md:9; related SYNTHESIS.md:89 and LBR-K02/K03.

The telephone study separates linked incumbents from successive cohorts, principally young white American-born women in small US cities. Stable later-cohort employment is not evidence that displaced individuals recovered, nor does it necessarily trace a cohort through an initial employment fall and recovery. The synthesis uses “resilience”; the future-role text should do the same. [Feigenbaum and Gross, 2024, pp. 1880–1883,1923–1934](https://www.dpgross.com/papers/answering-call-automation.pdf).

**Replacement:** “Adverse outcomes for linked incumbent operators coexisted with no measured reduction in overall employment for subsequent young female cohorts in the study population.” **Acceptance:** replace “recovery,” preserve population/observational qualifications near the example, and keep labour-demand outcomes separate from personal transitions.

## Chronology, rivals and future roles: what survived the attack

The prescribing example is one of the best demonstrations of discrimination in this collection. The programme correctly states the intervention as changing the expected purchase location, not as a nationwide elimination of financial incentives. Rival signals and service-quality trade-offs remain in the original paper; the synthesis does not claim a health or resistance effect. [Currie, Lin and Meng, 2014, study design and discussion](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4776334/).

The AT&T archival working paper offers a useful next historical comparison: organizational adaptation and later diffusion across market sizes are distinct margins, while scale and automation can reinforce each other. It prevents interpreting every late cutover as simply missing organization. Its author-linked PDF is a **July 2021 working-paper version**, not the 2024 final article; its archive and adoption data overlap the labour study. [Feigenbaum and Gross, 2021, pp. 1–3,12–13,23–25](https://jamesfeigenbaum.github.io/research/pdf/OFIRA_20210730.pdf).

The framework's prohibition on recovering preferences from uptake alone is sound. The stove authors' behavioural interpretations, insurance payout associations and digital device preferences should not be upgraded into independently identified trust, habit or status effects. This review found no new basis for that upgrade. Similarly, different actors and coercive arrangements genuinely prevent reading use as universal consent; they do not establish one signed effect of “institutions.”

FUTURE_ROLES.md is appropriately conditional. Its activities are research candidates, not a demonstrated shortlist of future human comparative advantage. The document explicitly addresses automation, commoditization, value capture and entry barriers. Apart from C06, I find **no future-role forecast leap requiring withdrawal**. A future study must compare a human contribution with feasible standardized, automated and organizational alternatives; historical necessity alone cannot answer that comparison. No new occupation forecasts are warranted here.

## Re-review gate and remaining research

For this working edition, acceptance requires closing C01–C03 by **correcting or narrowing claims**, not by accumulating more favourable examples. C04 is a non-blocking research clarification; C05–C06 are small text repairs. Preserve the source- and case-specific limitations already doing useful work.

If the programme later wants a stronger explanatory complements account, preregister a small challenge set before inspecting outcomes: one dated interaction contrast, one configuration with the named inputs present but persistent non-use, one successful price/quality change without the proposed extra intervention, and a reversal where changing demand may precede reduced upkeep. Fix the population, outcome, input adequacy and predictions; permit “underdetermined.” Select on opportunity to observe the exposure and alternatives rather than on a memorable adoption story. This would test the framework's usefulness without pretending the purposive library estimates mechanism prevalence.

**Blocker/repair summary:** no P1 defect found in the stated provisional historical scope; three P2 inferential repairs are needed before describing the cross-case tests as discriminating causal support. The strongest empirical anchors survive. The central framework survives as an evidence-organizing discipline and a source of bounded hypotheses, not as an established general explanation of adoption.
