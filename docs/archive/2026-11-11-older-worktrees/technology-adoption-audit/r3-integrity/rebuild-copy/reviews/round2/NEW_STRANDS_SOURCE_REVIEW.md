## Required revisions

### NSSR-01 — Calibrate the Martu test against the actual rival

**Target:** OCE-K11, principally `test.status`, `test.evidence_sought_found` and the description of the discriminating observation. **Severity:** moderate; bounded inference calibration, not a false observed result.

The frozen claim names nonrandom environment and settlement/track selection as its strongest rival, but marks the test `evaluated`. The source defines ignition regimes largely through distance to communities and tracks, assigns fires using their centroids, and obtains rainfall from one station. It does not supply a matched environmental intervention or demonstrate that the named selection account cannot produce the observed differences. Its separately significant and nonsignificant rainfall coefficients are not, by themselves, a test that the two slopes differ. The observed pattern is useful evidence, but discrimination against that realistic rival remains underdetermined.

There is also a precise validation boundary: the reported 90% check concerns **estimated burn age at 50 points within 500 m of a road or track**. It does not establish 90% accuracy in classifying human versus lightning ignition. The frozen evidence field mentions the percentage without specifying what was validated. [Bliege Bird et al. 2012, Results and Materials and Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3387077/).

**Repair:** use `underdetermined` for the mechanism test against the named environmental-selection rival; specify that the 90% check validates burn-age mapping in the accessible sample; retain the observed size, clustering and rainfall results. If keeping an evaluated test, it must be separated as a narrower test of a prespecified climate-only account, with the realistic selection rival still unresolved. Existing OCE-E10/E12 already preserve contribution scope, proxy/confounding limits and alternatives; they need no new necessity or global causal interpretation.

### NSSR-02 — Separate US market examples from the worldwide PV outcome

**Target:** ENE-K01 and its linked episode/measurement description where needed. **Severity:** low; population and measurement precision.

The claim begins with a US population label, then joins US buyer, concentration and standardization examples to the early decline in module prices. Nemet explicitly states that the modeled price series is worldwide and combines two world surveys. The US market examples are legitimate, but they do not turn that outcome into a US-only cost series. The source's §§3.1.1–3.1.4 support the changes in products and markets; §§1.2–2.1 establish the outcome's geography. [Nemet 2006, printed pp. 3221–3222 and 3224–3225](https://wikis.mit.edu/confluence/download/attachments/21139745/Nemet_BeyondtheLearningCurve_EP_06.pdf?api=v2).

**Repair:** identify the worldwide PV cost/price reconstruction and distinguish the partly US market evidence used to interpret it. For example: “Nemet's worldwide PV price reconstruction, interpreted alongside US market evidence, documents a 1970s shift from space to terrestrial applications with changing quality requirements, standardization and market structure; falling module prices alone do not measure factory learning.” Retain the current `underdetermined` test and the warning that the residual is not a causal allocation.

## Checks that constrain the interpretation

**PV and batteries.** The PV percentages are correctly matched to their intervals and are percentages of modeled module-cost change, not electricity prices or adoption rates. The two reconstructions also change input definitions: the 1980 plant-size input is 125 kW/year in Nemet versus 1 MW/year in Kavlak. Describing the comparison as sensitivity to both models and reconstructed inputs would be more precise than suggesting the cost equation alone explains the difference. The shared historical inputs and semiconductor account are already disclosed. [Nemet, Table 2](https://wikis.mit.edu/confluence/download/attachments/21139745/Nemet_BeyondtheLearningCurve_EP_06.pdf?api=v2), [Kavlak et al., Tables 1 and 3 and §4.2](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/56afb881e321406be6160228/t/5bff10822b6a2884d7267f17/1543442566890/Evaluating%2Bthe%2Bcauses%2Bof%2Bcost%2Breduction%2Bin%2Bphotovoltaic%2Bmodules.pdf).

For batteries, the inspected sensitivity exercise reallocates active-material prices and hardware costs. It is not an unrestricted reassignment of all overlapping routine and experimental process work. ENE-K15's underdetermined status correctly preserves that ambiguity. The 2% allocation cannot be converted into a share of workers' contribution, and the 54% allocation is not an identified public-funding effect. [Ziegler et al., pp. 6088–6090, Figures 7–9](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlepdf/2021/ee/d1ee01313k).

**Tae Rak and Martu governance.** The drainage mechanism has a concrete intermediate observation: water receded rapidly, and fixed traps worked mainly during floods. Recorded opposition makes voluntary replacement by a preferred fishing technique an inadequate sole explanation. It does not determine all changes in fishing labor or catches. The nomination cites Gunditjmara history, and its restoration account cites the community project's history; these are overlapping lines of evidence. OCE-K20 accurately retains rights and responsibilities alongside immediate hunting benefits, without inventing an enforcement rate or universal motivation. [Nomination, printed pp. 68–69](https://whc.unesco.org/document/166283), [Gunditj Mirring project account](https://www.gunditjmirring.com/lake-condah-restoration-project), [Martu study, Materials and Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3387077/).

**Qing telegraphs.** The original table contains 47,436 prefecture-month observations within a 130-prefecture, 420-month frame; it is not a complete 54,600-cell panel. Dividing the high-quality rice coefficients by the Table 1 mean reproduces 4.24%, 5.24% and 5.50%. The outcome is a reported monthly prefectural wholesale maximum. The source examines pretrends, covariates, sample exclusions and network/weather predictions, but relies on nonrandom office placement and assumed within-year dates. The frozen record appropriately preserves these conditions and does not infer welfare or household consumption. Its historical narrative cites Yoon; the price dataset is additional evidence, not independent confirmation of Yoon's financing mechanism. [Gao and Lei, pp. 10–21, Tables 1 and 3](https://assets.aeaweb.org/asset-server/files/13141.pdf), [Yoon, publisher abstract and source note 6](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/modern-asian-studies/article/abs/dashed-expectations-limitations-of-the-telegraphic-service-in-the-late-qing/62CACD9A5094EBEAFD5E0E760EB55C69).

**Yuan source correction, not a collection error.** An apparent discrepancy was investigated rather than promoted to a finding. The accepted manuscript incorrectly associates a doubling with 1260–1355; the publisher explicitly corrected that interval to 1346–1355 on 7 March 2024. The frozen claim does not repeat that sentence, and its three attributed rates are unchanged in the published version. The original commodity observations and CPI calculations were not independently reconstructed; the accessible replication landing page did not expose the data in this review. The final source ledger could add the known edition correction, but the selected claim need not be rejected for an error it did not reproduce. [Published paper, Figure 6 discussion and correction notice](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/ehr.13305).

## Acceptance boundary

Seven selected claims can be retained as scoped; two require the targeted revisions above. All eleven checked edge propositions remain supportable under their qualifications, including abstract-level limits for POL-E08/E09. The review does not establish raw-data validity, complete source genealogy, representative adoption denominators, worker-transition outcomes, or causal identification beyond the stated designs. It does not accept unreviewed claims by association.

The reviewed records are the frozen bytes identified above. Any subsequent synthesis or data export must either match those records, incorporate and explicitly verify the two revisions, or disclose further changes. No live contribution or snapshot file was edited by this reviewer.
