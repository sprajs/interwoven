# What remains uncertain, and which evidence would change the account

This register distinguishes unresolved historical questions, limits of the collection and proposed future research. Unknowns are retained deliberately. They do not all warrant another case: the useful next step is often to revisit a source, separate an outcome or obtain a different kind of observation. Case identifiers resolve through the [case library](../cases/README.md); each dossier's questions.md supplies fuller locators and acquisition leads.

## Consequential historical uncertainties

| Question | Current evidential boundary | Next discriminating observation |
|---|---|---|
| What caused local ancient systems to stop operating? | Barbegal cessation with water present weakens a water-loss-only account. Kuk has several plausible explanations for a local wetland interruption. Neither identifies a universal collapse mechanism. ANC-C02; OCE-C04. | Align repairs, settlement, demand and environmental changes before cessation; test whether their timing differs between still-active and interrupted sites. More artefacts of the same kind alone may not discriminate. |
| Which knowledge moved, and which was locally developed? | Objects, raw materials, primary production, taught practices and modern reconstruction are different evidence. West Mexican transfer remains abstract-supported; local Ile-Ife production does not prove isolation. MAT-C06/C07; OCE-C01/C05. | Read full object-level studies, trace securely dated intermediate practices and documented teaching, distinguish independent recipe variation from transfer. Avoid deciding an origin question from priority alone. |
| Did wages, energy prices, engineering or political conditions drive particular industrial choices? | British spinning-pay reconstructions challenge a general wage-surge premise but leave commercial cotton margins disputed. Coalfield and dear-coal Cornish steam are different episodes. IND-C02/C04–C06. | Comparable task-specific labour time, output quality, hourly costs, machine price/finance and fuel records; independently inspect measurement rejoinders. Observing low average daily pay cannot settle marginal machine profitability. |
| Why did women alter labour supplied to Gambian irrigation? | Control over returns, project enforcement, crop alternatives, machinery, fuel and marketing changed together. Early household gains and later conflict concern different observations. AGR-C03. | Follow the same people and plots through enforceable rights, credit sanctions, compensation and operating changes. Household income is not a measure of each member's control or welfare. |
| What made collective irrigation coordination effective? | Bali models expose water/pest trade-offs and sensitivity to assumptions; observed agreement does not identify a unique historical institutional cause. AGR-C02. | Independent out-of-sample seasonal observations, alternative governance and matched hydrological conditions; distinguish a model's optimal calendar from demonstrated historical optimality. |
| What explains device adoption rather than merely correlating with it? | Portability, tariffs, connectivity, software, price and bodily/task fit are bundled. Device coexistence is well observed in bounded surveys; one universally dominant attribute is not. DIG-C02–C04. | Designs that vary attributes or access separately, dated transaction-price distributions with weights, active-use and task-outcome measures. “Most laptops are cheaper” remains unestablished until its population is defined. |
| Does VR remain niche because of discomfort, task fit, cost or content? | The UK 2023 gaming measure and a small prolonged-work experiment answer different questions. Neither establishes current worldwide retention or general failure. DIG-C06. | Comparable tasks and alternatives; repeated use and abandonment by user group; isolate device fit, content, price and learning. Do not use a gaming percentage to estimate all professional or therapeutic use. |
| How do costs fall in manufacturing? | PV and cell-cost decompositions change with equations, periods and assignment rules; DRAM structural estimates lack directly observed plant costs. ENE-C01/C03/C04. | Reconstruct shared inputs and sensitivity analyses; observe yields, work and investment across successful and failed plants. Cost-account shares are not causal shares of policy, management or human contribution. |
| Why do nuclear construction trajectories differ? | Completed-reactor overnight costs exclude financing and cancelled projects; countries and cohorts differ in design, scale, regulation and imported knowledge. ENE-C02. | Build an ordered/cancelled/completed project denominator, then compare design and institutional changes within cohorts. Resolve source-table inconsistencies before quoting subgroup totals. |
| Who benefits when information networks expand? | Qing telegraph connections are associated with lower price maxima and redistributed weather exposure in a particular panel. Market effects are not household welfare; restricted popular access can coexist with market effects. POL-C03. | Replicate with modern staggered-treatment methods, office downtime and reporting-quality checks; follow net buyers/sellers, income and consumption. |
| Did an administrative rule produce use? | Firearm permissions, currency acceptance rules and metric deadlines establish institutions, not universal compliance. POL-C01/C02/C04. | Licences, transactions, calibrated instruments, sanctions and contemporaneous local records. Separate possession from use, units of account from settlement media, and familiar names from measured magnitudes. |
| When did access loss disable a technique? | Tae Rak documents opposed drainage and a negotiated partial restoration; Kuk does not identify exclusion as its unique explanation. OCE-C02/C04. | Control/permission histories combined with actual operating conditions and service outcomes. A physical constraint created by another actor must not be relabelled an intrinsic technical failure or voluntary rejection. |
| Does useful maintenance become a viable human livelihood? | Historical cases identify tasks more often than entry, wages or careers. Martu care/hunting and voyaging volunteering are not automatically paid occupations. OCE-C01/C03; LBR-C01/C02. | Person-level work, compensation, authority, unpaid obligations and transition records; distinguish social contribution from scarce paid demand. |


| Why did household textile work persist under socialist procurement? | CTX-C01 distinguishes local cash/ration access, unpaid labour and useful/social cloth properties; it shares an Eyferth source with the earlier IND episode. | Equivalent-service, household-cash and operator-refusal contrasts; original-language local records and testimony beyond the three villages. |
| Who made and maintained Ghanaian road alternatives? | GHA-C01–C03 retain African initiative, unequal asset/land access and disputed recruitment transitions. Austin/Heap transport context does not align transparently with the railway paper's summaries. | Reconcile definitions and original series before building any diffusion curve; match recruitment instructions, payrolls, sanctions and worker accounts by road/date. |
| Do the behavioural studies identify their proposed motives? | PSY reports local intervention effects while attention, computation, credibility, liquidity and self-report salience remain entangled. Positive aspirations wording appears in both the May manuscript abstract and final abstract, while pooled manuscript tables are imprecise. Final tables were not inspected; a changed published estimate is not inferred. | Acquire final tables and account for version changes; directly compare mechanism-specific arms, objective behaviour and prior beliefs. |
| Does Martu burning cause the observed regime difference? | OCE-K11 preserves the observed fire pattern but remains underdetermined against nonrandom environment/track selection; 90% validation concerns burn age, not ignition class. | A directly tested regime interaction and matched environmental comparison, with independent ignition validation and community-governed design. |
| What makes learning or repair successful? | SKL observes tools, references, contact, manuals and material failures in different configurations; partial Collins1975 footnotes do not establish the full replication history. | Complete original follow-up; all-attempt denominators; verified material/instrument changes, long-term performance and independent safety/repair outcomes. |

## Access and lineage limits

The [source bibliography](../data/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md) records what was acquired, the specific passage inspected and the underlying evidence lineage. Full-text availability does not mean every page was read. Abstracts can support bounded attributed findings; they cannot bear a detailed independent reconstruction of methods or causal estimates. A source cited by a later source is not counted as directly inspected merely because its argument was summarized.

The most consequential restricted-access areas include West Mexican metallurgical transmission, proposed British reservoirs, selected Tokugawa/Qing institutional interpretations and some original archival or proprietary datasets. Claims dependent on these remain qualified. Repeated URLs are not the only form of dependence: papers can share sites, investigators, censuses, engine reports, legal texts or price compilations while having different URLs. The programme does not report a numerical count of independent confirmations.

Archaeological and environmental proxies have their own missingness. Preserved ceramics are not all meals; excavated furnaces are not annual output; charcoal species do not by themselves reveal ownership or sustainable forestry; a ditch does not identify the political institution that built it. Contemporary surveys also omit people, tasks and periods. Explicit denominators improve interpretation without making the sample representative.

## What this collection cannot estimate

The selection is purposive, with unusually strong attention to contested, failed, delayed, coerced and hybrid cases. It cannot estimate a technology's probability of success, the prevalence of any mechanism, a common diffusion coefficient, or the proportion of all inventions lost. The relevant absence is often evidence not found in this investigation, rather than evidence that an event did not occur.

Geographic coverage is substantial but uneven. English-language accessible scholarship dominates. Quantitative US labour evidence is richer than individual transitions elsewhere. The Pacific cases do not represent all Oceania, and the African material does not represent all African industries. Ordinary maintenance, disabled users, unpaid care, women outside measured occupations, failed firms and nonparticipants remain less visible than successful projects and surviving records. Military systems outside the selected procurement/firearm cases, aviation, refrigeration, building technologies and many financial/administrative practices would require separate investigations.

Comparisons across centuries can expose an invalid universal proposition or suggest a useful question. They rarely identify the size of a shared causal effect. Even a genuine counterexample to a categorical claim does not establish a replacement theory. A more realistic rival often accommodates the counterexample: a printing-cost model can predict a niche for type without treating all scripts alike; an earnings model can distinguish average wages from the marginal cost a machine saves.

## Evidence that would warrant expanding this edition

Expansion should alter inference, not merely increase the case count. Priority goes to:

1. A credible rival that predicts the same observations used by a central claim, especially if the original test called those observations decisive.
2. A new source that changes the actor, denominator, chronology, configuration or evidence-access level of a consequential assertion.
3. A comparable failure or non-adoption episode where the proposed complement was demonstrably present, or successful use through a substitute that challenges necessity.
4. A transition study that follows affected individuals, with pay, entry and bargaining evidence, outside the heavily represented US cases.
5. A multilingual or community-led investigation able to change the questions, rather than merely supply another example to the existing framework.

The [future-role propositions](FUTURE_ROLES.md) require a separate empirical programme in actual contemporary settings before they can become opportunity claims. Their next evidence must include a payer, a measurable unresolved task, entry costs, value capture and a falsifier. Historical recurrence alone does not establish that a role will remain human or valuable.
