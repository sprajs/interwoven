## Inspected evidence and decision checkpoint

Full text availability does not imply entire book read.
- Mchome 2022 full article 26pp acquired, pp78,81–88,91–97 inspected. Original 2018/2020 interviews and news/archives; no household adoption census or engineering lifetime tests. Electrical appliance repair case will exclude electrical utility reconnection beyond brief contextual comparison, because legal/task scope differs. Do not adopt broad voltage incompatibility diagnosis as independently tested physics.
- Collins 1974 full article acquired, read pp171–183. Seven British labs in summer1971, snowball search checked by physics-department questionnaire, funding application and literature searches; later NA interviews. Written-only attempts failed among respondents; visits sometimes failed; p177 unnecessarily strict electrode tolerance vs rough successful alternatives. p176 n26 explicitly notices better instructions and pending attempt. Commercial purchase alternative described p173; competing priorities and selective disclosure pp180–82. Study purposive not universal tacit necessity.
