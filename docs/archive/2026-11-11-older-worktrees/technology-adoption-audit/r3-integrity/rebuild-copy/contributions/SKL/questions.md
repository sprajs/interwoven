# SKL questions, access limits and disconfirmation work

## Consequential unresolved tests

1. **K1 replication and service (SKL-C01, K01–K03).** Inspect Board of Longitude commissioning, delivery and rate records, then the original 1790 obituary scan. Separate completion from delivery to resolve the 1769/about 1771 chronology. Match watches by voyage exposure, care and service. Did later failure reflect a design constraint, handling or avoidable contamination? Obtain all-in making/servicing costs and actual merchant-fleet denominators before claiming that high price delayed diffusion. Named state voyages are selected demonstrations.
2. **Japanese clockmaking (SKL-C02, K04–K06).** Recover offers, workshop accounts and surviving Tanaka notebooks rather than repeat the unsold-clock anecdote as a cost calculation. Locate ordinary seasonal-clock buyers, maintainers and repair invoices. Test whether users who could obtain equal-hour clocks nevertheless retained seasonal arrangements for defined tasks. Follow ordinary makers across 1873 rather than infer their livelihood outcomes from Tanaka's exceptional career. Which exact files, jigs, cutting methods and quality checks produced the gears? Hashimoto explicitly says the tools are uncertain.
3. **Interchangeability (SKL-C03, K07–K09).** Acquire the 1829 musket test report cited via Schmidt and the Hall/Talcott archival originals. Identify model, component, production date, pass/fail counts and whether assembly required selective fitting. Examine gauge wear, reference checks, toolmaking expense and subsequent military repair costs. A Hall rifle and a Model 1816 musket are not a controlled experiment. Track workers' tasks/pay rather than read “ordinary workmen” as proof of complete deskilling.
4. **Automotive repair (SKL-C04, K10–K12).** Include unsuccessful trainees, inaccessible garages, failed repairs and vehicles permanently retired. Compare matching repairs by load, distance, parts, equipment, cost and failure time. Does owning tools open jobs before reputation grows, or mainly follow it? How are apprentices' domestic work, physical injury and occasional payments distributed? Which women and household contributors are excluded by a male-centred account of expertise?
5. **Electrical repair (SKL-C05, K13–K15).** Do not accept interviews as electrical safety validation or adopt broad claims about foreign voltage standards without measurement. Follow complete repair cohorts. Separate appliance-stock expansion from rising failure rates. Link utility job records to specific task/technology changes and post-exit earnings; fiscal cuts, prepaid metering and computerisation coincide. Does freelance work improve autonomy while reducing income security, and for whom?
6. **TEA transfer (SKL-C06, K16–K18).** Obtain the full 1975 follow-up and build logs. Complete documentation, checked components, equivalent prior expertise and a controlled contact opportunity would distinguish missing explicit details from learning-by-observation or motor practice. Seek subsequent independent builds, commercial purchase outcomes and published recipes that worked. Study other configurations before treating rough electrode success as a general engineering tolerance result. Collins selected an emerging field especially suitable for showing his thesis.

## Coverage boundaries

The six cases substantially cover Japan and Tanzania but cannot represent Asia or Africa. Missing useful comparisons include South Asian machine-tool repair, Chinese instrument workshops, Ottoman precision crafts, women repairers and apprentices who left no celebrated career. Optical grinding and lens manufacture remain a major omitted precision domain. The selection overrepresents unusually well-recorded devices and ethnographically visible workshops. It supplies mechanisms and falsifiers, not prevalence or worldwide chronology.

No forecasts, external publication, messages to researchers or paid procurement were undertaken. Drafts, acquisition records and source inspection notes remain in this contribution directory. Local structural validation can check IDs, references and contracts; it cannot establish historical truth.
