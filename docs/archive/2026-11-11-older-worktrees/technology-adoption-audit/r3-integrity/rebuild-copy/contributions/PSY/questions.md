# Questions, unresolved rivals and access limits

These are limits of this contribution, not implied requests for permission or forecasts. The current five-case handoff is complete within its bounded scope.

## Consequential tests still missing

1. **PSY-C01, present bias versus timed access.** A trial independently varying advance announcement, liquidity, delivery effort and commitment would discriminate better than uptake alone. Prospectively elicit intertemporal plans and beliefs before offering fertilizer, then observe purchases and actual applications. Early–late p=.08 is suggestive; an insignificant positive late effect is not zero. Dose-specific expected returns, household resource claims and weather risk must remain in the choice set.
2. **PSY-C02, attention versus computation or trust.** At one common follow-up date, randomise salience and arithmetic support separately while holding recommendation content constant. Measure recall, attention to physical input dimensions and subsequent practice. Current summaries arrive after trials for all treated farmers; farmers anticipated receiving them. A seven-gram estimate with weak direct precision cannot sustain a large general cognitive claim.
3. **PSY-C03, what does a second contact add?** Hold total communication time, source effort and information constant while varying number/independence of sources. Measure beliefs before adoption. Current centrality interventions identify selection-rule effects better than reinforcement thresholds. More contacts can provide redundancy, opportunities to ask questions, demonstrations and insurance against an unavailable source.
4. **PSY-C03, gendered reception versus ultimate opportunity.** Recipient ratings and training attendance differ, while pooled diffusion penalties are imprecise. Do selected women compensate with extra effort? Are informal channels substituting for formal demonstrations? Does the burden fall on unpaid labour or limit career entry? These are hypotheses. The source does not identify those compensations or a female wage effect. Reservation does not randomise biological sex or erase selection into the pool of willing communicators.
5. **PSY-C04, usability and social meaning.** Randomise social framing/support while keeping price, device performance and orientation constant; measure beliefs before decisions and distinguish successful task-specific use from hours worn. Include users excluded by the selected clinical samples: lower income, non-English language, major dexterity/cognitive barriers and more severe hearing loss. Separate Deaf cultural identification from older adults’ acquired hearing loss. None of the inspected studies establishes a universal stigma parameter.
6. **PSY-C05, components of the mobility package.** Compare supported bicycles with bicycle-only provision, unrestricted money, safe walking or other local transport, while measuring route quality and safety directly. Zambia’s mechanics, monitoring, school-use condition and resale prohibition are part of treatment. No independent maintenance or conditionality effect is estimated. Bihar’s health-facility road proxy is not the actual route to school.
7. **PSY-C05, reported agency and durable control.** Track who can lend, sell, repair and refuse use of a bicycle; observe family bargaining beyond survey statements. Education, agency and norms may reinforce each other, but the current studies do not identify the mediation sequence. School eligibility omits girls already outside schooling; urban and some very remote schools were excluded. The inspected sampling passages do not establish a disability or cycling-ability exclusion rule. Disability/cycling-ability representation and route accessibility remain unmeasured applicability limits. Long-run work and earnings remain unknown.

## Coverage gaps and omitted negative cases

This strand is modern and field-experiment-heavy. It does not independently establish ancient, medieval or industrial-era changes in trust, habit or identity; those require different records and cannot be backfilled from these experiments. It also lacks a rigorous historical comparison of cash/payment habits or communications routines after institutional change. Existing DIG and other programme strands should supply those if supported, without treating them as independent replications of the present cases.

Accessibility evidence is concentrated on selected older hearing-aid users and school-age cyclists. Wheelchairs, prostheses, visually impaired users, public-space design, nonusers never reaching a clinic and maintenance failures in low-resource settings remain underrepresented. The bicycle evidence does not establish disability/cycling-ability representation or outcomes for girls unable to use a standard bicycle; this is not evidence of an exclusion rule. The design does not estimate global non-adoption or abandonment prevalence.

The strongest existing negative cases are: nonpersistent fertilizer use; little change after raw seaweed trials and persistence in spacing; weak unincentivised extension and stalled benchmark villages; negative reception without a precise pooled gender uptake penalty; hearing-aid refusal/returns and null logged-use contrasts; non-significant fee/free differences; and absent/imprecise broader mobility or pooled aspirations effects in the inspected May2025 Zambia manuscript, with the final abstract’s different aspirations assertion unresolved. These should remain visible when integrating the cases.

## Hypotheses explicitly rejected or downgraded

- Higher output always makes an input package privately profitable — rejected for the tested Busia configurations.
- One successful trial automatically creates persistent use — not supported by SAFI follow-up.
- Available raw information automatically produces learning — not supported for the measured seaweed configuration; the exact cognitive mechanism remains open.
- Networks transmit knowledge without paid or unpaid communication work — contradicted by the Malawi incentive comparison in its scope.
- Negative identity perceptions necessarily produce lower ultimate diffusion — not established by pooled Malawi outcomes.
- Higher price is causally irrelevant for hearing aids — not identified by sequential price cohorts.
- Paying makes people value an asset more — not established by Zambia’s direct fee/free contrasts.
- All-weather roads are strictly necessary for a successful bicycle programme — not established by observational moderation and an imprecise non-road coefficient.
- Better attendance or an agency index establishes labour-market emancipation — outside the observed outcomes.
