## Four attempted falsifications

**1. Task-dependent performance and coexistence — SYN-K11; SYNTHESIS lines 23–29.** Lechtman’s mechanical comparisons establish trade-offs, not historical chooser motives. Printing workflows supply feasible reasons for persistence, but wages, capital and sponsorship remain rivals. The original Eyferth article supplies a useful additional countercase: later Zhouzhi households persisted with homespun while selling factory-cloth coupons amid cash scarcity. This is a separate post-1949 episode, not an explanation imported into IND-C07’s earlier period. It blocks an inference from coexistence to freely chosen task superiority. The synthesis already blocks that inference at line 27, and IND-C07 explicitly separates periods. **Accept the stated proposition:** universal replacement is falsified; the relative historical causes of coexistence remain unknown. [Lechtman, pp. 498–503](https://www.academia.edu/31356952/Lechtman_Arsenic_Bronze_Dirty_Copper_1996), [McDermott and Burke, pp. 13–15](https://hkupress.hku.hk/image/catalog/pdf-preview/9789888208081.pdf), [Eyferth, pp. 373–374 and 386–387](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0020859012000521).

**2. Risk versus cash — SYN-K14; SYNTHESIS line 35.** Random assignment and pre-investment treatments make this more than a retrospective needs story. The sharper countercheck is outcome specificity: cash has a significant chemical-input response even though its estimated total-cost response is small. Consequently the result cannot rank insurance above cash for every input or adoption decision. The paper also disallows direct treatment-intensity equivalence. **Accept the bounded aggregate-investment inference**, with AGR-K15/16’s existing qualifications; a general ordering of constraints is unestablished. [Karlan et al., pp. 599–601 and Table IV, p. 625](https://www.atai-research.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/Agricultural-Decisions-after-Relaxing-Credit-and-Risk-Constraints.pdf).

**3. Actor incentives — SYN-K12; SYNTHESIS line 51.** The prescribing audit varies an observable antecedent and predicts different encounter outcomes. Outside purchase can also signal distrust or offense; the relative-pharmacy, knowledge and gift scripts specifically probe those rivals. Demand still matters when dispensing remains in hospital, so the defensible finding is an interaction, not “patients do not matter.” **Accept the local incentive interpretation**, while keeping script identification separate from pure revenue identification or reform effects. Independent access was partial: substantial indexed original-text and publisher passages were obtained, but CAPTCHA/401 prevented a complete table recheck. Exact percentages here remain grounded in the frozen MED-K22 record, not a newly verified table. [Original paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304387814000704).

**4. Maintenance and reversal — SYN-K13; SYNTHESIS lines 53–63.** Demand can cause both low use and low repair; ecology can change with programme activity. The malaria review explicitly permits structural changes that remove the need for active suppression. HAPIN supplies a stronger endpoint countercase: high supported use and lower exposure did not yield a statistically distinguishable birth-weight benefit. It therefore blocks applying Odisha’s low-use explanation to all clinical nulls. **Accept the scoped recurring-operation proposition**, not a unique decline mechanism. SYN-K13 already marks the test underdetermined and line 63 names the necessary boundary. [Cohen et al., Background and Results](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1475-2875-11-122), [Hanna et al., 2012 working paper](https://remahanna.scholars.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum3176/files/remahanna/files/up_in_smoke_may_2012.pdf), [HAPIN, adherence/results and Table 2](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/124935/cdc_124935_DS1.pdf).
