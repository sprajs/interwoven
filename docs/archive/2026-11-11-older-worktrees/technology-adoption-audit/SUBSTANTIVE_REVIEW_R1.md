**Confirmed defects and required repairs.** P1 means correct before relying on the affected relation; P2 means resolve before edition handoff; P3 is a small consistency repair.

1. **R1-01 — P1: IND-E04 puts the cheap-coal mechanism in the wrong location.** In [IND graph, IND-E04](/Users/szymon/.codex/worktrees/4b39/big_idea/research/technology-adoption-audit/r1-snapshot/contributions/IND/graph.json:362), low delivered coal cost enables fuel-intensive atmospheric engines, but `scope.configuration` specifies Cornish pumping. Nuvolari distinguishes coal-mining locations with cheap fuel from Cornwall's expensive imported coal; the latter supported the value of Watt's fuel economy. IND-K03 preserves the distinction. [Nuvolari, working paper, printed pp.10–12](https://pure.tue.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/2343508/669221.pdf).

   **Repair:** scope IND-E04 to early coalfield applications and their deployment/diffusion; keep IND-E05's dear-coal comparison in Cornwall. **Re-review:** inspect both subepisodes of IND-C02 and the two incident edges. A reader querying the cheap-coal edge must recover the coalfield population, not Cornwall. This is a supported correction, not a dispute about the whole steam-adoption thesis.

2. **R1-02 — P2: shared node identities bridge distinct historical configurations.** [MAT-T28](/Users/szymon/.codex/worktrees/4b39/big_idea/research/technology-adoption-audit/r1-snapshot/contributions/MAT/graph.json:204) explicitly merges an ancient bloomery process with its modern replica.    The edge scopes are often careful, and the explorer contains **no causal-closure engine**. The defect is identity in the reusable graph, contrary to v2's distinct-configuration rule—not evidence that the current interface has already asserted those causal paths.

A shared concept category may remain separately identified as a category, without equating its instances. **Re-review:** check all incident edges and within-case subepisodes, not merely whether case IDs differ. MAT-E08 and MAT-E06 must no longer share an operation identity; a historical path must not arise solely from a common resource label. Do not mechanically prohibit every multi-case concept node.

3. **R1-03 — P2: IND-K10's diagnostic conclusion exceeds its bounded claim.** The [mechanism chain](/Users/szymon/.codex/worktrees/4b39/big_idea/research/technology-adoption-audit/r1-snapshot/contributions/IND/claims.json:265) concludes that the presumed wage surge cannot explain timing. The top-level statement more carefully challenges a general surge in reconstructed hand-spinning pay. Mixed fibres, working-time assumptions and sparse commercial cotton observations leave the relevant marginal incentive unresolved; the record itself admits this. The inspected manuscript labels its proposed alternative invention motives speculative. [Humphries and Schneider, accepted manuscript, pp.15–16,25–32,37–41](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid%3A4f321519-a0b7-4879-a521-139cbca81b25/download_file?safe_filename=Humphries_and_Schneider_Spinning_the_industrial_revolution.pdf&type_of_work=Journal+article).

   **Repair:** replace the diagnostic endpoint with “weakens the proposed general wage-surge premise.” Preserve `underdetermined`; distinguish wage levels, wage changes, hourly costs and returns to a particular machine. **Re-review:** ensure the test cannot be exported as a decisive exclusion of wage incentives. A credible competing measurement interpretation was located in [Allen's published abstract](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/ehr.12850); original full-text retrieval failed, so this audit does not adjudicate his calculations.
