# R1 independent industrial and labour evidence audit

Reviewed 5 November 2026. The target was exclusively the frozen `r1-snapshot`; no main-programme files were edited. METHODS.md and METHODS_ADDENDUM_V2.md supplied the audit contract. This is a bounded evidence review, not a replication or an endorsement of all industrial/labour records.

## Scope and selection

Verified frozen denominators: IND has 8 cases, 25 claims, 16 sources and 30 edges; LBR has 2 cases, 7 claims, 4 sources and 2 edges. Before opening source passages, `selection.json` recorded eight exact claims and reasons: **LBR-K01, LBR-K02, LBR-K03, LBR-K04, IND-K03, IND-K10, IND-K23, IND-K24**. LBR-K03 and IND-K23 are the ordinary descriptive controls. The rest were chosen for weak access, synthesis importance, causal interpretation or quantitative outcome risk. This purposive sample supplies no population error rate.

The review inspected 8/32 unique claims (4/25 IND, 4/7 LBR), connected to IND-C02, IND-C04, IND-C08 and LBR-C01. It examined 10/32 unique edges: **IND-E04, IND-E05, IND-E14, IND-E26, IND-E27, IND-E28, IND-E29, IND-E30, LBR-E01, LBR-E02**. Nine had their selected supporting evidence checked; IND-E14 is explicitly **partial** because co-support IND-K11/Ure was not independently passage-audited. It must not be counted as a fully verified edge. Adjacent unselected claims encountered in cases or source pages were not added to the audited denominator.

## Findings requiring attention

**R1-IND-01 — Moderate: IND-E04 puts the cheap-coal mechanism in the wrong episode.** Its node IND-T06 is low delivered coal cost; the edge enables fuel-intensive atmospheric engines. Yet `scope.configuration` calls this Cornish pumping. The cited Nuvolari working-paper pp.10–12 distinguishes early coalfield use from expensive imported coal in Cornwall. The claim IND-K03 and comparison IND-X01 preserve that distinction; the graph loses it. Change the edge scope to early coalfield Newcomen applications and its stage to early deployment/diffusion. Keep IND-E05 scoped to fuel economy under dear coal. No new historical inference is required for this repair. Re-review the corrected edge against the two explicit subepisodes in IND-C02. [Nuvolari working paper](https://pure.tue.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/2343508/669221.pdf).

**R1-IND-02 — Moderate: IND-K10's diagnostic chain is more conclusive than its claim.** The bounded attributed claim is supported. But `test.mechanism_chain` ends by saying the presumed wage surge cannot explain timing. The reconstruction challenges a general surge in typical observed pay; it does not alone identify the marginal commercial incentive, the relevant hourly cost, or profitability at changing machine prices/productivity. Its own `remaining_ambiguity` acknowledges this. Replace that endpoint with “weakens the proposed general wage-surge premise.” Keep `underdetermined`, the mixed-fibre and hours limitations, and the distinction between a wage level and a wage increase. This is a wording/logic repair, not a finding that the reconstruction is wrong. [Inspected accepted manuscript, especially pp.15–16,25–32,37–41](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid%3A4f321519-a0b7-4879-a521-139cbca81b25/download_file?safe_filename=Humphries_and_Schneider_Spinning_the_industrial_revolution.pdf&type_of_work=Journal+article).

**R1-IND-03 — Moderate, provisional: IND-E14 upgrades a partially speculative connection.** “Cheap and controlled labour recruitment complements mechanised British spinning” has `basis: observed-contribution`. The audited K10 source directly discusses labour supply/control in hand spinning, while p.41 labels the proposed alternative invention motives speculative. That is not yet direct evidence for this factory-recruitment edge. Co-support K11 is described as Ure's advocacy, which itself cannot demonstrate realised practice. Preserve the link as hypothesis or acquire direct recruitment/production evidence; alternatively narrow it to the documented hand-spinning organisation. A final disposition requires passage-review of K11, outside this fixed sample. Do not treat this provisional objection as a disproof of the historical mechanism.

**R1-LBR-01 — Minor diagnostic repair:** the first clause of LBR-K02's `strongest_rival` (“Aggregate recovery may conceal incumbent losses”) restates the focal distinction instead of opposing it. Use the already-listed local-demand/selection alternative as the rival, with a prediction and an explicit account of which checks address it. The study's conditional causal design remains useful; a poor rival-field clause does not invalidate it.

## Defensible claims and boundaries

LBR-K01 is an accurate attribution to the author-page abstract; its limited access is honestly stated. LBR-K02–K04 preserve the essential distinction between incumbent outcomes and later cohorts. Population definitions and the wage-power caveat match the inspected passages. Linkage weighting does not make the linked sample random, occupation scores are not observed personal pay, and decadal observation cannot establish the duration of unemployment. The appendix and raw data were not replicated. [Author summary](https://jamesfeigenbaum.github.io/research/att/); [labour study](https://www.dpgross.com/papers/answering-call-automation.pdf).

IND-K23's descriptive configuration distinction passes. IND-K24 accurately attributes the role of compatible machines, redesign, utilities and services. The source also describes productive group-drive arrangements, so the mechanism must not become “only unit drive enabled reorganisation.” The existing claims largely respect this. IND-E27/28's joint-input arrays should identify a local service/redesign bundle more explicitly if a downstream system uses them: their presence cannot create joint necessity. The source does not establish that workers gained pay, autonomy or compensating opportunities. IND-C08 appropriately leaves those outcomes unresolved. [Devine, pp.353–363,368–371](https://gwern.net/doc/economics/automation/1983-devine.pdf).

IND-K03 supports a historically plausible fuel-price pathway rather than an isolated coal-price effect. Its proposed matched-site comparison is diagnostic but was not performed. IND-K24 similarly proposes a comparison capable of addressing demand-led expansion, but the acquired engineering examples do not perform that identification. Their `underdetermined` labels are appropriate. A filled test object is not a completed test.

## Competing interpretation and lineage

A targeted competing-interpretation search located Robert Allen's *Spinning their wheels* (2020) and its 2018 working-paper antecedent. The publisher abstract disputes the relevance of the wage/productivity evidence to mechanisation incentives; the longer working-paper abstract specifies unknown hours and selective institutional productivity observations. These are meaningful alternatives to the measurement interpretation in IND-K10. The Oxford full-text attempts returned HTTP 403, so this audit does **not** adjudicate Allen's calculations or claim to have read that article in full. Humphries and Schneider's later reply was located but not passage-audited. The frozen dossier already admits that the rejoinders were not assessed. [Allen publisher abstract](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/ehr.12850); [working-paper abstract and original-repository pointer](https://ideas.repec.org/p/oxf/esohwp/_166.html).

Five unique frozen sources underpin this selected sample: IND-S03, IND-S08, IND-S16, LBR-S01 and LBR-S02. The industrial PDFs/text are preserved in the snapshot; the published 61-page QJE PDF was retrieved into this audit directory. Locators identify the correct editions: Nuvolari working-paper printed pagination differs from PDF indexing by two pages; Humphries/Schneider uses accepted-manuscript pagination; Devine begins at printed p.347. The original archival records and engineering articles behind the scholarship were not independently acquired. The two telephone papers share researchers and related corporate history; their claims are not independent historical corroborations. Industrial technical examples and reconstructed wages likewise cannot be counted as independent repeated experiments merely because the graph contains several edges.

## Transition implications and acceptance

The selected evidence supports distinguishing task displacement, aggregate employment, occupational destinations, measured pay and welfare. It cannot show that a specific displaced person could access a growing occupation, how long adjustment took, or who captured productivity gains. IND-C04/C08 and LBR-C01 mostly preserve these unknowns well. The strongest usable synthesis is a question to investigate—entry barriers, demand, ownership and bargaining—not a forecast of safe human roles.

No selected top-level claim was found fabricated or directly contradicted by its cited passage. Accept the bounded claims with their existing qualifications, repair IND-E04 and the IND-K10 diagnostic wording, and hold IND-E14's observed-contribution interpretation pending narrower wording or additional evidence. This acceptance applies only to the disclosed sample and its stated access, not to the whole programme.
