# Labour-transition follow-ups

- Read the full published AT&T adoption paper and its archival contrasts before using organisational adaptation as a decisive causal explanation.
- Independently audit the linked-operator sample, weighting and occupational-income versus observed-wage distinctions.
- Inspect the title-share imputation and alternative weights in New Frontiers; a new-title share is not a direct employment count.
- Add non-US transitions, unpaid labour, worker ownership and excluded racial/gender populations.
- Compare transitions with and without accessible growing substitute sectors, rather than extrapolating an aggregate rebound.
- Historical maintenance or trust requirements do not establish future wages or resistance to automation.
