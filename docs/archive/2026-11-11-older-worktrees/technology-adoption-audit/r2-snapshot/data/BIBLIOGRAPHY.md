## AGR-S01 — Prehistoric Landscape Management in the Andean Highlands: Raised Field Agriculture and its Environmental Impact

[Prehistoric Landscape Management in the Andean Highlands: Raised Field Agriculture and its Environmental Impact](https://anthropology.sas.upenn.edu/sites/www.sas.upenn.edu.anthropology/files/page/EricksonPrehistoricLandscapeManagementAndeanRaisedField%26EnvironImpact1992b.pdf).
