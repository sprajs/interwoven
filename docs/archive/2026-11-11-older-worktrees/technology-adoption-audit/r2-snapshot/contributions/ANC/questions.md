# Ancient engineering: open questions and discriminating follow-up

This contribution establishes bounded observations and candidate mechanisms. It does not supply a representative ancient-technology sample, a diffusion denominator, a comparative cost series, or an estimate of net welfare. Its six cases were selected partly because they expose the difference between an impressive object and an operating service; that selection may overrepresent monumental hydraulic systems and underrepresent ordinary repairs, unsuccessful projects, household work, and stable older techniques.

## Consequential unresolved questions

1. **What was Hero's rotating sphere for?** The transmitted instructions and the inspected Roby excerpt are compatible with intellectual, teaching, display, and practical experimental purposes. An ancient user's testimony or an excavated apparatus connected to a specified task would discriminate more strongly than a modern efficient reconstruction. The latter could test physical capability under historically defensible materials and fuel assumptions, but could not establish ancient deployment. Acquire Keyser's original analysis and the full Roby monograph before resolving the historiographical dispute (ANC-C01; ANC-K02–K03; ANC-S01–S02).

2. **What explains Barbegal's seasonality and staged cessation?** Carbonate records support operation, interruptions, and differences among selected flumes. Shipping demand, other flour markets, water availability, equipment deterioration, and changing grain supply can share those predictions. Independently dated bakery/storage or shipment evidence, hydrological reconstruction, and repair sequences across both wheel trains would help separate them. The 2018 and 2024 papers reuse the carbonate archive and share investigators; their agreement must not be counted as independent replication. The market, workforce status, ownership, output, and final abandonment cause remain unresolved (ANC-C02; ANC-K05–K07; ANC-S03–S04).

3. **What did Rome's maintenance arrangements accomplish in practice?** Frontinus identifies duties, funding, access rules, and repair scheduling, but prescriptions do not establish compliance or the distribution of service. Compare independently dated repairs and failures across districts or conduits, including wells, household supply, and provincial alternatives. Distinguish the workforce around 100 CE from Procopius's emergency account in 537; they are separate episodes, not one continuous labour panel. Independent evidence for boatmill scale and duration would test the reported substitution, while leaving the narrative's quantitative sufficiency unverified (ANC-C03; ANC-K08–K11; ANC-S05–S07).

4. **Where, when, and under which rights did early underground galleries operate?** Similar shafts and tunnels may access different water sources. Original excavation sequences, luminescence contexts, source tracing, and elevation surveys are needed before treating southeast Arabian and Iranian works as one configuration or assigning a single origin. Polybius's reported five-generation cultivation rights do not themselves establish tax exemption, enforceable incentives, consent, or a complete financing arrangement. Ancient grants, disputes, work records, and matched projects under different arrangements would discriminate incentive explanations from political command or inherited local organisation (ANC-C04; ANC-K12–K15; ANC-S08–S09).

5. **How continuous was Dujiangyan's maintenance institution, and at what cost?** Late Han attestations do not establish identical institutions from the conventional Qin foundation onward. Obtain the journal PDF, inscription photographs or rubbings, and competing readings before strengthening claims based on Peng's reproduced article. Reconstruct dated channel configurations, repair quantities, service interruptions, labour mobilisation, and competing schemes. A present-day hydraulic model can clarify mechanisms but cannot fill historical gaps. Evidence that geometry reduced repair needs and evidence that political mobilisation sustained expensive repair are compatible; their relative contributions require measurements beyond longevity (ANC-C05; ANC-K16–K18; ANC-S10–S11).

6. **Did Dholavira's water damage cause settlement decline, follow it, or share another cause?** The institutional phase narrative and GPR hypotheses are different evidence types. Excavation must confirm the latter's proposed features and damage; independently dated flood deposits, seismic evidence, changing inflow, settlement reorganisation, and repairs could test competing explanations. Storage volume alone cannot establish household reliability without inflow, leakage, withdrawal, access, and population evidence. Neither monumental scale nor later non-restoration identifies the political regime or the direction of causation (ANC-C06; ANC-K19–K21; ANC-S12–S13).

## Access and evidence gaps

- **Hero:** the nineteenth-century translation and a ten-page publisher excerpt were inspected. The full Roby book, Keyser's original article, Greek textual apparatus, and ancient user records were not acquired. Editorial headings and later reception should remain distinct from ancient terminology and purpose.
- **Barbegal:** full 2018 XML and the 2024 article PDF were inspected. The broader excavation and specimen-selection archives, ownership accounts, prices, and a regional mill denominator were not assembled.
- **Rome:** transmitted primary narratives and Bannon's scholarly analysis were inspected. Most underlying inscriptions and juristic contexts in Bannon were not checked independently; no audited expenditure or household service series was obtained.
- **Arabian/Iranian galleries:** Boucharlat's full chapter OCR and Polybius's translation were inspected. Underlying excavation reports and dating datasets were largely not acquired; the host flags Polybius's page as not fully proofread. Translation into modern technical or fiscal terminology needs care.
- **Dujiangyan:** the substantial attributed Chinese reproduction was inspected and publisher metadata/abstract checked, but the original journal PDF could not be acquired (404/412 responses). Original rubbings were not inspected. English formulations in this contribution are translations, and the reproduction may omit apparatus. The modern model PDF was inspected; its historical continuity claims are not treated as independently validated history.
- **Dholavira:** the full GPR article and ICOMOS evaluation were inspected. The full excavation archive and independent chronological reassessment were not acquired. The GPR article gives inconsistent surveyed-area totals, so no exact area is adopted.

## Missing contrasts and labour evidence

The most useful additions would be contemporary handmills and animal mills that remained in service; small waterworks and wells; local irrigation without extensive state mobilisation; settlements without a suitable source or hydraulic gradient; and failed schemes with well-preserved construction and repair records. Their absence limits claims about rejection of a feasible alternative, the distinctiveness of state capacity, and the opportunity cost of machinery. Non-exposure to a workable configuration is not demonstrated refusal.

For nearly every case, ordinary operators, unpaid household contributions, bargaining rights, and transition durations are poorly observed. Frontinus's enslaved maintenance gangs directly prevent interpreting all work as voluntary employment, but they do not identify the status of Barbegal's millers. Peng's account of labour obligations likewise does not quantify individual burdens or prove one arrangement across centuries. Future livelihood implications should therefore be framed as questions about access to resources, command over work, and distribution of gains, without asserting ancient wage effects or worker transitions that these sources cannot show.

## Hypotheses deliberately withheld

- Roman slavery prevented all productive mechanisation.
- Hero's sphere was a commercially competitive industrial engine whose use was irrationally rejected.
- A single Iranian invention explains all groundwater-gallery systems.
- Five-generation cultivation rights in Polybius are explicitly a tax exemption in the inspected translation.
- Dujiangyan has operated at an unchanged service level under an unchanged institution since its conventional foundation.
- Abandonment of an installation proves the disappearance of its technical knowledge.
- Dholavira's water-system failure uniquely caused its urban decline.
- More monumental infrastructure implies greater welfare or a more capable political regime.

These propositions are withheld because they overrun the inspected evidence or turn a conditional mechanism into a universal explanation. Their rejection does not establish the opposite universal claim.
