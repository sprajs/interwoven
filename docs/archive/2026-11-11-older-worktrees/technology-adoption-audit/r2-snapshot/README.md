# A research programme on technological development and adoption

Status: research in progress, started 5 November 2026. This edition studies how technologies become useful systems and why uptake varies across people, places and periods. Historical findings will be kept separate from future-facing hypotheses.

Start with [the charter](CHARTER.md), [methods and evidence contract](METHODS.md) and [execution record](PLAN.md). Independent domain investigations will appear in `contributions/`; integrated findings in `analysis/`; reusable ledgers and graph in `data/`; criticism and responses in `reviews/`.

This collection is a purposive research programme, not an exhaustive tree of all technologies or a statistical sample of historical adoption.
