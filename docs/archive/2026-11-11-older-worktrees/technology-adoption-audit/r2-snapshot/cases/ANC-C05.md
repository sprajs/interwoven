# ANC-C05 — Dujiangyan's late Han repair evidence and the limits of longevity claims

**Period:** Ancient focus: conventional third-century BCE foundation through late Han inscriptions of 168/199 CE; 2020 model is a separate analytical comparator. **Region:** Min River and Chengdu Plain, Sichuan, China. **Domain:** irrigation; river engineering; sediment management; maintenance institutions.

Late Han evidence supports organised upkeep. Modern hydraulics clarifies physical processes but cannot close gaps in ancient institutional or configuration history.

[Read the substantive research dossier](../contributions/ANC/report.md).

## Stages

**invention:** Conventional Li Bing/Qin foundation is context, not a precise date newly established here.

**feasibility:** Modern geometry offers a modelled mechanism; ancient functioning cannot be quantified from that model.

**deployment:** Inscription-based maintenance attestations by late Han.

**diffusion:** No count of Chinese irrigation adopters or direct transmission to other systems.

**scaling:** No historical area/benefit series established.

**non-adoption/niche:** No matched alternative design choice observed.

**displacement/reversal:** Continuity in naming and later use does not prove unchanged operation or no interruptions.

## Episode

**technology configuration:** A: late Han repair episodes interpreted from inscriptions. B: 2020 numerical geometry of Fish Mouth, Feishayan, Baopingkou and associated channels.

**task:** A: maintain water diversion. B: explain water/sediment partition under specified flows.

**population:** A: local water officials and repair workforce; household population unknown. B: simulated hydraulic system, not ancient adopters.

**interval:** A: 168/199 CE attestations with uncertain earlier continuity. B: modern model only.

**contemporary alternatives:** Different diversion geometries or water sources are possible analytical alternatives; no contemporary rejected proposal established.; Dredging plus repair rather than permanent avoidance of maintenance.

**adoption measure:** Inscription readings mediated by Peng; numerical experiment. Neither gives a continuous adoption or uptime series.

**observation limits:** Modern simulation and later traditions cannot fill centuries of missing records; original inscription rubbings not checked.

## Actors

**chooser:** Water officials and appointed supervisors in Peng's late Han account; exact foundation decisions not reconstructed.

**payer:** Ancient detailed funding unknown; Peng explicitly lacks pre-Tang treasury records. Later funding cannot be imputed to Han amounts.

**operator maintainer:** Organised repair workers with labour-obligation interpretation; names and categories selectively commemorated.

**affected nonchooser:** Cultivators, obligated households and other river users; no quantified allocation of benefits and burdens.

**ability to refuse:** Corvée interpretation indicates constrained choice for some workers; no basis to treat all participation as voluntary or all work as unpaid.

## Labour

**tasks:** Dredging, embankment work, monitoring and coordination. Ancient episodes show labour mobilisation; modern model does not count historical work hours.

**entry barriers:** Specialist knowledge and official organisation; actual occupational recruitment unknown.

**bargaining ownership:** Official supervision is attested in interpretation; worker bargaining and allocation of crops/water unknown.

**transition:** No individual or cohort transition series; institutional evolution cannot substitute for one.

**distribution:** No ancient wage, mortality, household burden or irrigated-benefit distribution measured.

**evidence limit:** Longevity cannot establish that maintenance work was light, fairly distributed or universally beneficial.

## Mechanisms and counterevidence

Candidate/supported mechanisms: Flow and sediment depend on local geometry.; Repeated repair requires labour organisation and appropriate timing.; Official and ritual authority can coexist with technical work.

Qin maintenance continuity is inferred, rules have disputed dates, and the model needs further validation and does not reproduce every ancient configuration.
