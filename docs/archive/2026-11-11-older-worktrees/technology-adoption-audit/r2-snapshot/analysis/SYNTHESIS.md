# What the histories explain—and what they do not

Working synthesis 1, 5 November 2026. The first wave contains 59 cases, 210 claims and 129 source records, including a two-case labour supplement and integrated inference records. Independent review and expansion are still in progress. This text is a comparative inference from a purposive case library, not an estimate of universal adoption laws. Case and claim identifiers lead to the underlying evidence, rivals and observation limits.

The principal cross-case propositions are preserved as SYN-K10–K18 in the [claim ledger](../data/CLAIM_LEDGER.md), with comparison logic in [SYN-X01–X04](../data/COMPARISONS.md). Counts are descriptive of the collection; sources and cases are not independent replications.

## The principal result

The useful historical unit is usually a **technology in a particular operating arrangement, doing a task for particular actors**. A device's maximum capability is only one part of that arrangement. Inputs, production quality, skills, distribution, maintenance, decision rights and alternatives determine what service it can actually deliver. The same apparatus can serve different tasks, be combined with older practices, or benefit its purchaser while imposing costs on people who did not choose it.

This proposition is a way to ask better questions. It is not sufficient as an explanation: saying that every outcome depends on “the system” would make the account impossible to disprove. The cases become informative where they identify a particular missing input, a measured change in incentives, a contemporary alternative or a counterexample to a claimed necessity. They remain underdetermined where several mechanisms fit the same record.

The strongest cross-case finding is therefore a set of distinctions, supported by concrete contrasts, followed by conditional mechanisms. It is not one master motive shared by all humans.

## 1. Invention, effective service and welfare are different outcomes

Hero's steam sphere is a transmitted description of rotation, with no productive load documented in the inspected passage. Barbegal is evidence of an operating mill complex and repeated interventions. Neither source supplies an empire-wide diffusion rate. The comparison rules out jumping from a physical principle to a missed industrial revolution; it does not establish every obstacle to ancient steam power. [ANC-C01–C02; ANC-K01–K07](../contributions/ANC/report.md).

The same distinction matters for much later systems. A municipal decision, plant completion, network connection and effective water service can occur at different dates. A smartphone in a household need not be available to a child at lesson time. A laboratory stove's performance does not establish continued use, exposure reduction or a health effect. These are different links in the chain, requiring different observations. [MED-C04](../contributions/MED/report.md); [DIG-C04](../contributions/DIG/report.md); [AGR-C07](../contributions/AGR/report.md).

An adoption claim should identify its numerator, denominator, task and time window—or state why the historical evidence only supports a qualitative proxy. Surviving objects, city press installations, editions, copies, readers, subscriptions, owners and active users are not interchangeable. Missing records may reflect preservation and research intensity. A graph edge derived from engineering possibility is not evidence of actual transmission.

## 2. “Better” is conditional on a task and an alternative

Metallurgy does not support a universal hardness ladder. Arsenical bronze and tin bronze differ in working properties; an alloy suitable for resilient sheet need not be the preferred cutting material. Iron's growth in Canaan is not adequately established as a simple consequence of superior heat treatment or exhausted tin. The evidence is stronger on material properties and surviving production than on prices or every user's motive. [MAT-C02, MAT-C05; MAT-K04–K07, MAT-K15–K17](../contributions/MAT/report.md).

Printing offers a corresponding organisational contrast. Woodblocks could remain useful for repeat printing, while type served particular runs and projects. Korean manuscript circulation could expand commercially alongside printed books. Copying sometimes produced selections or variants rather than a defective substitute for identical printed copies. These observations challenge automatic replacement; they do not prove that every surviving practice was efficient or freely chosen. [NET-C02–C03](../contributions/NET/report.md).

Industrial systems also combined old and new. Steam could supplement water; electric motors could use electricity generated from steam; machine-spun yarn could support handloom weaving while displacing hand spinning. The correct unit is the operation or task, not an entire civilisation crossing a technological threshold. [IND-C03, IND-C07–C08](../contributions/IND/report.md).

## 3. Cost matters, but purchase price is an incomplete and politically formed measure

Whole-use burdens include financing, inputs, training, time, uncertainty, repair, switching and coordination. These categories do not always reduce to money. A worker's bodily effort, a caregiver's time and a household member's control over output can differ even when the purchaser's accounts improve.

The northern Ghana experiment is a useful discriminating case. Insurance changed farm investment more than cash grants alone for the sampled farmers, supporting a risk constraint under the tested contracts. It does not show that credit never binds, that insurance uptake will remain unsubsidised, or that greater input use necessarily improved welfare. [AGR-C06; AGR-K15–K16](../contributions/AGR/report.md).

Jahaly-Pacharr exposes a different margin: improved water control and household income could coexist with changed rights over rice and demands for women's labour. Women responded through negotiation, withdrawal and paid work where alternatives permitted. Later machinery and input problems prevent a labour-conflict-only account. [AGR-C03; AGR-K07–K09](../contributions/AGR/report.md).

Transport adds an interface test. Roman road-versus-water comparisons change when loading and onward delivery enter the journey; early electric fleets could serve bounded routes without displacing petrol for all trips. The relevant service configuration can therefore change the apparent performance ranking. [MOB-C01, MOB-C05](../contributions/MOB/report.md).

Historical prices are not outside institutions. Labour recruitment, tenure, tariffs, ownership and access to infrastructure help produce the observed costs. A factor-price explanation and an account of power may describe different parts of the same causal chain. Neither is automatically sufficient: the relative contributions of wages, coal, engineering, trade and colonial coercion to industrialisation remain contested. [IND-C04–C06](../contributions/IND/report.md).

## 4. Institutions can enable, redirect, compel or obstruct use

“The state,” “religion” and “culture” are too coarse to have one signed effect on adoption. A religious commission can finance a book; learned correctors can legitimate an edition; a mandate can expand access or enforce compliance; an institution can exclude people from the gains it produces.

The Ottoman printing case rejects a well-established blanket-ban story as unsupported by the inspected original-document trail. It does not substitute a claim of unrestricted printing everywhere. Specific permissions, subject restrictions, imported books, manuscript alternatives and selected customers require their own histories. [NET-C05; NET-K17–K20](../contributions/NET/report.md).

Ottoman vaccination rules similarly establish state ambition and instruments of compulsion, not realised population coverage. Staff, supply and rural reach remained different constraints. Roman aqueduct upkeep makes enslaved maintenance labour visible alongside technical competence. Productive mechanisation and coercion can coexist; that fact rejects an absolute incompatibility, while leaving local effects of labour prices unresolved. [MED-C02](../contributions/MED/report.md); [ANC-C02–C03](../contributions/ANC/report.md).

The Chinese prescribing audit provides unusually direct evidence about incentives. Changing the expected place of drug purchase sharply changed antibiotic prescribing in the sampled encounters, and altered the effect of patient requests. This challenges a demand-only explanation in that setting. It is not a national prevalence estimate or proof that a system-wide financing reform would have no unintended response. [MED-C08; MED-K22–K24](../contributions/MED/report.md).

## 5. Continued operation is an achievement, and reversals need explanation

Operating knowledge includes rhythms, tolerances and judgement that may not be recoverable from an artifact's geometry. Experimental Meroe smelting demonstrates how changing ore, air, charging and working practice changes outcomes; it does not certify one ancient recipe. Surgical antisepsis likewise involved changing routines, demonstration and contested evidence, rather than merely possession of a chemical or assent to a theory. [MAT-C04](../contributions/MAT/report.md); [MED-C03](../contributions/MED/report.md).

Maintenance can be technically necessary without being institutionally secured. Frontinus describes staffing and planned repairs; Dujiangyan's record makes recurring work visible but does not establish unchanged continuous operation from its conventional foundation. Barbegal's staged cessation while water still flowed prevents a water-supply-only collapse story. [ANC-C02–C05](../contributions/ANC/report.md).

Malaria resurgence is a useful warning against treating successful suppression as permanent capacity. Reduced programme activity, surveillance, ecology and later resistance must be sequenced. The broader resurgence literature's many proposed causes often have weak supporting analysis; recurrence counts cannot become causal probabilities. [MED-C06; MED-K15–K18](../contributions/MED/report.md).

Persistence can also concern consequences rather than the original machine. Ghanaian railway towns retained relative importance after rail traffic declined, partly in a transport geography later served by roads. This does not prove the initial location was optimal or identify one unique persistence mechanism. [MOB-C04](../contributions/MOB/report.md).

The implication is conditional: where service depends on recurring work, measure its funding, skill, supply and actual performance. It does not follow that maintenance is always the dominant bottleneck, or that every decline reflects neglect rather than a changed alternative or objective.

## 6. Human behaviour must be observed in context

People can value safety, comfort, status, trust, autonomy, ritual meaning and convenience, but invoking those words after seeing an outcome explains little. Specify whose behaviour, what information they had, what they could afford or refuse, and what observation distinguishes the proposed motive from another constraint.

The materials cases include sound and appearance as technical objectives; this broadens function beyond a narrow production metric without proving a timeless preference. Household-appliance histories distinguish physical effort, time, output standards and the division of work. A machine can reduce exertion while expectations and the household production boundary change. [MAT-C06](../contributions/MAT/report.md); [AGR-C08](../contributions/AGR/report.md).

Trust also needs a specific object: a practitioner, a measurement, a payment promise or an institution. In the Ghana insurance case, experienced payouts, network observation, price and basis risk leave more than one explanation for demand. Evidence that people declined a product does not itself establish irrationality or distrust. [AGR-C06](../contributions/AGR/report.md).

Direct psychological and accessibility evidence remains a gap in this first synthesis. The second research wave will test these hypotheses against measured behaviour and institutional change rather than filling the gap with assumed human universals.

## The user's digital examples, stated precisely

**Pocket internet is a plausible mechanism, not an established universal causal ranking.** The evidence is compatible with portability making computing available at useful moments, while computers remain better suited to some inputs and tasks. UK 2015 reported device importance differed by age; US 2015 household data show substantial device coexistence. Those observations do not isolate portability from apps, tariffs, distribution, price, performance or network reach. [DIG-C02–C03](../contributions/DIG/report.md).

**The laptop-versus-smartphone price claim is unestablished.** “Most” needs a place, date, product population, new/used distinction, sales weighting and treatment of contracts and recurring costs. No suitable price-distribution comparison has been acquired. This is a question left open, not a finding that the opposite claim is true. [DIG-K24](../contributions/DIG/report.md).

**VR has bounded minority-use evidence, not a global failure verdict.** Ofcom's 2023 UK adult survey reports gaming on VR headsets at 4%, with non-exclusive comparisons including phones at 34% and consoles at 22%. The question has no weekly/monthly use threshold; it is not global ownership, retention or industrial use. A small prolonged-work experiment identifies friction in a specified Quest 2 setup, not the value of every VR task or future device. [DIG-C06; DIG-K18–K21](../contributions/DIG/report.md).

**Technical capability, sales and business success differ.** Microsoft's documented 2015 phone sales coexisted with impairment and strategic retrenchment. The filing establishes those outcomes, not a uniquely identified ecosystem explanation. Kenya's mobile-money experience shows that ordinary phones could support a useful service when cash agents, balances, identification and network access were available; a handset alone did not guarantee immediate cash withdrawal. [DIG-C05, DIG-C07](../contributions/DIG/report.md).

## What these histories permit us to ask about future human roles

Historical adoption repeatedly required skilled production, interpretation, teaching, coordination, repair and adaptation. That is a reason to investigate those activities. It is not proof that they will remain human, scarce, well paid or accessible. A firm may capture the gains, workers may lose bargaining power, and new tasks may require resources displaced people lack.

The telephone-automation comparison demonstrates the distinction: later cohorts' employment resilience coexisted with adverse outcomes for incumbents. A new-work database similarly measures changing titles and tasks, not a list of jobs automatically open to those displaced. [LBR-C01–C02](../contributions/LBR/report.md).

Containerisation adds a bargaining contrast: court records show negotiated funds and disputes over tasks moved off the dock. Trade gains, new equipment roles and compensation arrangements cannot establish that every displaced worker entered an equivalent job. [MOB-C06](../contributions/MOB/report.md).

A defensible future-facing proposition must identify a historical mechanism, a bounded modern analogue, who pays, what skills or rights are needed, and a scenario that would invalidate the analogy. The eventual future-roles document will use that structure. This synthesis does not predict winning occupations, products or investments.

## What would change this synthesis?

The broad framework would lose value if its mechanisms remained interchangeable labels. New research should challenge its named constraints through cases with comparable tasks but different uptake; cases where complements were present yet use did not persist; cases where alternative institutions supplied the same function; and reversals following changed prices, rights or ecology. Shared datasets and connected trade/imperial settings must not be counted as independent confirmations.

This edition's current strengths are source criticism, stage separation and contrasting operating arrangements. Its weaknesses include purposive selection, uneven language access, sparse ordinary-user records, incomplete livelihood transitions and relatively thin dedicated coverage of Oceania, military procurement, precision/repair, direct psychology and modern energy/manufacturing. The next wave is intended to change the account where those omissions matter, not merely add more examples that fit it.
