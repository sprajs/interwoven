## Evidence and scope

- At Xihuatoxtla the layer D charcoal range is 8,990–8,610 cal BP. Older maize-bearing strata are undated. Ranere et al. report the chronology and worked tools, while Piperno et al. identify the plant residues. These are companion reports from one excavation. Matsuoka et al.’s modern sample phylogeny is independent evidence for broad regional ancestry only. The later Yang et al. abstract reports admixture with highland teosinte several millennia after initial domestication, another reason to leave the local population history unresolved.
