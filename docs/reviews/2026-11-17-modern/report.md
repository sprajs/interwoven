## What the inventory verifies

## How the branches connect

**A shared data product can support different measurements.** The EO-1 Advanced Land Imager demonstrated a design that informed Landsat 8’s Operational Land Imager. Landsat 8 reflectance from that imager contributes to USGS Collection 2 Level-2 products. Digital Earth Africa uses reflectance to classify surface water. An OpenET model uses Collection 2 surface temperature, for which Landsat 8 has a separate thermal instrument. Australia’s Water Observations from Space supplies another parent for the African service. Its decision tree was transferred and evaluated on African data. The relationship to Collection 2 is bounded to the documented OpenET method and version. [USGS product and services](https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/news/landsat-product-supports-water-management-worldwide) · [Digital Earth Africa processing specification](https://docs.digitalearthafrica.org/en/latest/data_specs/Landsat_WOfS_specs.html) · [Reviewed evidence for each edge](reviews/land.json)

**A receiving community can adapt an older design more than once.** Japan-to-Taiwan rail transfer is documented twice, for different vehicle generations. Earlier Series 700 technology went into the 700T. The later N700ST drew on the N700S and was adapted for Taiwan. The 700T’s role in the newer train’s name and livery would be a poor basis for a hardware-inheritance claim. In clinical imaging, the Flash scanner genuinely branches into both the Force production line and the CounT research prototype. The Alpha regulatory record identifies Force geometry as its predecessor. Direct inheritance of the CounT detector remains unresolved. [THSRC account of the adaptation](https://www.thsrc.com.tw/ArticleContent/e1e25e6a-4139-44d4-ac30-3f9724f08310) · [Rail review](reviews/transport.json) · [CT review and device submissions](reviews/health.json)

**An intervention can preserve the milestone and change the service status.** Jülich’s allocations and published results support use of its Booster during early access. Fugaku’s benchmark result survived the interruption to its operations. A separate institutional succession link connects K and Fugaku at Kobe. Two technical interconnect links remain candidates because a purportedly independent engineering account repeats the designer’s wording. [RIKEN suspension](https://www.riken.jp/en/news_pubs/news/2026/20261116_2/index.html) · [Technical and institutional evidence review](reviews/computing.json)

Of the 33 new established links, 29 describe dependence, adaptation, succession, structural inclusion or transmission. Four record an affinity such as a shared technical principle. The reader draws the former kinds and shows candidate evidence in record details. Its 62-record selection includes earlier context, so its 31 drawn relationships include two already present at the research baseline. The canonical dataset preserves all link types.
