# Preserved paused batch-12 drafts — not admitted research

This directory preserves the exact 92 unfinished files supplied by the coordinating chat on 11 November 2026. These are historical **drafts**, not current dataset records or independent approval of their claims. The app does not load this directory.

`original/` mirrors their former repository paths. `original-manifest.json` records each original SHA-256 and the dataset base. Every original byte is retained, including claims later corrected, deferred, or not admitted. Do not silently update these originals.

The final disposition manifest and the review report under `docs/reviews/2026-11-11-paused-batch12/` identify what was admitted, repaired, or retained only as a draft, with reasons and review provenance. Small original wave-12 register and lead handoffs are preserved in `handoffs/` to allow resumption. They are draft proposals, not admitted register entries.

Preservation is not an endorsement. Dates, people, places, causal links, resource estimates and source notes here may be incomplete or wrong. Use the active reviewed record and its review artifact for current conclusions.
