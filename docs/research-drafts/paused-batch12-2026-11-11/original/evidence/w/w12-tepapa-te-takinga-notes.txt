Source: Te Tākinga pātaka
URL: https://collections.tepapa.govt.nz/object/159281
Accessed: 2026-11-10

Te Papa identifies Te Tākinga as a pātaka carved by Te Hārete Te Whanarere, Ngāti Makino, around 1850 and originally at Te Hape, Lake Rotoiti. It describes its importance to Ngāti Pikiao, later acquisition and restoration. The catalogue does not identify the particular contents originally stored.
