27 June 1744 afternoon session, Tachanoontia speech: The proceedings report Tachanoontia presented a wampum string in speech at Lancaster; later in the same speech he recalled an earlier boundary treaty associated with a belt. This is a colonial English-language report with an interpreter, not a full record of Haudenosaunee protocol.
