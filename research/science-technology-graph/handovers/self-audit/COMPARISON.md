## Where the independent review is wrong or goes too far

1. **Shared source is not duplicate identity.** The Shen Gua units, board assembly and reuse workflow are different contributions. The treated shaft and composite CB3 assembly are different material/process roles. Joule's revised magnetic configuration is an adaptation, not a second name for unchanged equipment. Reservoir/inlet/duct, relay practice/station and mill/coaking machine are distinct parts. Decision 5 preserves deliberate splits on which these chains depend. The review's 15 `duplicate` entry flags are not 15 proved duplicates.
2. **A missing broad entry does not falsify a bounded one.** Wyatt really is cited; Forbes really used a stethoscope; Elsecar really is a later Newcomen installation; a casting really can be an engine component. Coverage gaps and witness dates need repair without silently replacing their referents. “Wrong witness” is a modelling criticism here, not a false-object verdict.
3. **Some proposed promotions repeat the original scope error.** The original Marshall adoption case is not all antisepsis; the Morse paid-service episode is not all telegraphy; Pearl Street is not all electric supply; EDSAC is not all stored-program computing. The original narrow referents do not acquire tier 0 because the broader identity is missing. Conversely, YBC7289 and Rhind do not deserve promotion merely because they are unusually strong surviving witnesses.
4. **A patent contribution is not refuted by a different isolation implementation.** Last's project could implement Noyce's monolithic/interconnection approach while using epoxy isolation. The existing record already distinguishes patent illustrations from fabricated isolation. More precise wording is welcome; absence of the contribution has not been shown.
5. **A requirement is not a personal transmission claim.** The Blackwall link is explicitly `bounded-technical-requirement`; it does not assert that Cooke read Ørsted. Cooke's credit to Ampère concerns a signalling idea, not automatically the parallel-current experiment. Both scientific content and receiving event matter.
6. **Confidence and influence remain separate.** The number of other laboratories using PCR does not show that its use in the stated workflow was minor. Wyatt being one citation does not measure its influence. A corporate retrospective needs corroboration but is not automatically false. Stage 4 should use `not_assessed` where influence has not been established.
7. **Carnot's substantive note is part of his work.** “Discussed and criticised in note 27” is more precise than “incorporated”; being appended does not itself justify a confidence downgrade. The table is absent from this retained rendering, so its numerical contents remain unchecked.
8. **The Barbegal Facts sentence conflates papers and bounds.** The reopened 2024 *Operation and decline* paper concerns machinery, maintenance and decline; no hardtack/bread argument appears in its text. The earlier 2018 paper is the hardtack lead. The 2024 paper places construction in the second century and cessation in the third. A “by 300” upper bound is weak presentation, not a claim of first operation in 300 or proof of use after a separately fixed abandonment. Function and precise dates stay on the historian list. [2024 paper](https://doi.org/10.1002/gea.22016).
9. **The Shang criticism also confuses a bound with a dated onset.** The node is labelled Chinese section-mold casting and expressly calls “by c. 900 BCE” a contextual upper limit from a museum's 1300–900 interval, not a Shang invention in900. It needs better interval/earliest-record representation; choosing 1300 as an exact first use would invent a date.
10. **The Remote Oceania gap has the wrong date scope.** Its c. 1000–1300 CE label fits a late East Polynesian expansion, not the beginning of all Remote Oceanian settlement. Reopened research places Lapita dispersal to Vanuatu/Fiji millennia earlier; another study explicitly dates East Polynesian pulses. Split the lead into appropriate regional episodes before Stage 7. This does not settle every island's chronology. [Denham et al., abstract](https://doi.org/10.1002/j.1834-4453.2012.tb00113.x), [Wilmshurst et al., abstract](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3033267/).
