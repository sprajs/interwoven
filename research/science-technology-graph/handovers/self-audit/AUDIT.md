# Sceptical self-audit of the science and technology graph

6 November 2026. Report only. No graph, schema, tools, review decisions or site data were edited by this audit.

## Scope and judgement

**The narrowly worded connections are generally better supported than the graph's presentation of a hierarchy of breakthroughs.** I found no connection that I can responsibly reject as a demonstrated historical falsehood. Many records already do the right things: distinguish an apparatus from a result, preserve alternatives, decline to claim exclusive necessity, and distinguish a first surviving witness from first invention. Agreement is an outcome of this review, not evidence that the graph is complete or historically infallible.

The audit is pinned to **`30df3e79`** (the full SHA is in `scope.json`), the committed `main` batch present when this task began. This is **221 entries, 387 events, 131 relationships and 310 sources**, with 35 tier-0 and 85 tier-1 entries. `origin/main` matched the checkout at task start; there was no tracked unfinished batch of mine to commit. Untracked source acquisitions were preserved. The running Vite site process was left alone.

**Concurrent-work boundary:** another writer subsequently changed canonical records and the web labelling while this audit was running. In particular, the conception/device and H4/K1 identities were being consolidated in the shared checkout. I did not adopt those changes as audit conclusions or commit them. The report therefore deliberately audits the immutable starting batch, not an unstable mixture of versions. `concurrent-drift.json` records a later mechanical comparison of the permitted canonical inputs. Findings about identities or labels that have since changed must be compared with that later batch before implementation. Later records and modified claims have **not** received an audit verdict here. The independent review, its folder and its branches were not consulted.

The graph is useful as a collection of investigated episodes. It is **not yet a defensible ranking of the world's most consequential science and technology**, nor a single causal dependency graph. Its vertices mix general methods, individual specimens, texts, experiments, commercial products, institutions and particular deployments. Its arrows mix physical composition, functional prerequisites, evidential reasoning, copying, transmission and development. These are useful relationships, but they answer different questions.

## The ten most important corrections

1. **Separate a broad breakthrough from its particular witness before ranking it.** The sharpest case is `bio-crispr-cas9-fitness-screen-2015`: its configuration explicitly denotes Hart's particular screen, while tier 0 is appropriate to a much broader programmable-editing breakthrough. Propose tier 2 for the existing study, not a silent renaming into all CRISPR. Likewise `mat-nylon-filament-bristles` is toothbrush-bristle filament, not the entire invention of nylon; propose tier 2. These are modelling and importance judgements, not denials that the studies/products happened. The inspected Hart abstract describes the five-cell-line application, not the original development of Cas9 editing.

2. **Repair the pH-to-clinical-trial endpoint mismatch.** `chem-ph-scale-to-oxford-penicillin` has evidence of pH use in the Oxford production/extraction research programme. That does not establish pH's documented role in the particular treatment dated 12 February 1941, to which the target is anchored. The record already admits this limit, which is good; the endpoint still needs to match the proposition. Propose a properly scoped programme/production endpoint or contextual association. Do not claim the historical clinical contribution is false. Basis: reopened Abraham et al. 1941, pp.177–180, including culture-medium pH passages.

3. **Bind the Clausius-to-Thomson link to supported events.** `mech-clausius-to-thomson-dynamical` has no source event, so substituting its selected 1852 recognition anchor makes an apparent backward arrow to the 1851 presentation. The historical relation is credible: Thomson explicitly credits Clausius and distinguishes his independent proof. What is established is printed wording, not every sentence of the earlier oral reading. Use the existing 1850 publication event and a supported printed receiving event, or leave the precise oral claim unresolved. This is an event-modelling/evidence issue, not proof of time-reversed influence. Basis: reopened Clausius 1867 collection p.14 and Thomson §§9,14.

4. **Give composition and evidential use different semantics.** `bio-base-ratios-to-double-helix` concerns evidence entering a model; `mat-objectives-with-compensating-eyepieces` concerns coordinated components. Both currently use `observed-combination`. A single display style can make both look like causal ancestry. Keep their supported facts, but distinguish evidential contribution, used-with and part-of. `mech-blockmill-to-coaking` is system membership, not a completed system causing its component to be invented. These are modelling corrections. Basis: reopened Watson–Crick p.737, Zeiss printed p.1, Abbe pp.25–26 and the [coaking-machine object record](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co46697/original-coaking-machine-installed-at-portsmouth).

5. **Distinguish practical expertise directing an investigation.** `global-korent-to-buoy-placement` is stronger and more specific than mere combination: Korent's identifications directed sampling locations. A sampling-design/practice-informs-investigation facet would represent his contribution better. Do not translate this into a claim that instruments independently validated all of the navigation system. Basis: reopened Genz et al. 2009, p.237; the paper preserves inconclusive results elsewhere.

6. **Stop letting ID spelling determine substantive topics.** `chem-beckman-acidimeter-1935` is assigned Optics and Materials because of the glass electrode. Propose Chemistry, Measurement and Instruments. `bio-penicillin-selective-culture-1929` and `bio-hybridoma-monoclonal-antibodies-1980` are genetics-only despite their microbiological and immunological referents. These are label defects established by recomputing the code's assignments and reading the configurations, not new influence claims.

7. **Rank comparable scopes consistently across eras and regions.** Propose promotions for `math-nine-chapters-fangcheng` and `math-euclid-greatest-common-measure` from 2 to 1, and `math-brahmagupta-zero-rules-628` from 1 to 0. Conversely, the `global-diamond-sutra-paper-print-868` scroll is an important witness to printing, not the total printing breakthrough: propose 0 to 2. Reopening the [IDP source](https://idp.bl.uk/discover/learning/buddhism-on-the-silk-roads/articles/buddhism-on-the-ground/buddhist-texts-the-diamond-sutra/) verifies the dated printed scroll and explicitly identifies an earlier printing tradition. These are importance judgements; they do not establish a global priority date.

8. **Do not give an installation the importance of its whole technology.** Propose `mech-bloomfield-pump-1776` from 0 to 1 and the separately scoped condenser arrangement `mech-condenser-model-1765` from 1 to 0. Treat Barbegal, Dholavira and the constructed Hōkūleʻa as major particular configurations rather than substitutes for all milling, water management and voyaging. Their cultural and regional importance remains real. This is an importance/identity policy decision, not a factual correction to their existence.

9. **Expose class/product overlap explicitly.** The broad Hoerni planar-transistor node includes the separately represented 2N1613; bristle filament and the whole toothbrush are a component and a product. These are not automatically duplicate objects, but counting each at broad-breakthrough scale inflates apparent independent achievements. The general plasmid-cloning node is supported by a particular 1979 insulin application and needs its earlier general-method evidence, rather than silently making that application the method's origin. Later concurrent identity consolidations are outside this pinned judgement.

10. **Preserve actual transfer dates and distinguish adaptation from continuation.** The EDSAC-to-LEO exchange began in 1947, not at the selected 1950 EDSAC service anchor. Bind the relationship to the appropriate development episode. The Bessemer-to-Gerlafingen edge records reuse of a converter with changed gas and geometry, so `documented-adaptation` is more informative than generic development. The latter is supported by a later court account of a memo, not independently inspected 1949 experimental logs. [Court opinion, §II.C(2)](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/FSupp/257/372/1600622/).

## Connections: agreement, chronology and uncertainty

Several difficult-looking links survived sceptical checking. They should not be removed merely because they are indirect, involve overlapping date bounds or lack exclusive necessity:

- The original Watson–Crick paper actually cites Wyatt and Chargaff; the graph does not need to invent an exclusive chemical route. The reopened Wyatt abstract also supports the measured balance. Its **received** date of 1 July is not its November publication date; the relationship uses a reported-measurement bound, not a public-release claim. [Wyatt's original abstract and article dates](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2147366/).
- Faraday's ring, moving-magnet and disc links are supported by explicit experimental rationales in §§39 and 83. Paragraph sequence alone would not establish laboratory chronology, and the record correctly avoids that shortcut.
- The Hertz/Helmholtz/Maxwell chain is better than a direct textbook-to-radio story. Hertz explicitly distinguishes the theory being tested from direct guidance by Maxwell's difficult book.
- The Shen Gua relationships describe parts of one reported operating configuration. Equal reign-bounded dates do not establish separate serial inventions.
- Procopius supports failure, response and later protection, and the record preserves the military purpose of the chain barrier. It does not claim that particular hill-mill parts were physically reused.
- The Bessemer/Gerlafingen/Linz sequence preserves changed operating conditions and alternative steelmaking routes. Corporate and institutional retrospectives remain sources with interests, not neutral experimental logs.
- The public genome account supports fluorescence contributions, varied detectors, mapped clones and a draft snapshot. It does not claim a complete, gap-free human genome in 2000.

**Confidence is proposition-specific.** There are 109 high and 22 medium records. High confidence in an explicit citation, identified component use or actor statement is often reasonable. It must not automatically cover the priority of an idea, an exact day, the truth of an actor's explanation, a counterfactual necessity, or the absence of other contributions. I did not find grounds for a wholesale confidence downgrade.

One fresh cross-source tension deserves checking: the CHM planar-IC narrative dates working junction-isolated circuits to 27 September 1960; Jay Last's tentative recollection in `src-chm-sector`, PDF pp.84–85, places initial successful diffused isolation in October or November. A recollection need not defeat dated reports, but the exact date is not independently settled by merely repeating the CHM summary. The historical programme sequence survives this disagreement.

Humulin is a useful example of an already-disclosed disagreement, **not a newly discovered error**: the reopened National Academies chapter p.173 says first sale in 1983, while the node already records accounts of a 1982 launch/market entry and a distinct FDA approval date. I have not independently checked all those additional sources and do not resolve the conflict here. Missing evidence and conflicting retrospective accounts do not warrant inventing a precise first-sale day.

### Are the eight types the right set?

The eight used types are workable as a starting vocabulary, but their boundaries are not sufficiently orthogonal for a causal interpretation. The schema actually permits **eleven** types: the additional `supersedes`, `revival-of` and `abandonment-of` have zero records in the audited snapshot. An empty type is not evidence that replacement, revival or abandonment did not occur.

| Used type | Records | Defensible boundary / problem |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| documented-development | 51 | Explicit historical continuation or acknowledged contribution. Currently also includes interpretation, comparison, implementation and response to criticism. Do not use it as the residual category for everything that precedes something else. |
| documented-transmission | 16 | A source identifies a carrier or channel of content, skill or technique. Shared vocabulary and chronological availability are insufficient. Explicit citation can establish use of an idea without showing physical transfer of apparatus. |
| documented-adaptation | 19 | A receiving configuration preserves identifiable features and changes them for a stated purpose or context. It naturally overlaps development; record which feature changed. |
| instrument-enables-observation | 12 | Identified tool/measurement operation used for the specified evidence. It need not be uniquely necessary or yield a correct interpretation automatically. |
| process-enables-production | 4 | A specified production route used to make the stated output. Distinguish actual use from universal physical necessity. |
| theory-informs-design | 4 | Explicit use of a model or theoretical diagnosis in a design choice. It should not absorb theory-informed interpretation of another scientific result. |
| observed-combination | 22 | Demonstrated use together in a scoped configuration. It currently conflates component membership, companion components, a vessel's use, sampling guidance and evidence used in a model. Co-presence alone should not become influence. |
| bounded-technical-requirement | 3 | A functional requirement within a defined configuration. A required physical interaction does not prove that the earlier discovery report was historically indispensable. |

Propose keeping historical predicates while adding an explicit **relationship role**: developmental, transmission, evidential/interpretive, physical composition/use, or functional requirement. Make used-with nonchronological and distinguish it from part-of. `documented` concerns evidence; it does not explain what happened. This is a schema/presentation proposal only. A historian or the user should decide whether new predicates or facets give the best interface; forcing all of these into one exclusive eight-way partition will continue to lose meaning.

## Entries, the two kinds and identity

The two-way split is defensible **only if its scope is stated**. Under a broad convention, science means a knowledge claim, observation, dataset, theory or mathematical result, and technology means a designed artifact, operational process, material, infrastructure or skilled practice. This convention allows a polymerase preparation to be technology and a copying inference to be science, or a telescope to be technology and inferred lunar relief to be science. It does not imply that historical people used today's discipline names.

The split is inadequate as the only ontology. Mathematics is not reducible to empirical science; an algorithm is simultaneously mathematical knowledge and an operational method. A text can embody results, instructions and pedagogy. Subak is an institutional and ecological practice as well as channels and weirs. Navigation incorporates learned judgement that an instrument category cannot express. A diffraction pattern, the experiment making it, and an interpretation of it require different referents.

Propose a retained display grouping plus explicit subkinds: mathematical result/method, observation/dataset, explanatory model, experimental method, instrument, production process, material, infrastructure, documentary witness and sociotechnical practice. These facets should describe the node, not rank cultures as more or less scientific.

Five computational-exposition nodes are flagged for a convention decision: al-Khwarizmi's arithmetic, the two Latin reworkings, Liber abaci and al-Hawari's worked arithmetic. Comparable mathematical texts are science, whereas these are technology. The proposed science assignment follows a knowledge-versus-physical-artifact convention; the opposite convention could be coherent if applied consistently to the comparable procedures. This is not a factual discovery about the historical texts.

I did **not** establish a pair of entries that must be deleted as a literal duplicate. Class/product overlap, a process and a product, or several components of a hydraulic system are not the same as duplicated identities. Their granularity must be visible, however. A whole text and one chapter's algorithm can legitimately coexist, but not count as two independent broad breakthroughs without explanation. The specific Gwalior inscription should not stand for all positional notation; the YBC tablet should not stand for every later theorem about right triangles. A named site's survival does not establish a global origin.

## Breakthroughs and ranking

The current tier reasons alternate between chart prominence, historical consequence, documentation quality and examples selected to diversify coverage. Those goals are not interchangeable. A sparse, precisely dated record can be a useful visual example without being a top-tier historical transformation. A broad mathematical method can be consequential despite having only indirect or late reception evidence.

For this audit I used the breadth and durability of the **scoped referent's** consequences, its enabling reach, and whether that consequence is already represented by a broader identity. I did not use source count, exact dating, commercial success alone, current degree in this incomplete graph, or an era/region quota as an impact measure. The 28 proposed changes are inspectable in `entry-verdicts.jsonl`; equal tiers are not claims of equal measurable benefit.

Examples ranked **too high under that criterion** include the Hart screen, bristle filament, Diamond Sutra specimen, constructed Hōkūleʻa, Bloomfield installation, a single Shang burial jar, aluminum teakettle and Dr West toothbrush. Examples **too low** include the common-measure algorithm, fangcheng, arithmetic on zero, the separate-condensation arrangement, Ampere's force result, H4, the Qhapaq Ñan network and subak. The khipu promotion is conditional on whether the node denotes the accounting practice or only the 1533 witnessed transaction. The general practice's importance must not be silently transferred to a narrowly defined specimen.

Many tier-0 choices remain defensible: the DNA model, MOS, point-contact amplification, electromagnetic field theory, induction, bulk steel conversion, Carnot's analysis and Clausius's reconciliation are consequential within their stated scopes. These agreements do not establish that the 35 are the world's top 35. Large absent fields and the concentration on a few extensively inspected workshops make that claim untenable.

## Labels and the vocabulary

At the pinned revision I read all **24 regex rules**, all **63 override keys**, and the replacement precedence `override ?? node.labels ?? regex matches`. Recomputed assignments agree with the saved site dataset for all 221 entries. Thus the reported topic problems are **semantic**, not evidence that the import failed. `label-verdicts.jsonl` provides one row per entry, including raw regex matches and the actual replacement route.

Definite or strong defects include the acidimeter's optical classification; penicillin selective culture and hybridoma work being genetics-only; absent steam labels on Shadwell and Elsecar; aluminum engine castings missing material labels; missing optics on magnetic optical rotation; and inconsistent Mathematics coverage between older entries and the newer explicit overrides. The negative lookbehind for `google-` does not exclude the words “Google Glass” in a title with a space. Optics can reasonably apply to the wearable's display, but Materials should not be inferred from its brand name. These are spelling-to-topic failures, not claims that glass has no optical uses.

Glass vessels and optical glass research are conflated in **Optics & glass**. If the label intentionally includes every glass craft, the vessels are not erroneous assignments under that broad definition; the label itself is then too broad for an optics filter. The proposed split is Glassmaking/Materials versus Optics, with overlap where justified. Likewise transport should include boats and navigation applications, not mainly roads. Food/agriculture, aquaculture, microbiology and immunology are material omissions for the existing entries. Regional practices need topic facets without being forced into a modern laboratory-discipline hierarchy.

Some proposals are **vocabulary choices**, not categorical errors: whether energy is an umbrella over heat; whether every electric-power installation should also receive electromagnetism; whether generic semiconductor components should have Computing; and whether all practical arithmetic belongs in Mathematics. Labels can overlap, but their inclusion rules should be explicit. Regexes are useful draft suggestions, not historical metadata authorities. Overrides replace the full set, so an override can silently discard a useful cross-topic label. Names should not silently control either importance or the first-match ink used in the chart.

## Decisions for a historian or the user

- **Purpose of tiers:** global historical consequence, navigational prominence in a curated collection, or representative regional examples. A single number cannot silently mean all three. The proposed tier changes implement the first interpretation requested in this audit.
- **Identity policy:** when a general method, a design proposal, its patent, an early specimen and a commercial product deserve separate nodes; how component and class/product overlap should appear and be counted.
- **Mathematics and practices:** whether to retain two display groups with subkinds, or use additional primary kinds. Five proposed kind changes depend on that convention.
- **Historical adjudication:** the exact early junction-isolation chronology; Humulin launch versus first sale; disputed archaeological dating and phasing; indirect text histories; and the two original connection passages that remain unavailable. Translations and OCR were not re-edited against original languages.
- **Evidence versus importance:** agreement with a well-qualified proposition should remain possible without elevating a precisely documented specimen above a poorly preserved but consequential tradition.
- **Graph semantics:** which arrows may be interpreted causally, which are functional requirements, and which merely describe material composition, operational use or comparison. A user should be able to tell without opening every caveat.

No correction or gap in this report authorizes an addition or alteration to the graph. All await the user's review results and a later explicit implementation step.
