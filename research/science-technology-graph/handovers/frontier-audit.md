## Review boundary

The first voyaging reviewer deferred all three exclusions because of possible experimental learning and the apparatus-to-trial causal path. A second independent Terra review and root adjudication distinguish the asserted technology identities from potentially new scientific findings: documented experimental learning or a later reconstruction is not itself an established later global-frontier advance. Both assessments are retained in the current decision. The exclusions do not assert cultural insignificance, absence of useful observations or proof about ancient routes and contacts.

Do not apply the excluded vessel events to another technology or relabel them as scientific discoveries. Reconsider an explanatory identity only when a specific later frontier result and its actual enabling relationship are established.

- Nylon filament and the finished toothbrush retain their component/application distinction. An earlier 1894 commercial aluminum tea kettle is now documented, but the 1895 kettle's forming method and any meaningful cost/access difference remain unresolved.
