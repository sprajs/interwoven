W. W. Rouse Ball, An Essay on Newton’s Principia (London: Macmillan, 1893), appendix, printed pp. 139 and 147–148. https://rcin.org.pl/Content/235304/6087.pdf

INSPECTED PASSAGE (researcher paraphrase; not the full source): Appendix p. 139 says the transcribed 6 January 1680 Hooke letter is copied from Hooke’s rough draft. Printed p. 147 states Hooke’s supposition that attraction varies in reciprocal duplicate proportion to distance from the centre. This is an explicit earlier inverse-square celestial proposal, not proof that Hooke had the later Principia synthesis or its mathematical derivation.
