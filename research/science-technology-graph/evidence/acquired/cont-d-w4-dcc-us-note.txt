Attributed research note, not an original press-wire capture. Philips Consumer Electronics press release dated 5 November 1992, reproduced in the Digital Compact Cassette section of Key Production's history, https://www.keyproduction.co.uk/about/history/ . The text says the DCC900 recorder/player was on sale in five US cities and available in France, Germany, Japan, the Netherlands and the UK. It also says PolyGram prerecorded DCC albums went on sale 3 November. This is a reproduced corporate release, not inspection of PRNewswire original bytes; it does not establish first sale.
