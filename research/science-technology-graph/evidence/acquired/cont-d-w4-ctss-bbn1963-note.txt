Attributed partial research note; no reproduction of the proceedings. John McCarthy, S. Boilen, E. Fredkin and J. C. R. Licklider, “A Time-Sharing Debugging System for a Small Computer,” Proceedings of the Spring Joint Computer Conference, 1963, printed pp. 51–57, https://www.bitsavers.org/pdf/afips/1963-05_%2323.pdf . On pp. 51–52 the authors describe a PDP-1 system giving five typewriter users separate 4,096-word views with magnetic-drum swapping. On p. 56 they report operation at Bolt Beranek and Newman since September 1962, four hours per day, initially with two and later five typewriters. They explicitly describe the lack of a drum program library and unavailable magnetic-tape user files as system weaknesses. This is an earlier interactive time-sharing comparison; it does not establish that CTSS originated all file services.
