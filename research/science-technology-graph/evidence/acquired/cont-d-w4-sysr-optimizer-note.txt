Attributed research note, not a reproduction. P. Griffiths Selinger, M. M. Astrahan, D. D. Chamberlin, R. A. Lorie and T. G. Price, “Access Path Selection in a Relational Database Management System,” ACM SIGMOD 1979, printed pp. 23–34, https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~michjc/eecs584/Papers/selinger_1979.pdf . The digital recreation describes System R's SQL access-path and join-order selection using catalog statistics and estimated CPU plus I/O cost. Its conclusion identifies expanded statistics, CPU costs and join-order method as differences from earlier work. The authors say System R was experimental and not generally available outside IBM Research.
