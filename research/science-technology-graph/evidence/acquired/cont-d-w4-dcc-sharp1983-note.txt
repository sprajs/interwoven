Attributed partial research note, not a reproduction. “Spring New Product Guide,” High Fidelity, April 1983, printed p. 42, https://www.worldradiohistory.com/Archive-All-Audio/Archive-High-Fidelity/80s/High-Fidelity-1983-04.pdf. The contemporary report describes Sharp’s CX-3 prototype, which used an 18-track head to make digital recordings on a conventional chromium-dioxide Compact Cassette at its usual speed and running time. It does not establish playback of pre-existing analogue recordings, a completed sale, or worldwide priority. Philips/Matsushita’s later DCC specifications describe PASC compression and mandatory analogue-cassette playback, a different combination.
