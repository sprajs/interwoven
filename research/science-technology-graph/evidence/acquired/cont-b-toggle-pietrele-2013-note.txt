Source-identified research note, not the original article. Original full PDF was inspected from https://revije.ff.uni-lj.si/DocumentaPraehistorica/article/download/40.14/808 on 6 November 2026; retained here as a short researcher paraphrase.

Norbert Benecke, Svend Hansen, Dirk Nowacki, Agathe Reingruber, Kenneth Ritchie, and Jürgen Wunderlich (2013), “Pietrele in the Lower Danube region: integrating archaeological, faunal and environmental investigations,” Documenta Praehistorica 40, 175–193, DOI 10.4312/dp.40.14.

Inspected: printed p. 181 (main settlement period circa 4600–4250 cal BCE); pp. 185–187 (Fishing in Pietrele, including the authors' separate description and photograph of toggle heads from Pietrele, Fig. 12, and their functional reconstruction, Fig. 13); and p. 187 (tell construction layers and contextual limits). The report identifies antler toggle harpoons among the settlement's fishing/hunting assemblage. The site is multiperiod. The specific heads are not individually radiocarbon dated in the inspected discussion and the report does not assign an actual named captured animal to them. It distinguishes the toggle heads from separately discussed rod-shaped barbed harpoons. The span assigned to the occupation does not prove an exact day or invention date.
