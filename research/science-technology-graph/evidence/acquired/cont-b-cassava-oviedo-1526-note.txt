Gonzalo Fernández de Oviedo y Valdés, Sumario de la natural y general historia de las Indias, 1526, chapter V, digital transcription https://es.wikisource.org/wiki/Sumario_de_la_natural_y_general_historia_de_las_Indias/V. The text describes a long palm-woven cibucan used to squeeze grated yuca by twisting it. This supplies an earlier documented woven pressing sack but does not describe the stone-weighted axial-stretching operation documented in the separate 1535 passage. It is not independently assigned to the latter configuration or treated as proof of first invention.
