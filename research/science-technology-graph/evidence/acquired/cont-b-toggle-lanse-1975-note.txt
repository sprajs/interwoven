Inspected 6 November 2026 from https://www.flutopedia.com/refs/McGhee_1975_ArchaicSequenceLabrador_FP.pdf, an online selection from Robert McGhee and James A. Tuck, An Archaic Sequence from the Strait of Belle Isle, Labrador, National Museum of Man, Archaeological Survey of Canada Mercury Series paper 34, 1975.

Printed p. 88 places a toggling harpoon head beneath the upper chest in the L'Anse Amour burial, and a separate decorated ivory object under the sacrum; the latter was suggested as a hand-toggle. Printed p. 92 describes the head as antler, broken at its tip, with a hollow oblique base and lateral spur; no line-hole was present, so a line wrapped around the body was inferred. The report records a charcoal determination of 7,530 ± 140 radiocarbon years BP from an apparent grave-fire on the skeleton's level; this number is not a calibrated calendar age. The harpoon head itself was not directly dated. The artifact's inclusion in the grave documents community access but does not establish a particular observed prey-capture event.
