Source: Douglas W. Fuerstenau oral history, Regional Oral History Office, University of California, Berkeley, 2011.
Original URL: https://digicoll.lib.berkeley.edu/record/218602/files/fuerstenau_doug.pdf
Inspected printed pp. 107 and 493: the interviewee describes the Bessel brothers' graphite operation and plant, a slurry heated toward boiling to produce bubbles that lifted graphite, and relates the work to an 1877 patent. On p. 107 he recalls that the brothers' mine closed in 1883; p. 493 gives the plant as near Dresden. A separate 1907 report discusses Bessel process changes dated 1887 and ore from Kropfmuehle, so neither the exact closure nor processing location is accepted as settled.
