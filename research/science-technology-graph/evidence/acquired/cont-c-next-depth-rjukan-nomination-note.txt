Printed pp. 52–53 describe Rjukan arc facilities, say certain plants were used until 1928, and separately say Rjukan Birkeland–Eyde furnaces operated until 1940 while Notodden furnaces lasted until 1934. The relationship between those statements is unresolved. Printed p. 56 inventories Rjukan furnaces with some uncertain entries. Printed p. 103, Furnace House A, gives 6 April 1934 as the date the last Notodden Birkeland–Eyde furnaces were extinguished.
