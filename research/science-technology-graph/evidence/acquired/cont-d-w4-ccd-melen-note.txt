Roger Melen, “The tradeoffs in monolithic image sensors: MOS vs CCD,” Electronics, 24 May 1973, printed pp. 106–111, https://www.worldradiohistory.com/Archive-Electronics/70s/73/Electronics-1973-05-24.pdf. Melen compares MOS photodiode arrays using transistor switching with CCD arrays that transport charge, describing both capabilities and nonidentical noise and imaging tradeoffs. The introduction states that MOS linear arrays and 50-by-50 camera systems were already sold, and that the CCD was newly available as a 500-element linear device. It discusses further CCD video capabilities prospectively; no such projection is treated as an achieved specification of the 1973 device.
