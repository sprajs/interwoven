Attributed partial research notes, not the original: Te-Pang Hou, Manufacture of Soda, second edition, chapter III, printed pp. 31–32, inspected through the scan at https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a0/Manufacture_Of_Soda_by_Hou_Te-Pang.pdf. The monograph says John Thom produced about 200 kg soda using ammonium carbonate and salt in 1837, while a historical 1885 account says 1836. It describes Dyar and Hemming and early Newton and Widnes plants as unsuccessful against Leblanc, and reports Couillet operation in 1865. Its title page reads 1923 despite a foreword discussing 1937, so the exact edition year is not resolved here. Full original bytes and hash are not retained.
