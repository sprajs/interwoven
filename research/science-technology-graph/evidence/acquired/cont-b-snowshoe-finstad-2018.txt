Finstad, Martinsen, Hole and Pilø 2018, printed p. 45, note an object from Siberia claimed to be a ski at about 6000 BCE, citing Burov 1989. The authors explicitly describe it as a claimed ski. This is an earlier comparator to a framed snowshoe, not proof of transfer or an independently checked direct sample date.
