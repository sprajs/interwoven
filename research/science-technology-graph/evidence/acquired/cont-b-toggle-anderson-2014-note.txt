Anderson, “Paleoindian and Archaic Periods in North America,” chapter 2.15 in The Cambridge World Prehistory, eds. Colin Renfrew and Paul Bahn, Cambridge University Press, 2014, DOI 10.1017/CHO9781139017831.063. This proof labels its first page 913 and the relevant page 927; Cambridge's final online record gives a different overall page range.

The opening p. 913 states that dates are in calibrated years before present unless otherwise noted. Printed p. 927 describes the L'Anse Amour burial and a toggling harpoon among its grave goods, giving a rounded context estimate of about 7,500 cal BP. This is a contextual approximate estimate, not a hard maximum-latest bound or a direct date on the harpoon. A later human-bone date and marine correction are discussed in Duggan et al. 2017.
