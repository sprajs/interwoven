Source: Y. C. Ho and E. C. Johnson, “Design of a Numerical Milling Machine System,” Proceedings of the Eastern Joint Computer Conference, Washington, D.C., 9–13 December 1957, published by the Institute of Radio Engineers, copyright 1958, pp. 11–24.
Original URL: https://bitsavers.org/pdf/afips/1957-12_%2312.pdf
Inspected pp. 21–24: the authors describe a digital servo comparing command and feedback pulses, and an application to a large Kearney & Trecker three-axis contour mill using hydraulic servodrives. The digital interpolator and control receive distance/feed commands from punched tape. A pictured aircraft fitting was machined on a functionally similar machine. They state that productivity of the controlled machine had been demonstrated in actual field use; the field installation and exact dates are not named. Conference dates are December 1957, but this version is copyrighted 1958; publication timing is not equated to the presentation.
