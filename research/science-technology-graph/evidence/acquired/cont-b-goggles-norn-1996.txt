Norn 1996, abstract and printed pp. 17–18: tests and measurement of a sample of Inuit snow goggles examine narrow apertures, restricted field of view and shielding from harmful light. Performance varies by specimen and setup; these modern experiments do not date a historical object or establish uniform medical protection.
