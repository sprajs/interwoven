Source-identified paraphrase of the publisher-rendered abstract and article preview; the full article was not read. Dawei Li, Wei Wang, Feng Tian, Wei Liao and Christopher J. Bae, “The oldest bark cloth beater in southern China (Dingmo, Bubing basin, Guangxi),” Quaternary International 354 (2014), 184–189, DOI 10.1016/j.quaint.2014.06.062, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1040618214004364. The authors interpret an in-situ grooved stone at Dingmo as a barkcloth beater; its stratigraphic layer is reported at 7898 ± 34 BP; the abstract does not identify whether this figure is calibrated, not a direct date of a recovered barkcloth. This comparator does not establish a surviving cloth sample, a precise calendar year, or a transmission to the Xuanquanzhi sheets.
The separately inspected 2024 excavation report identifies 7898 ± 34 as a calibrated BP value for contextual charcoal sample XA7222, with uncalibrated value 7052 ± 31 radiocarbon BP.
