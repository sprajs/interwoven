Attributed partial research note, not a reproduction. Joseph-Louis Proust, “Researches on Copper,” Annales de chimie 32 (1799), pp. 26–54, excerpted in Henry M. Leicester and Herbert S. Klickstein, A Source Book in Chemistry, 1400–1900 (1952), and presented online at https://web2.lemoyne.edu/~giunta/proust.html (inspected 6 November 2026). In the translated excerpt Proust sets out mass proportions for copper carbonate and argues that genuine compounds retain fixed proportions, including in natural and prepared materials. This is evidence for an earlier composition-by-mass result, not for Dalton’s later relative atomic-weight framework. Only the excerpted 1952 English translation was inspected; the original French paper and complete translation were not examined.
