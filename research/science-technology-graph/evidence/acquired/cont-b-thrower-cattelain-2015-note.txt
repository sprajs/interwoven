Pierre Cattelain and Jean-Marc Pétillon, Le « type 2a », plus ancien modèle de propulseur paléolithique, PALEO 26 (2015), pp. 17–32, DOI 10.4000/paleo.2876. Original PDF independently inspected from https://pdfs.semanticscholar.org/0699/189494aa393a26568471f6b27117fe3bb5b7.pdf.
Printed p. 22 says one La Garenne Abri Blanchard spear-thrower specimen came from B5; the second was found during clandestine excavations. The paper lists B5 as 15050 ± 90 uncalibrated radiocarbon BP. Figure 6 no. 3 on printed p. 24 identifies the B5 specimen as Musée d’Argentomagus 999-10-1292 SM.-L1-B5; it is incomplete. Table 2 on p. 26 lists Bos bone sample ETH 28493, not a direct assay of the artifact. Figure 8 p. 27 and the abstract p. 17 identify a probable phase of approximately 19000–18000 calibrated BP for the type at several sites, including La Garenne. These estimates are contextual, not individual direct dates or a first-invention date.
Printed pp. 21–22 discuss the fragmentary Combe-Saunière hook and the heterogeneous layer IV radiocarbon results, including much younger determinations; the authors exclude it provisionally from the chronological discussion. Le Placard finds were from old excavations without reliable stratigraphic association. The 2015 discussion does not rule out all earlier use.
