Inspected original PDF printed p. 47: Styles states that Arkwright's horse-powered Nottingham spinning mill went into full production in late 1772 and the yarn was subsequently woven into calicoes, citing R.S. Fitton, The Arkwrights: Spinners of Fortune (1989), pp. 26–37. Earlier operation is possible; the paragraph does not claim that production started globally in 1772. The complete author-hosted PDF was inspected but is not reproduced in this cache; no full-original checksum is asserted.

Also inspected printed p. 49: Styles links Paul’s drafting rollers to machines used commercially at Birmingham and at Northampton’s Marvel’s Mill, which he dates 1743 to about 1761. This differs from Seymour-Jones’s interpretation that only the spindle-drawing alternative reached operation; the Styles passage cites David L. Bates’s 1996–97 study. The exact drafting method of those early operated machines remains disputed.
