“It was not Liebig, however, but an Irish doctor, James Murray (1788–1871), who became the first commercial vendor of inorganic fertilizers with his 1841 offer of liquid superphosphate made by dissolving bones in H₂SO₄.⁵⁶ Two years later Lawes set up a superphosphate factory at Deptford on the Thames. His fertilizer, patented in May 1842 on the same day Murray got his patent, was a virtually identical product, containing ‘Super-Phosphate of Lime, Phosphate of Ammonia, Silicate of Pot-ass, etc.’⁵⁷”
