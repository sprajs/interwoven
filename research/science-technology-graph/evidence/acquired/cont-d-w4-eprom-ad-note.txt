Intel advertisement for the 1701 and 1702 E-ROM, Electronics, 27 September 1971, advertisement between printed pp. 35 and 38, https://www.worldradiohistory.com/Archive-Electronics/70s/71/Electronics-1971-09-27.pdf . The advertisement describes 2048-bit electrically programmable, ultraviolet-erasable MOS read-only memories, says both products are in stock, and lists distributors offering immediate delivery of versions programmed for EBCDIC-to-ASCII conversion. It names contacts in the US, Europe and Japan. This is a supplier's commercial offer by the issue date, not proof of a completed sale or global first. Original issue inspected through a search-provider-rendered scan; direct download returned 403, so no original-byte hash is claimed.
