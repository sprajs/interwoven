STAR Methods, “L’Anse Amour Burial Mound – EiBf-4”, and supplementary figure S3: the authors identify the earlier hearth charcoal measurement as 7,530 ± 140 uncalibrated radiocarbon BP (I-8099). They report a separate direct measurement of bone collagen from the buried individual of 7,115 ± 20 uncalibrated BP (UCIAMS-107237); after a marine dietary carbon correction their calibrated median is 7,714 cal BP. This median is an estimate, not a confidence interval or a hard latest endpoint. The human sample is a date on the associated individual, not a direct radiocarbon determination on the harpoon; different sample types and correction assumptions must not be interchanged.
