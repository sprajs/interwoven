Attributed partial research note; this is a paraphrase, not a reproduction. Pierre-Léonard Zaffalon, “Carl Friedrich Rammelsberg and the Early Diffusion of the Periodic Table of Dmitri Mendeleev,” Chemistry: Bulgarian Journal of Science Education 26 (2017), pp. 133–138, https://journals.azbuki.bg/en/science/carl-friedriech-rammelsberg-and-the-early-diffusion-of-the-periodic-table-of-dmitri-mendeleev/view. Printed pp. 136–137 report that Rammelsberg addressed Mendeleev’s proposal for uranium at the Berlin Chemical Society on 9 December 1872 and reproduced a related table in his 1873 textbook. The 1873 main text retained an earlier uranium value while the table gave the revised value. The article cites Rammelsberg’s 1872 paper; that original German paper was not independently inspected. Zaffalon calls the German Mendeleev paper 1872, while the 1895 Seubert reprint calls it 1871. Only the indicated online passage was inspected; no original-file digest is asserted.
